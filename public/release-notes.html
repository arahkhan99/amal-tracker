Release notes

Newest first. Zad is a phone app for Android and iPhone.

Coming next

  • Prayer & Amal Tracker is now called Zad, and it's coming to Google Play and the App Store for Android and iPhone. Your logs, qada and settings carry over as they are.
  • New Settings → Delete my account: delete your cloud backup and Zad sign-in right in the app, with the option to back up first or clear Zad on your phone too.
  • Signing in to cloud backup now asks you to agree to the Terms of use and Privacy policy, and asks again if they change. Your version number now shows in Settings → About.
  • Easier to use: clearer screen-reader labels, keyboard support and easier-to-read text. On newer Android phones, reminders no longer open the Alarms & reminders screen.

Cloud backup and automatic updates 30 September 2026

  • New Settings → Cloud backup: sign in with Google to back up your prayers, amal, qada and settings to your own private space, and sync them between devices. (Sign-in and sync also worked in the web version, since retired.)
  • Setting up on a new phone? Sign in during setup and everything comes back.
  • Android updates now arrive automatically through Firebase App Tester and install over the old version, keeping your data.

Android polish 23 September 2026

  • The Android back button closes open panels and returns to Home before leaving the app.
  • Status bar icons now match each screen, with a cleaner look at the top of the screen.
  • Finding your location falls back to GPS when network location isn't available.
  • City names show the country (or the state in the US and Canada).

First release 22 September 2026

  • Log the five daily prayers and your daily amal, with streaks and day, week, month and year progress.
  • Qada tracking for prayers and fasts: a missed prayer is added automatically, and a monthly goal tracks what you make up.
  • Prayer times calculated offline from your location, Hijri dates, and reminders before a prayer becomes qada.
  • Works fully offline, with an optional fingerprint lock and backup to a file.
