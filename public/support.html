Common questions

The prayer times don't match my masjid

Go to Settings and choose the calculation method and Asr method your masjid follows, and check that your location is right. Times are calculations, so small differences are normal; your masjid's timetable comes first.

My location is wrong or says "Your location"

Open Settings → Location and use your current location again, or search for your city. "Your location" means the city name couldn't be looked up (usually because you were offline); prayer times still use your coordinates.

Reminders are late or missing

  • Allow notifications for Zad in your phone's settings.
  • On Android 14 and newer, reminders can be a few minutes late unless Alarms & reminders is allowed for Zad. To allow it, open your phone's Settings → Apps → Zad and turn on Alarms & reminders (the exact wording can differ by phone).
  • On Android, turn off battery optimisation for Zad if your phone limits background apps.
  • Open Zad once in a while: it schedules reminders for the next 7 days each time it opens or you log something.

Moving to a new phone

Turn on Settings → Cloud backup on the old phone and sign in with the same Google account on the new one. Or use Settings → Back up to save a file and restore it on the new phone.

How do I delete my account?

While signed in to cloud backup, go to Settings → Delete my account. It deletes your cloud backup and your Zad sign-in, and you can choose to clear Zad on your phone too. Details and an email option: Delete your account and data.

Sync isn't working

Check you're online and signed in under Settings → Cloud backup, then tap Sync now. Changes made offline sync automatically when you're back online.

I forgot to log something

You can go back to earlier days and log or change them. Marking a prayer as missed adds it to your qada; changing it later removes it again.
