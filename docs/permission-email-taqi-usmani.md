**Subject:** Permission request: Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani's English translation of the Qur'an in a free app

Assalamu alaikum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh,

My name is [Your full name], and I am [your title / role, e.g. a scholar and teacher at ...]. I am developing **Zad**, a mobile app for Android and iOS. It helps Muslims build consistency in their daily worship: prayer, make-up (qada) prayers, morning and evening adhkar, and Qur'an recitation.

The app includes a Qur'an reader with the 15-line IndoPak and Uthmani mushaf. We would be honoured to offer **"The Meanings of the Noble Qur'an" by Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani (hafizahullah)** as the app's main English translation. Readers would see it beneath each ayah when they choose to show a translation.

We are requesting your permission to include the translation text in the app, on these terms:
- **Free:** Zad is free to download and has no advertisements. The Qur'an reader and this translation will always stay free; they will never be sold or put behind a payment.
- **Attribution:** every screen that shows it will name the translation, Mufti Muhammad Taqi Usmani and Maktaba Ma'ariful Qur'an, with a link to your website.
- **No changes:** the text will be shown exactly as published. We will not edit or abridge it, and we will not add commentary to it.
- **Text only:** we will use the translation itself. The footnotes and commentary will be included only if you permit it.
- **Your conditions:** we will follow any other conditions you set, and we will remove the text on request.

If you are willing, please let us know your terms, and whether you can provide a digital copy of the text. If you cannot, please tell us the edition we should use. We are happy to share a preview of the app before release.

JazakumAllahu khayran for your time and for your service to the Book of Allah.
