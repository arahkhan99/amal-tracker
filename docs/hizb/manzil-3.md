# al-Hizb al-Aʿzam — Manzil 3: Monday (يوم الاثنين)

- **Main text:** Nomani takhrij edition (Banuri Town), PDF pages 64–78. The printed page numbers match the PDF page numbers. The running head reads «المنزل الثالث / يوم الاثنين», but page 66 has «المنزل الثاني» by misprint.
- **Check text:** Hazim Dawud edition (Cairo 2011), «وِرْدُ يَوْمِ الإِثْنَيْنِ». This is PDF pages 57–72, printed pages 55–70.
- **Third check:** Dabhel 2006, PDF pages 99–127. I used it only to settle the disputed readings flagged below.
- **Count:** 52 numbered duas, in Nomani's numbering. None of them is a Qurʾānic passage: everything in this manzil is a hadith du'a.
- **Source tags:** Nomani's footnotes cite *al-Ḥiṣn al-Ḥaṣīn* of Ibn al-Jazarī ("Ḥiṣn") for almost every entry, then the collections that Ibn al-Jazarī or al-Suyūṭī named. The tags below repeat only those collection names. They contain no gradings and no wording from the notes.
- **Orthography:** the Arabic follows Nomani. I write the dagger alif as Nomani does (السَّمٰوٰتِ, الْحَيٰوةِ, الْقِيٰمَةِ, هٰذِهِ, إِلٰهَ, اللّٰهُ). Dawud spells these words with a full alif; that is only a spelling convention and is not counted as a difference.
- **Transliteration:** `'` marks ʿayn or hamza, and a doubled letter marks shadda.

### 1
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ ارْحَمْنِي بِتَرْكِ الْمَعَاصِي أَبَدًا مَا أَبْقَيْتَنِي، وَارْحَمْنِي أَنْ أَتَكَلَّفَ مَا لَا يَعْنِينِي، وَارْزُقْنِي حُسْنَ النَّظَرِ فِيمَا يُرْضِيكَ عَنِّي.

*Allahumma-rhamni bi-tarkil-ma'asi abadan ma abqaytani, warhamni an atakallafa ma la ya'nini, warzuqni husnan-nazari fima yurdika 'anni.*

O Allah, have mercy on me by helping me leave sins for good, for as long as You keep me alive. Have mercy on me so that I do not take on what does not concern me. Grant me good judgement in what makes You pleased with me.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn (du'a for memorising the Qurʾān); Tirmidhī, al-Ḥākim.

### 2
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ بَدِيعَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ، وَالْعِزَّةِ الَّتِي لَا تُرَامُ، أَسْأَلُكَ يَا اَللّٰهُ يَا رَحْمٰنُ بِجَلَالِكَ وَنُورِ وَجْهِكَ أَنْ تُلْزِمَ قَلْبِي حِفْظَ كِتَابِكَ كَمَا عَلَّمْتَنِي، وَارْزُقْنِي أَنْ أَتْلُوَهُ عَلَى النَّحْوِ الَّذِي يُرْضِيكَ عَنِّي.

*Allahumma badi'as-samawati wal-ard, dhal-jalali wal-ikram, wal-'izzatil-lati la turam, as'aluka ya Allahu ya Rahmanu bi-jalalika wa nuri wajhika an tulzima qalbi hifza kitabika kama 'allamtani, warzuqni an atluwahu 'alan-nahwil-ladhi yurdika 'anni.*

O Allah, Originator of the heavens and the earth, Lord of majesty and generosity, and of a might that no one can reach: I ask You, O Allah, O Most Merciful, by Your majesty and the light of Your Face, to make my heart hold fast to Your Book as You have taught it to me. Grant that I recite it in the way that makes You pleased with me.

Source (Nomani): same narration as 1.

### 3
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ بَدِيعَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ، وَالْعِزَّةِ الَّتِي لَا تُرَامُ، أَسْأَلُكَ يَا اَللّٰهُ يَا رَحْمٰنُ بِجَلَالِكَ وَنُورِ وَجْهِكَ أَنْ تُنَوِّرَ بِكِتَابِكَ بَصَرِي، وَأَنْ تُطْلِقَ بِهِ لِسَانِي، وَأَنْ تُفَرِّجَ بِهِ عَنْ قَلْبِي، وَأَنْ تَشْرَحَ بِهِ صَدْرِي، وَأَنْ تَسْتَعْمِلَ بِهِ بَدَنِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا يُعِينُنِي عَلَى الْحَقِّ غَيْرُكَ، وَلَا يُؤْتِيهِ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ.

*Allahumma badi'as-samawati wal-ard, dhal-jalali wal-ikram, wal-'izzatil-lati la turam, as'aluka ya Allahu ya Rahmanu bi-jalalika wa nuri wajhika an tunawwira bi-kitabika basari, wa an tutliqa bihi lisani, wa an tufarrija bihi 'an qalbi, wa an tashraha bihi sadri, wa an tasta'mila bihi badani, fa-innahu la yu'inuni 'alal-haqqi ghayruk, wa la yu'tihi illa ant, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil-'aliyyil-'azim.*

O Allah, Originator of the heavens and the earth, Lord of majesty and generosity, and of a might that no one can reach: I ask You, O Allah, O Most Merciful, by Your majesty and the light of Your Face, to light up my sight with Your Book. Free my tongue with it, lift the distress from my heart with it, open my chest with it, and put my body to work by it. No one but You helps me to the truth, and no one but You gives it. There is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Tremendous.

Source (Nomani): same narration as 1.

### 4

*Allahumma inni atubu ilayka minal-ma'asi, la arji'u ilayha abada.*

O Allah, I turn to You in repentance from sins, and I will never go back to them.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; al-Ḥākim.

### 5
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ مَغْفِرَتُكَ أَوْسَعُ مِنْ ذُنُوبِي، وَرَحْمَتُكَ أَرْجَى عِنْدِي مِنْ عَمَلِي.

*Allahumma maghfiratuka awsa'u min dhunubi, wa rahmatuka arja 'indi min 'amali.*

O Allah, Your forgiveness is wider than my sins, and I place more hope in Your mercy than in my own deeds.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; al-Ḥākim.

### 6
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنَّا.

*Allahumma innaka 'afuwwun tuhibbul-'afwa fa'fu 'anna.*

O Allah, You are the One who pardons, and You love to pardon, so pardon us.

### 7
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ، وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ.

*Allahumma-kfini bi-halalika 'an haramik, wa aghnini bi-fadlika 'amman siwak.*

O Allah, let what You have made lawful be enough for me, so that I need nothing You have forbidden. By Your bounty, make me free of need for anyone but You.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; Tirmidhī, al-Ḥākim.

### 8
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ فَارِجَ الْهَمِّ، كَاشِفَ الْغَمِّ، مُجِيبَ دَعْوَةِ الْمُضْطَرِّينَ، رَحْمٰنَ الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ وَرَحِيمَهُمَا، أَنْتَ تَرْحَمُنِي، فَارْحَمْنِي بِرَحْمَةٍ تُغْنِينِي بِهَا عَنْ رَحْمَةِ مَنْ سِوَاكَ.

*Allahumma farijal-hamm, kashifal-ghamm, mujiba da'watil-mudtarrin, Rahmanad-dunya wal-akhirati wa Rahimahuma, anta tarhamuni, farhamni bi-rahmatin tughnini biha 'an rahmati man siwak.*

O Allah, who relieves worry, who removes grief, who answers the call of those in desperate need, Most Merciful and Most Kind in this world and the next: it is You who has mercy on me. So have mercy on me with a mercy that frees me from needing the mercy of anyone besides You.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; al-Ḥākim, Ibn Marduwayh.

### 9
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ رَبَّ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، عَالِمَ الْغَيْبِ وَالشَّهَادَةِ، إِنِّي أَعْهَدُ إِلَيْكَ فِي هٰذِهِ الْحَيٰوةِ الدُّنْيَا أَنِّي أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ وَحْدَكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ، وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُكَ وَرَسُولُكَ، فَلَا تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي، فَإِنَّكَ إِنْ تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي تُقَرِّبْنِي مِنَ الشَّرِّ وَتُبَاعِدْنِي مِنَ الْخَيْرِ، وَإِنِّي لَا أَثِقُ إِلَّا بِرَحْمَتِكَ، فَاجْعَلْ لِي عِنْدَكَ عَهْدًا تُوَفِّينِيهِ يَوْمَ الْقِيٰمَةِ، إِنَّكَ لَا تُخْلِفُ الْمِيعَادَ.

*Allahumma rabbas-samawati wal-ard, 'alimal-ghaybi wash-shahadah, inni a'hadu ilayka fi hadhihil-hayatid-dunya anni ashhadu an la ilaha illa anta wahdaka la sharika lak, wa anna Muhammadan 'abduka wa rasuluk, fa-la takilni ila nafsi, fa-innaka in takilni ila nafsi tuqarribni minash-sharri wa tuba'idni minal-khayr, wa inni la athiqu illa bi-rahmatik, faj'al li 'indaka 'ahdan tuwaffinihi yawmal-qiyamah, innaka la tukhliful-mi'ad.*

O Allah, Lord of the heavens and the earth, Knower of the unseen and the seen: in this worldly life I give You my pledge that I bear witness that there is no god but You alone, with no partner, and that Muhammad is Your servant and Your Messenger. So do not leave me to myself. If You leave me to myself, You bring me nearer to evil and further from good, and I trust in nothing but Your mercy. So make a covenant for me with You that You will fulfil for me on the Day of Resurrection. You never break Your promise.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; Aḥmad.

### 10
status: draft

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللّٰهَ الَّذِي لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّومُ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ.

*Astaghfirullahal-ladhi la ilaha illa huwal-Hayyul-Qayyumu wa atubu ilayh.*

I ask forgiveness of Allah, besides whom there is no god, the Ever-Living, the Self-Sustaining, and I turn to Him in repentance.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; Abū Dāwūd, Tirmidhī.

### 11
status: draft

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَتُبْ عَلَيَّ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ.

*Rabbigh-fir li wa tub 'alayy, innaka antat-Tawwabur-Rahim.*

My Lord, forgive me and accept my repentance. You are the One who accepts repentance, the Merciful.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; the four Sunan, Ibn Ḥibbān.

### 12
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْهَرَمِ، وَالْمَغْرَمِ وَالْمَأْثَمِ.

*Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-'ajzi wal-kasal, wal-jubni wal-haram, wal-maghrami wal-ma'tham.*

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from helplessness and laziness, from cowardice and the frailty of old age, and from debt and sin.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; the six books (al-Jamāʿa).

### 13
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ عَذَابِ النَّارِ، وَفِتْنَةِ النَّارِ، وَفِتْنَةِ الْقَبْرِ، وَعَذَابِ الْقَبْرِ، وَشَرِّ فِتْنَةِ الْغِنَى، وَشَرِّ فِتْنَةِ الْفَقْرِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْقَسْوَةِ وَالْغَفْلَةِ وَالْعَيْلَةِ وَالذِّلَّةِ وَالْمَسْكَنَةِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْفَقْرِ وَالْكُفْرِ وَالْفُسُوقِ وَالشِّقَاقِ وَالسُّمْعَةِ وَالرِّيَاءِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الصَّمَمِ وَالْبَكَمِ وَالْبَرَصِ وَالْجُنُونِ وَالْجُذَامِ وَسَيِّئِ الْأَسْقَامِ.

*Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min 'adhabin-nar, wa fitnatin-nar, wa fitnatil-qabr, wa 'adhabil-qabr, wa sharri fitnatil-ghina, wa sharri fitnatil-faqr, wa a'udhu bika minal-qaswati wal-ghaflati wal-'aylati wadh-dhillati wal-maskanah, wa a'udhu bika minal-faqri wal-kufri wal-fusuqi wash-shiqaqi was-sum'ati war-riya', wa a'udhu bika minas-samami wal-bakami wal-barasi wal-jununi wal-judhami wa sayyi'il-asqam.*

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the punishment of the Fire and the trial of the Fire, from the trial of the grave and the punishment of the grave, from the evil of the trial of wealth, and from the evil of the trial of poverty. I seek refuge in You from hardness of heart, heedlessness, want, humiliation and wretchedness. I seek refuge in You from poverty, unbelief, wrongdoing, discord, seeking fame and showing off. I seek refuge in You from deafness, muteness, leprosy, madness, elephantiasis and all evil diseases.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; Ibn Ḥibbān, al-Ḥākim, al-Ṭabarānī (al-Muʿjam al-Ṣaghīr). For the leprosy clause he also cites al-Suyūṭī's al-Jāmiʿ al-Ṣaghīr (al-Ḥākim, al-Bayhaqī).

### 14
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِعِزَّتِكَ، لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، أَنْ تُضِلَّنِي، أَنْتَ الْحَيُّ الَّذِي لَا يَمُوتُ، وَالْجِنُّ وَالْإِنْسُ يَمُوتُونَ.

*Allahumma inni a'udhu bi-'izzatik, la ilaha illa ant, an tudillani, antal-Hayyul-ladhi la yamut, wal-jinnu wal-insu yamutun.*

O Allah, I seek refuge in Your might (there is no god but You) from Your letting me go astray. You are the Ever-Living who does not die, while jinn and humans die.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; Bukhārī, Muslim, Nasāʾī.

### 15
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنَّا نَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ جُهْدِ الْبَلَاءِ، وَدَرَكِ الشَّقَاءِ، وَسُوءِ الْقَضَاءِ، وَشَمَاتَةِ الْأَعْدَاءِ.

*Allahumma inna na'udhu bika min juhdil-bala', wa darakish-shaqa', wa su'il-qada', wa shamatatil-a'da'.*

O Allah, we seek refuge in You from crushing trials, from being overtaken by misery, from an evil decree, and from enemies gloating over us.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; Bukhārī.

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I know and from the evil of what I do not know.

### 18
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ زَوَالِ نِعْمَتِكَ، وَتَحَوُّلِ عَافِيَتِكَ، وَفُجَاءَةِ نِقْمَتِكَ، وَجَمِيعِ سَخَطِكَ.

### 19

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ سَمْعِي، وَمِنْ شَرِّ بَصَرِي، وَمِنْ شَرِّ لِسَانِي، وَمِنْ شَرِّ قَلْبِي، وَمِنْ شَرِّ مَنِيِّي.

### 20
status: draft

اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَدْمِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ التَّرَدِّي، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْغَرَقِ وَالْحَرَقِ وَالْهَرَمِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ يَتَخَبَّطَنِيَ الشَّيْطَانُ عِنْدَ الْمَوْتِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ أَمُوتَ فِي سَبِيلِكَ مُدْبِرًا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ أَمُوتَ لَدِيغًا.

*Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hadm, wa a'udhu bika minat-taraddi, wa a'udhu bika minal-gharaqi wal-haraqi wal-haram, wa a'udhu bika min an yatakhabbataniyash-shaytanu 'indal-mawt, wa a'udhu bika min an amuta fi sabilika mudbira, wa a'udhu bika min an amuta ladigha.*

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from being crushed under a collapsing building, and from falling from a height. I seek refuge in You from drowning, from burning, and from the frailty of old age. I seek refuge in You from Satan confusing me at the time of death. I seek refuge in You from dying in Your path while running from battle, and from dying of a venomous sting.

Source (Nomani): Ḥiṣn; Abū Dāwūd, Nasāʾī, al-Ḥākim.

## Uncertain readings

Line numbers count only the Arabic lines inside the box, from the top of each Nomani PDF page.

1. **p71, l.7 (dua 30):** there is no dua number in the margin beside «رَبِّ أَعِنِّي». I treated it as part of 30 because the next printed number, on p72, is 31. The number may simply have been left out of the print.
2. **p71, l.8 (dua 30):** «وَاهْدِنِي الْهُدَى وَيَسِّرِ الْهُدَى لِي». Both N and D have the first «الْهُدَى», which the usual Tirmidhī wording does not have. I kept it as printed.
3. **p76, l.2 (dua 45):** «كَلِمَةَ الْحَقِّ» is clear in the Arabic, but N's own Urdu translation renders it as «اخلاص», and D and Dabhel both read «الْإِخْلَاصِ». The reviewer should decide which to keep.
4. **p77, l.2 (dua 47):** «كُلَّ قَضَاءٍ لِي خَيْرًا». The space after «قَضَاءٍ» is crowded with marks. N and D both lack «قَضَيْتَهُ», which the Ibn Mājah wording has.
5. **p77, l.3 (dua 47):** the vowels of «رَشَدًا» are crowded. I read them as rashadan; D has «رُشْدًا».
6. **p66, l.7 (dua 9):** «وَإِنِّي» (with a kasra) against D's «وَأَنِّي». It looks like a kasra, but the mark is small.
7. **p68, l.1 (dua 15):** the damma on «جُهْدِ» is crowded, but it is probably a damma. D has a fatha.
8. **p71, l.3 (dua 28):** the hamza seat in «وَخَطَئِي» is unclear in the scan. I typed the standard spelling.
9. **p75, l.1 (dua 41):** the scan clearly reads «فَكَمَا», and D agrees. The Tirmidhī wording is «مَا رَزَقْتَنِي». I kept «فَكَمَا».
10. **Footnote markers:** 39, 41 and 52 have no footnote marker of their own. I assigned their source tags to the narration they continue.
11. **p66, running head:** it reads «المنزل الثاني», a misprint for الثالث. This does not affect the text.
