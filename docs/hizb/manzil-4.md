# al-Hizb al-Aʿzam — Manzil 4 (Tuesday, يوم الثلاثاء)

- **Base text:** Nomani takhrij edition (Banuri Town), scan `design-review/hizb-work/nomani.pdf`, **PDF pp. 79–95**. The printed page numbers are the same as the PDF page numbers. The running heads read «المنزل الرابع / يوم الثلثاء».
  - Manzil 3 ends on p. 78, and Manzil 5 («المنزل الخامس / يوم الاربعاء») starts on p. 96.
- **Collated against:** Hazim Dawud (Cairo 2011), `alexandrina-297077.pdf`, «وِرْدُ يَوْمِ الثُّلَاثَاءِ», PDF pp. 73–89 (printed pp. 71–87). Both editions start and end Tuesday at the same place, with the same content in the same order.
- **Count:** 58 numbered duas, following Nomani's numbering. Several numbers contain more than one hadith; they are shown as separate paragraphs inside one entry, exactly as Nomani boxes them.
- **Orthography:** typed in standard Arabic (imlāʾī) spelling. Indo-Pak rasm forms such as الحيوة / السموت / الظلمت are written الحياة / السماوات / الظلمات. The vowelling follows Nomani; places where it is unclear are listed at the end.
- **Source tags:** a short tag is given only where Nomani has a footnote marker on that text. "No separate note" means Nomani prints no marker there. Normally this is because the text continues the hadith noted at an earlier number. Nomani's notes are not reproduced and no gradings are given.
- **Status:** every entry is `status: draft`, pending review.

### 1
**Arabic**
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ الْمَسْأَلَةِ، وَخَيْرَ الدُّعَاءِ، وَخَيْرَ النَّجَاحِ، وَخَيْرَ الْعَمَلِ، وَخَيْرَ الثَّوَابِ، وَخَيْرَ الْحَيَاةِ، وَخَيْرَ الْمَمَاتِ، وَثَبِّتْنِي، وَثَقِّلْ مَوَازِينِي، وَحَقِّقْ إِيمَانِي، وَارْفَعْ دَرَجَتِي، وَتَقَبَّلْ صَلَاتِي، وَاغْفِرْ خَطِيئَتِي، وَأَسْأَلُكَ الدَّرَجَاتِ الْعُلَى مِنَ الْجَنَّةِ، آمِينَ.

**Transliteration:** Allahumma inni as'aluka khayra l-mas'alah, wa khayra d-du'a', wa khayra n-najah, wa khayra l-'amal, wa khayra th-thawab, wa khayra l-hayah, wa khayra l-mamat; wa thabbitni, wa thaqqil mawazini, wa haqqiq imani, warfa' darajati, wa taqabbal salati, waghfir khati'ati, wa as'aluka d-darajati l-'ula mina l-jannah. Amin.

**Translation:** O Allah, I ask You for the best of asking, the best of supplication, the best success, the best deeds, the best reward, the best life and the best death. Make me firm, make my scales heavy, make my faith true, raise my rank, accept my prayer and forgive my sin. And I ask You for the highest ranks of Paradise. Amin.

**Source (Nomani):** al-Hakim (al-Mustadrak); al-Tabarani (al-Kabir, al-Awsat), from Umm Salama. The note covers nos. 1–3.

### 9
**Arabic**
اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ أَوْسَعَ رِزْقِكَ عَلَيَّ عِنْدَ كِبَرِ سِنِّي، وَانْقِطَاعِ عُمُرِي.

يَا مَنْ لَا تَرَاهُ الْعُيُونُ، وَلَا تُخَالِطُهُ الظُّنُونُ، وَلَا يَصِفُهُ الْوَاصِفُونَ، وَلَا تُغَيِّرُهُ الْحَوَادِثُ، وَلَا يَخْشَى الدَّوَائِرَ، يَعْلَمُ مَثَاقِيلَ الْجِبَالِ، وَمَكَائِيلَ الْبِحَارِ، وَعَدَدَ قَطْرِ الْأَمْطَارِ، وَعَدَدَ وَرَقِ الْأَشْجَارِ، وَعَدَدَ مَا أَظْلَمَ عَلَيْهِ اللَّيْلُ وَأَشْرَقَ عَلَيْهِ النَّهَارُ، وَلَا تُوَارِي مِنْهُ سَمَاءٌ سَمَاءً، وَلَا أَرْضٌ أَرْضًا، وَلَا بَحْرٌ مَا فِي قَعْرِهِ، وَلَا جَبَلٌ مَا فِي وَعْرِهِ، اجْعَلْ خَيْرَ عُمُرِي آخِرَهُ، وَخَيْرَ عَمَلِي خَوَاتِيمَهُ، وَخَيْرَ أَيَّامِي يَوْمَ أَلْقَاكَ فِيهِ.

يَا وَلِيَّ الْإِسْلَامِ وَأَهْلِهِ، ثَبِّتْنِي بِهِ حَتَّى أَلْقَاكَ.

**Transliteration:** Allahumma j'al awsa'a rizqika 'alayya 'inda kibari sinni, wanqita'i 'umuri.
Ya man la tarahu l-'uyun, wa la tukhalituhu z-zunun, wa la yasifuhu l-wasifun, wa la tughayyiruhu l-hawadith, wa la yakhsha d-dawa'ir; ya'lamu mathaqila l-jibal, wa maka'ila l-bihar, wa 'adada qatri l-amtar, wa 'adada waraqi l-ashjar, wa 'adada ma azlama 'alayhi l-laylu wa ashraqa 'alayhi n-nahar; wa la tuwari minhu sama'un sama'an, wa la ardun ardan, wa la bahrun ma fi qa'rih, wa la jabalun ma fi wa'rih: ij'al khayra 'umuri akhirah, wa khayra 'amali khawatimah, wa khayra ayyami yawma alqaka fih.
Ya waliyya l-islami wa ahlih, thabbitni bihi hatta alqak.

**Translation:** O Allah, make Your provision most abundant for me when I am old and my life is coming to its end.
O You whom eyes do not see, whom thoughts cannot reach, whom no describer can describe, whom events do not change, and who does not fear the turns of fortune. You know the weight of the mountains, the measure of the seas, the number of raindrops, the number of leaves on the trees, and the number of all that the night darkens and the day lights up. No heaven hides another heaven from You, no earth another earth, no sea what lies at its bottom, and no mountain what lies in its rugged places. Make the best of my life its end, the best of my deeds the last of them, and the best of my days the day I meet You.
O Guardian of Islam and its people, keep me firm in it until I meet You.

**Source (Nomani):** first paragraph: al-Hakim (from ʿA'isha) and al-Tabarani (al-Awsat). "يا من لا تراه العيون…": al-Tabarani (al-Awsat). "يا ولي الإسلام…": al-Tabarani (al-Kabir).
**Nomani pages:** 80–81

### 15
**Arabic**
اللَّهُمَّ فَاجْعَلْ رَغْبَتِي إِلَيْكَ، وَاجْعَلْ غِنَايَ فِي صَدْرِي، وَبَارِكْ لِي فِيمَا رَزَقْتَنِي، وَتَقَبَّلْ مِنِّي، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ رَبِّي.

يَا مَنْ أَظْهَرَ الْجَمِيلَ، وَسَتَرَ عَلَيَّ الْقَبِيحَ، يَا مَنْ لَا يُؤَاخِذُ بِالْجَرِيرَةِ، وَلَا يَهْتِكُ السِّتْرَ، يَا عَظِيمَ الْعَفْوِ، يَا حَسَنَ التَّجَاوُزِ، يَا وَاسِعَ الْمَغْفِرَةِ، يَا بَاسِطَ الْيَدَيْنِ بِالرَّحْمَةِ، يَا صَاحِبَ كُلِّ نَجْوَى، يَا مُنْتَهَى كُلِّ شَكْوَى، يَا كَرِيمَ الصَّفْحِ، يَا عَظِيمَ الْمَنِّ، يَا مُبْدِئَ النِّعَمِ قَبْلَ اسْتِحْقَاقِهَا، يَا رَبَّنَا وَيَا سَيِّدَنَا وَيَا مَوْلَانَا وَيَا غَايَةَ رَغْبَتِنَا، أَسْأَلُكَ يَا اللهُ أَنْ لَا تَشْوِيَ خَلْقِي بِالنَّارِ.

**Transliteration:** Allahumma fa-j'al raghbati ilayk, waj'al ghinaya fi sadri, wa barik li fima razaqtani, wa taqabbal minni, innaka anta rabbi.
Ya man azhara l-jamil, wa satara 'alayya l-qabih; ya man la yu'akhidhu bil-jarirah, wa la yahtiku s-sitr; ya 'azima l-'afw, ya hasana t-tajawuz, ya wasi'a l-maghfirah, ya basita l-yadayni bir-rahmah, ya sahiba kulli najwa, ya muntaha kulli shakwa, ya karima s-safh, ya 'azima l-mann, ya mubdi'a n-ni'ami qabla stihqaqiha; ya rabbana wa ya sayyidana wa ya mawlana wa ya ghayata raghbatina, as'aluka ya Allahu an la tashwiya khalqi bin-nar.

**Translation:** O Allah, make my longing be for You. Put my sufficiency in my heart, bless me in what You have provided me, and accept from me; You are my Lord.
O You who made the beautiful appear and covered the ugly for me. O You who does not punish every offence and does not tear the veil away. O You whose pardon is great, whose overlooking is fine, and whose forgiveness is vast. O You whose hands are spread open with mercy. O Companion of every private plea, and final Recourse of every complaint. O You who forgive generously, whose favour is great, and who begin blessings before they are deserved. O our Lord, our Master and our Protector, the goal of all our longing: I ask You, O Allah, not to let the Fire burn my body.

**Source (Nomani):** first paragraph: no separate note (continues no. 14). "يا من أظهر الجميل…": al-Hakim (al-Mustadrak).
**Nomani pages:** 82–83

اللَّهُمَّ الْطُفْ بِي فِي تَيْسِيرِ كُلِّ عَسِيرٍ، فَإِنَّ تَيْسِيرَ كُلِّ عَسِيرٍ عَلَيْكَ يَسِيرٌ، وَأَسْأَلُكَ الْيُسْرَ وَالْمُعَافَاةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ.

**Transliteration:** Allahumma ltuf bi fi taysiri kulli 'asir, fa-inna taysira kulli 'asirin 'alayka yasir, wa as'aluka l-yusra wal-mu'afata fi d-dunya wal-akhirah.

**Translation:** O Allah, be gentle with me by making every hard thing easy, for making every hard thing easy is easy for You. And I ask You for ease and well-being in this world and the Hereafter.

**Source (Nomani):** al-Tabarani (al-Awsat), from Abu Hurayra.

## Uncertain readings (Nomani scan)

Line numbers count only the large Arabic lines inside the box, from the top of the page.

1. **p. 83, l. 1 (no. 15), «وَسَتَرَ عَلَيَّ الْقَبِيحَ».** A shadda seems to sit over the yāʾ of على, giving ʿalayya. The Urdu ("my faults") supports this. Dawud reads عَلَى الْقَبِيحِ. The final vowel on القبيح in Nomani is not clear.
2. **p. 89, l. 5 (no. 35), the first «وَزِدْنِي نُورًا».** The word looks like وَّا + زِدْنِي, with what may be an extra alif. The reading is possibly وَازْدُدْنِي, or it is just a calligraphic stroke. I typed وَزِدْنِي, as in Dawud, where the phrase is repeated twice.
3. **p. 81, l. 3 (no. 9), «وَمَكَائِيلَ».** Nomani clearly writes a hamza seat. Kept as printed; Dawud has مَكَايِيلَ.
4. **p. 87, l. 10 and p. 88, l. 1 (nos. 30, 31), «قَصُرَ».** The damma on the ṣād is crowded but readable. Dawud has قَصَّرَ.
5. **p. 86, l. 1 (no. 24), «عَيْشُ».** The second عيش carries a damma in Nomani. Dawud has the fatha.
6. **p. 85, l. 2 (end of no. 21).** The rubric «وَفِي الصَّحِيحِ كَانَ أَكْثَرُ دُعَاءِ النَّبِيِّ ﷺ» is printed inside box 21. I kept it there as a non-recited rubric. A reviewer may want to move it to the head of no. 22.
7. **p. 88, l. 9 (no. 32), no wāw before the second «إِنَّكَ».** The Urdu has «اور». Dawud agrees with the Arabic having no wāw, so this is resolved, but I list it for review.
