# al-Hizb al-Aʿzam: Manzil 1 (Saturday, يوم السبت)

- **Main text:** the Nomani edition (takhrij by ʿAbd al-Rashid al-Nuʿmani; Banuri Town), `design-review/hizb-work/nomani.pdf`, pages 16–49. The PDF page number is the same as the printed page number. Nomani numbers the duas 1–109.
- **Check text:** the Hazim Dawud edition (Cairo 2011), `alexandrina-297077.pdf`, «وِرْدُ يَوْمِ السَّبْتِ», printed pages 15–42 (PDF pages 17–44). Both editions start and end Saturday at the same places: from the Fatiha to «اللّٰهُمَّ رَبَّ جِبْرَئِيلَ…». Manzil 2 starts on Nomani p. 50 with «اللّٰهُمَّ اهْدِنِي فِيمَنْ هَدَيْتَ».
- **Arabic:** typed by us, following Nomani's wording and vowelling. It uses plain naskh spelling with the dagger alif where Nomani has one (السَّمٰوٰتِ، الرَّحْمٰنِ، إِلٰهَ). The Qurʾān passages follow the standard mushaf wording, and any place where Nomani quotes a different extent is noted.
- **Source tags:** these give the compilers that Nomani's footnote names, shortened. They are not gradings, and Nomani's notes are not copied. "—" means Nomani gives no footnote for that item.
- **Translations:** our own plain English. For Qurʾān passages: "Qurʾān translation from Pickthall".
- All entries: `status: draft`.

### 1
- **Arabic:** أَعُوذُ بِاللّٰهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطٰنِ الرَّجِيمِ. بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعٰلَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مٰلِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ ۝ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ ۝ اِهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ ۝ صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ ۝ آمِينَ.
- **Ref:** al-Fātiḥa 1:1–7, preceded by the taʿawwudh and followed by "Āmīn"
- **Transliteration:** A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim. Bismillahir-rahmanir-rahim. Al-hamdu lillahi rabbil-'alamin. Ar-rahmanir-rahim. Maliki yawmid-din. Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in. Ihdinas-siratal-mustaqim. Siratal-ladhina an'amta 'alayhim, ghayril-maghdubi 'alayhim wa lad-dallin. Amin.
- **English:** I seek refuge in Allah from Satan, the outcast. Then: Qurʾān translation from Pickthall. "Āmīn" means "O Allah, accept."
- **Source:** ʿAbd al-Razzaq (Musannaf), Ibn al-Mundhir (for the taʿawwudh); al-Daylami (for the Fatiha with Āmīn)
- **Nomani:** pp. 16–17

### 107
