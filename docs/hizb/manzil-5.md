### 1
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ طَهِّرْ قَلْبِي مِنَ النِّفَاقِ، وَعَمَلِي مِنَ الرِّيَاءِ، وَلِسَانِي مِنَ الْكَذِبِ، وَعَيْنِي مِنَ الْخِيَانَةِ، فَإِنَّكَ تَعْلَمُ خَائِنَةَ الْأَعْيُنِ وَمَا تُخْفِي الصُّدُورُ.
- **Qurʾānic wording:** «خَائِنَةَ الْأَعْيُنِ وَمَا تُخْفِي الصُّدُورُ» is from Ghafir 40:19.
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma tahhir qalbi minan-nifaq, wa ʿamali minar-riya', wa lisani minal-kadhib, wa ʿayni minal-khiyanah, fa innaka taʿlamu kha'inatal-aʿyuni wa ma tukhfis-sudur.
- **English:** O Allah, purify my heart of hypocrisy, my deeds of showing off, my tongue of lying, and my eyes of treachery. For You know the treachery of the eyes and what the breasts conceal.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Hakim al-Tirmidhi, *Nawadir al-Usul*; al-Khatib, *Tarikh Baghdad* (Umm Maʿbad).
- Nomani p. 96 · `status: draft`

### 2
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ ارْزُقْنِي عَيْنَيْنِ هَطَّالَتَيْنِ تَسْقِيَانِ الْقَلْبَ بِذُرُوفِ الدَّمْعِ مِنْ خَشْيَتِكَ، قَبْلَ أَنْ تَكُونَ الدُّمُوعُ دَمًا وَالْأَضْرَاسُ جَمْرًا.
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma-rzuqni ʿaynayni hattalatayni tasqiyanil-qalba bi dhurufid-damʿi min khashyatik, qabla an takunad-dumuʿu daman wal-adrasu jamra.
- **English:** O Allah, grant me two eyes that pour freely and water the heart with flowing tears out of fear of You, before tears become blood and molars become embers.
- **Source (Nomani):** Ibn ʿAsakir (Ibn ʿUmar).
- Nomani p. 96 · `status: draft`

### 3
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي قُدْرَتِكَ، وَأَدْخِلْنِي فِي رَحْمَتِكَ، وَاقْضِ أَجَلِي فِي طَاعَتِكَ، وَاخْتِمْ لِي بِخَيْرِ عَمَلِي، وَاجْعَلْ ثَوَابَهُ الْجَنَّةَ.

### 5

### 7
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ لَا يُدْرِكْنِي زَمَانٌ، وَلَا يُدْرِكُوا زَمَانًا لَا يُتَّبَعُ فِيهِ الْعَلِيمُ، وَلَا يُسْتَحْيَى فِيهِ مِنَ الْحَلِيمِ، قُلُوبُهُمْ قُلُوبُ الْأَعَاجِمِ، وَأَلْسِنَتُهُمْ أَلْسِنَةُ الْعَرَبِ.

### 10
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَتَّخِذُ عِنْدَكَ عَهْدًا لَنْ تُخْلِفَنِيهِ، فَإِنَّمَا أَنَا بَشَرٌ، فَأَيُّمَا مُؤْمِنٍ آذَيْتُهُ أَوْ شَتَمْتُهُ أَوْ جَلَدْتُهُ أَوْ لَعَنْتُهُ، فَاجْعَلْهَا لَهُ صَلَاةً وَزَكَاةً وَقُرْبَةً تُقَرِّبُهُ بِهَا إِلَيْكَ.
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma inni attakhidhu ʿindaka ʿahdan lan tukhlifanih, fa innama ana bashar, fa ayyuma mu'minin adhaytuhu aw shatamtuhu aw jaladtuhu aw laʿantuh, fajʿalha lahu salatan wa zakatan wa qurbatan tuqarribuhu biha ilayk.
- **English:** O Allah, I take a promise from You that You will not break: I am only a human being. So any believer I have hurt, insulted, struck or cursed, make that for him a blessing, a purification and a means of nearness by which You bring him close to You.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Bukhari; Muslim.

### 11
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ خَلَقْتَ نَفْسِي وَأَنْتَ تَوَفَّاهَا، لَكَ مَمَاتُهَا وَمَحْيَاهَا، إِنْ أَحْيَيْتَهَا فَاحْفَظْهَا بِمَا تَحْفَظُ بِهِ عِبَادَكَ الصَّالِحِينَ، وَإِنْ أَمَتَّهَا فَاغْفِرْ لَهَا وَارْحَمْهَا.

- **Source (Nomani):** Muslim (Ibn ʿUmar).

### 26
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ عَذِّبِ الْكَفَرَةَ أَهْلَ الْكِتَابِ وَالْمُشْرِكِينَ، الَّذِينَ يَجْحَدُونَ آيَاتِكَ، وَيُكَذِّبُونَ رُسُلَكَ، وَيَصُدُّونَ عَنْ سَبِيلِكَ، وَيَتَعَدَّوْنَ حُدُودَكَ، وَيَدْعُونَ مَعَكَ إِلٰهًا آخَرَ، لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، تَبَارَكْتَ وَتَعَالَيْتَ عَمَّا يَقُولُ الظَّالِمُونَ عُلُوًّا كَبِيرًا.
- **Qurʾānic wording:** the close echoes al-Isra' 17:43 («عَمَّا يَقُولُونَ عُلُوًّا كَبِيرًا»).
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma ʿadhdhibil-kafarata ahlal-kitabi wal-mushrikin, alladhina yajhaduna ayatik, wa yukadhdhibuna rusulak, wa yasudduna ʿan sabilik, wa yataʿaddawna hududak, wa yadʿuna maʿaka ilahan akhar, la ilaha illa ant, tabarakta wa taʿalayta ʿamma yaquluz-zalimuna ʿuluwwan kabira.
- **English:** O Allah, punish the disbelievers among the People of the Book and the idolaters, who deny Your signs, call Your messengers liars, bar others from Your path, overstep Your limits and call on another god besides You. There is no god but You. Blessed and exalted are You, high above what the wrongdoers say.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Tabarani, *Kitab al-Duʿa'* (ʿAli).

### 49
- **Arabic:** اللَّهُمَّ احْرُسْنِي بِعَيْنِكَ الَّتِي لَا تَنَامُ، وَاكْنُفْنِي بِرُكْنِكَ الَّذِي لَا يُرَامُ، وَارْحَمْنِي بِقُدْرَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، فَلَا أَهْلِكُ وَأَنْتَ رَجَائِي، فَكَمْ مِنْ نِعْمَةٍ أَنْعَمْتَ بِهَا عَلَيَّ قَلَّ لَكَ بِهَا شُكْرِي، وَكَمْ مِنْ بَلِيَّةٍ ابْتَلَيْتَنِي بِهَا قَلَّ لَكَ بِهَا صَبْرِي، فَيَا مَنْ قَلَّ عِنْدَ نِعْمَتِهِ شُكْرِي فَلَمْ يَحْرِمْنِي، وَيَا مَنْ قَلَّ عِنْدَ بَلِيَّتِهِ صَبْرِي فَلَمْ يَخْذُلْنِي، وَيَا مَنْ رَآنِي عَلَى الْخَطَايَا فَلَمْ يَفْضَحْنِي، يَا ذَا الْمَعْرُوفِ الَّذِي لَا يَنْقَضِي أَبَدًا، وَيَا ذَا النَّعْمَاءِ الَّتِي لَا تُحْصَى أَبَدًا، أَسْأَلُكَ أَنْ تُصَلِّيَ عَلَى (سَيِّدِنَا) مُحَمَّدٍ (صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ) وَعَلَى آلِ (سَيِّدِنَا) مُحَمَّدٍ (صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ)، وَبِكَ أَدْرَأُ فِي نُحُورِ الْأَعْدَاءِ وَالْجَبَابِرَةِ. اللَّهُمَّ أَعِنِّي عَلَى دِينِي بِالدُّنْيَا، وَعَلَى آخِرَتِي بِالتَّقْوَى، وَاحْفَظْنِي فِيمَا غِبْتُ عَنْهُ، وَلَا تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي فِيمَا حَضَرْتُهُ، يَا مَنْ لَا تَضُرُّهُ الذُّنُوبُ، وَلَا تَنْقُصُهُ الْمَغْفِرَةُ، هَبْ لِي مَا لَا يَنْقُصُكَ، وَاغْفِرْ لِي مَا لَا يَضُرُّكَ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْوَهَّابُ، أَسْأَلُكَ فَرَجًا قَرِيبًا، وَصَبْرًا جَمِيلًا، وَرِزْقًا وَاسِعًا، وَالْعَافِيَةَ مِنْ جَمِيعِ الْبَلَاءِ، وَأَسْأَلُكَ تَمَامَ الْعَافِيَةِ، وَأَسْأَلُكَ دَوَامَ الْعَافِيَةِ، وَأَسْأَلُكَ الشُّكْرَ عَلَى الْعَافِيَةِ، وَأَسْأَلُكَ الْغِنَى عَنِ النَّاسِ، وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ الْعَلِيِّ الْعَظِيمِ، يَا رَبِّ، يَا رَبِّ، يَا رَبِّ.
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma-hrusni bi ʿaynikal-lati la tanam, waknufni bi ruknikal-ladhi la yuram, warhamni bi qudratika ʿalayy, fa la ahliku wa anta raja'i, fa kam min niʿmatin anʿamta biha ʿalayya qalla laka biha shukri, wa kam min baliyyatin-ibtalaytani biha qalla laka biha sabri, fa ya man qalla ʿinda niʿmatihi shukri fa lam yahrimni, wa ya man qalla ʿinda baliyyatihi sabri fa lam yakhdhulni, wa ya man ra'ani ʿalal-khataya fa lam yafdahni, ya dhal-maʿrufil-ladhi la yanqadi abada, wa ya dhan-naʿma'il-lati la tuhsa abada, as'aluka an tusalliya ʿala sayyidina Muhammadin (sallallahu ʿalayhi wa sallam) wa ʿala ali sayyidina Muhammadin (sallallahu ʿalayhi wa sallam), wa bika adra'u fi nuhuril-aʿda'i wal-jababirah. Allahumma aʿinni ʿala dini bid-dunya, wa ʿala akhirati bit-taqwa, wahfazni fima ghibtu ʿanh, wa la takilni ila nafsi fima hadartuh, ya man la tadurruhudh-dhunub, wa la tanqusuhul-maghfirah, hab li ma la yanqusuk, waghfir li ma la yadurruk, innaka antal-wahhab, as'aluka farajan qariba, wa sabran jamila, wa rizqan wasiʿa, wal-ʿafiyata min jamiʿil-bala', wa as'aluka tamamal-ʿafiyah, wa as'aluka dawamal-ʿafiyah, wa as'alukash-shukra ʿalal-ʿafiyah, wa as'alukal-ghina ʿanin-nas, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billahil-ʿaliyyil-ʿazim, ya Rabb, ya Rabb, ya Rabb.
- **English:** O Allah, guard me with Your eye that never sleeps, shelter me in Your stronghold that no one can reach, and have mercy on me by Your power over me, so that I do not perish while You are my hope. How many blessings You have given me for which my thanks were small! How many trials You have tested me with in which my patience was small! O You before whose blessing my thanks were small, yet who did not deprive me; O You before whose trial my patience was small, yet who did not abandon me; O You who saw me in my sins and did not expose me; O Owner of kindness that never ends, and Owner of favours that can never be counted: I ask You to send blessings on (our master) Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) and on the family of (our master) Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him). Through You I push back against enemies and tyrants. O Allah, help me in my religion by means of this world, and in my Hereafter by means of God-consciousness. Guard me in what I am absent from, and do not leave me to myself in what I am present at. O You whom sins do not harm and whom forgiving does not diminish: grant me what does not diminish You, and forgive me what does not harm You. You are the Giver. I ask You for relief that comes soon, beautiful patience, ample provision and well-being from every trial. I ask You for complete well-being, lasting well-being and gratitude for well-being. I ask You to make me need no one but You. There is no power and no strength except through Allah, the Most High, the Mighty. O Lord, O Lord, O Lord.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Daylami (ʿAli); Ibn al-Najjar (Jaʿfar al-Sadiq). For «يا رب» three times: al-Daylami (Abu Hurayra) and Ibn Abi al-Dunya, *Kitab al-Duʿa'* (ʿA'isha).
- **Note:** the brackets around «سيدنا» are Nomani's. There are marginal variant readings on p. 113 (see the uncertain readings list).

## Uncertain readings (Nomani PDF page / line)

Line numbers count the Arabic lines inside the page's frame from the top. The Urdu lines are not counted.

1. **p. 108, line 3 (#39):** «وَلَا تُغْفِلْنَا». The print looks like «تَغْفُلْنَا» (fatha on ta, damma on fa?). The Urdu («غافل کر») and Dabhel («وَلَا تُغْفِلْنَا», p. 185) support تُغْفِلْنَا. Dawud has تُعْجِلْنَا.
2. **p. 105, line 6 (#32):** «جَبْرَئِيلَ». The vowel on the jim looks like a fatha, but the scan is crowded. Dawud has جِبْرِيلَ.
3. **p. 105, line 1 (#30):** «جُهْدِ» with a damma, as printed. Dawud has جَهْدِ. Both forms are attested.
4. **p. 107, line 1 (#35):** «أَسْبِغْ». The word is cramped at the top of the page. The well-known wording of this hadith has «وَانْشُرْ», but Dawud also prints وأسبغ, and the Urdu («کامل فرما») fits.
5. **p. 102, line 10 (#27):** there is no dua number at «سُبْحَانَكَ», only a footnote mark ٢. I kept the passage within #27. The reviewer should decide whether the app splits it.
6. **p. 112, line 2 (#48):** the same situation at «يَا مَنْ يَكْفِي» (footnote ٢, no number). Kept within #48.
7. **p. 104, lines 3–4 (#28/#29):** «بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الَّذِي لَا إِلٰهَ غَيْرُهُ» comes before the number 29, but in the hadith it belongs to #29. Placement to be decided.
8. **p. 110, lines 1–6 (#43):** the round brackets. There is a closing bracket after «عَرْشُكَ» and an opening one before «أَوْ سَائِلٍ». A mark before «سَيِّدِنَا» on line 1 may also be an opening bracket, or it may be the frame. Should the app show the bracketed words?
9. **p. 106, line 3 (#33):** «تُشْرِكَنَا». It is unclear whether there is a damma on the ta or a shadda (تُشَرِّكَنَا). Line 5 has «(فِيهِ)» in brackets.
10. **p. 98, line 4 (#10):** «مُؤْمِنٍ». The tanwin could be kasra or damma (Dawud has مُؤْمِنٌ).
11. **p. 111, line 2 (#45):** «خَمْسًا وَعِشْرِينَ» is set inside the Arabic box. I treat it as a rubric (recite 25 times), not as part of the dua.
12. **p. 113, right margin (#49):** marginal variants are printed against the text. They are «عَدَدًا» (at «لَا تُحْصَى أَبَدًا», line 6), «الْجَبَّارِينَ» (at «وَالْجَبَابِرَةِ», line 8), something like «خَطَرْتُ عَلَى» (at «فِيمَا حَضَرْتُهُ», line 10), and «لَا يَنْقُصُهُ الْعَفْوُ» (at «وَلَا تَنْقُصُهُ الْمَغْفِرَةُ», line 11). They are not in the main text and are only partly legible.
13. **p. 96, top:** the heading box (bismillah or a manzil title) is blank in this scan.
14. **p. 103, line 3 (#27):** «يَا رَءُوفُ ارْأَفْ بِي». The print shows رؤف / ارؤف with an Indo-Pak hamza seat. I normalised it (Dawud has رَءُوف / ارْؤُفْ).
