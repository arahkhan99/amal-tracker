# al-Hizb al-Aʿzam — Manzil 2 (Sunday, يوم الأحد)

- **Main text:** Nomani takhrij edition (Banuri Town), `nomani.pdf`, PDF pages **50–63** (running heads «المنزل الثاني / يوم الأحد»).
- **Checked against:** Hazim Dawud edition (Cairo 2011), `alexandrina-297077.pdf`, «وِرْدُ يَوْمِ الأَحَدِ», PDF pages 45–56 (printed pp. 43–54). Third check, used only where noted: Dabhel 2006, `dabhel-2006.pdf`, «اتوار», PDF pages ~62–95.
- **Day boundary:** both editions agree. Sunday starts with the qunūt «اللهم اهدني فيمن هديت» and ends with the ṣalāt al-ḥāja duʿāʾ «لا إله إلا الله الحليم الكريم…». The next day opens with «اللهم ارحمني بترك المعاصي».
- **Count:** 50 entries. Nomani prints the numbers 1–45 and 47–49. Entry 46 is not numbered in the scan, so it takes its number from its place in the sequence. Entry 50 has no printed number either (see "Uncertain readings").
- **Orthography (ours):** full ḥarakāt; dagger alif as in Nomani (اللّٰه، الرَّحْمٰن، السَّمٰوٰت، إِلٰه، الْحَيٰوة). No sukūn is written on the long-vowel letters ا/و/ي. The Indo-Pak shadda that marks tanwīn assimilation (نُورًا وَّفِي) is left out.
- **Source tags:** these are only the collections Nomani names in his footnotes, shortened. His notes are not reproduced, and no gradings are given.
- **Transliteration:** kept simple, with macrons for long vowels, ʿ for ʿayn and ' for hamza. Phrase-final words are in pause form.
- `status: draft` for every entry.

### 1
اَللّٰهُمَّ اهْدِنِي فِيمَنْ هَدَيْتَ، وَعَافِنِي فِيمَنْ عَافَيْتَ، وَتَوَلَّنِي فِيمَنْ تَوَلَّيْتَ، وَبَارِكْ لِي فِيمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَقِنِي شَرَّ مَا قَضَيْتَ، فَإِنَّكَ تَقْضِي وَلَا يُقْضٰى عَلَيْكَ، إِنَّهُ لَا يَذِلُّ مَنْ وَالَيْتَ، وَلَا يَعِزُّ مَنْ عَادَيْتَ، تَبَارَكْتَ رَبَّنَا وَتَعَالَيْتَ، نَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وَنَتُوبُ إِلَيْكَ، وَصَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-hdinī fīman hadayt, wa ʿāfinī fīman ʿāfayt, wa tawallanī fīman tawallayt, wa bārik lī fīmā aʿṭayt, wa qinī sharra mā qaḍayt, fa-innaka taqḍī wa lā yuqḍā ʿalayk, innahū lā yadhillu man wālayt, wa lā yaʿizzu man ʿādayt, tabārakta rabbanā wa taʿālayt, nastaghfiruka wa natūbu ilayk, wa ṣalla-llāhu ʿala-n-nabiyy.
- **English:** O Allah, guide me among those You have guided. Grant me well-being among those You have granted well-being. Take me into Your care among those You have taken into Your care. Bless me in what You have given, and shield me from the evil of what You have decreed. For You decree, and no one decrees over You. Whoever You befriend is never humbled, and whoever You oppose is never honoured. Blessed are You, our Lord, and exalted. We ask Your forgiveness and turn to You in repentance. And may Allah send blessings upon the Prophet.
- **Source (Nomani):** the four Sunan, Ibn Ḥibbān, al-Ḥākim, Ibn Abī Shayba; the closing blessing: al-Nasāʾī.
- status: draft

### 2
اَللّٰهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَلِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمَاتِ، وَأَلِّفْ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِهِمْ، وَأَصْلِحْ ذَاتَ بَيْنِهِمْ، وَانْصُرْهُمْ عَلَى عَدُوِّكَ وَعَدُوِّهِمْ، اَللّٰهُمَّ الْعَنِ الْكَفَرَةَ الَّذِينَ يَصُدُّونَ عَنْ سَبِيلِكَ، وَيُكَذِّبُونَ رُسُلَكَ، وَيُقَاتِلُونَ أَوْلِيَاءَكَ، اَللّٰهُمَّ خَالِفْ بَيْنَ كَلِمَتِهِمْ، وَزَلْزِلْ أَقْدَامَهُمْ، وَأَنْزِلْ بِهِمْ بَأْسَكَ الَّذِي لَا تَرُدُّهُ عَنِ الْقَوْمِ الْمُجْرِمِينَ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-ghfir lī wa lil-mu'minīna wal-mu'mināti wal-muslimīna wal-muslimāt, wa allif bayna qulūbihim, wa aṣliḥ dhāta baynihim, wanṣurhum ʿalā ʿaduwwika wa ʿaduwwihim. Allāhumma-lʿani-l-kafarata-lladhīna yaṣuddūna ʿan sabīlik, wa yukadhdhibūna rusulak, wa yuqātilūna awliyā'ak. Allāhumma khālif bayna kalimatihim, wa zalzil aqdāmahum, wa anzil bihim ba'saka-lladhī lā taruddu-hū ʿani-l-qawmi-l-mujrimīn.
- **English:** O Allah, forgive me and the believing men and women and the Muslim men and women. Bring their hearts together, set right what is between them, and help them against Your enemy and theirs. O Allah, curse the disbelievers who bar others from Your path, deny Your messengers and fight Your friends. O Allah, divide their word, shake their footing, and send down on them Your punishment, which You do not turn back from the criminal people.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Bayhaqī, al-Sunan al-Kubrā (from ʿUmar).
- status: draft

### 3
بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ، اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْتَعِينُكَ وَنَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وَنَسْتَهْدِيكَ وَنُؤْمِنُ بِكَ وَنَتُوبُ إِلَيْكَ وَنَتَوَكَّلُ عَلَيْكَ وَنُثْنِي عَلَيْكَ الْخَيْرَ وَنَشْكُرُكَ وَلَا نَكْفُرُكَ، وَنَخْلَعُ وَنَتْرُكُ مَنْ يَفْجُرُكَ، اَللّٰهُمَّ إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ، وَلَكَ نُصَلِّي وَنَسْجُدُ، وَإِلَيْكَ نَسْعٰى وَنَحْفِدُ، وَنَرْجُو رَحْمَتَكَ وَنَخْشٰى عَذَابَكَ الْجِدَّ، إِنَّ عَذَابَكَ الْجِدَّ بِالْكُفَّارِ مُلْحِقٌ.

- **Transliteration:** Bismi-llāhi-r-raḥmāni-r-raḥīm. Allāhumma innā nastaʿīnuka wa nastaghfiruka wa nastahdīka wa nu'minu bika wa natūbu ilayka wa natawakkalu ʿalayka wa nuthnī ʿalayka-l-khayra wa nashkuruka wa lā nakfuruk, wa nakhlaʿu wa natruku man yafjuruk. Allāhumma iyyāka naʿbud, wa laka nuṣallī wa nasjud, wa ilayka nasʿā wa naḥfid, wa narjū raḥmataka wa nakhshā ʿadhābaka-l-jidd, inna ʿadhābaka-l-jidda bil-kuffāri mulḥiq.
- **English:** In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Kind. O Allah, we ask Your help, Your forgiveness and Your guidance. We believe in You, turn to You in repentance and rely on You. We praise You with good, we thank You and we are not ungrateful to You. We cast off and leave whoever disobeys You. O Allah, You alone we worship; to You we pray and bow down; to You we hasten and strive. We hope for Your mercy and fear Your real punishment. Your real punishment will surely overtake the disbelievers.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Bayhaqī (al-Sunan al-Kubrā), Ibn Abī Shayba, al-Ṭaḥāwī; al-Suyūṭī (al-Durr al-Manthūr).
- status: draft

### 4
اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِرِضَاكَ مِنْ سَخَطِكَ، وَبِمُعَافَاتِكَ مِنْ عُقُوبَتِكَ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْكَ، لَا أُحْصِي ثَنَاءً عَلَيْكَ، أَنْتَ كَمَا أَثْنَيْتَ عَلَى نَفْسِكَ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bi-riḍāka min sakhaṭik, wa bi-muʿāfātika min ʿuqūbatik, wa aʿūdhu bika mink, lā uḥṣī thanā'an ʿalayk, anta kamā athnayta ʿalā nafsik.
- **English:** O Allah, I take refuge in Your pleasure from Your anger, and in Your pardon from Your punishment. I take refuge in You from You. I cannot count Your praises; You are as You have praised Yourself.
- **Source (Nomani):** Muslim and the four Sunan.
- status: draft

### 5
اَللّٰهُمَّ رَبَّ جِبْرَئِيلَ وَمِيكَائِيلَ وَإِسْرَافِيلَ وَمُحَمَّدٍ صَلَّى اللّٰهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ النَّارِ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma rabba Jibra'īla wa Mīkā'īla wa Isrāfīla wa Muḥammadin ṣalla-llāhu ʿalayhi wa sallam, aʿūdhu bika mina-n-nār.
- **English:** O Allah, Lord of Jibrīl, Mīkāʾīl and Isrāfīl, and of Muhammad (Allah bless him and give him peace), I take refuge in You from the Fire.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ḥākim, Ibn al-Sunnī.
- status: draft

### 6
اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ أَضِلَّ أَوْ أُضَلَّ، أَوْ أَزِلَّ أَوْ أُزَلَّ، أَوْ أَظْلِمَ أَوْ أُظْلَمَ، أَوْ أَجْهَلَ أَوْ يُجْهَلَ عَلَيَّ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika min an aḍilla aw uḍall, aw azilla aw uzall, aw aẓlima aw uẓlam, aw ajhala aw yujhala ʿalayy.
- **English:** O Allah, I take refuge in You from going astray or being led astray, from slipping or being made to slip, from wronging or being wronged, and from acting foolishly or being treated foolishly.
- **Source (Nomani):** Abū Dāwūd, Ibn Mājah.
- status: draft

### 7
اَللّٰهُمَّ اجْعَلْ فِي قَلْبِي نُورًا، وَفِي بَصَرِي نُورًا، وَفِي سَمْعِي نُورًا، وَعَنْ يَمِينِي نُورًا، وَعَنْ شِمَالِي نُورًا، وَمِنْ خَلْفِي نُورًا، وَمِنْ أَمَامِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْ مِنْ فَوْقِي نُورًا، وَمِنْ تَحْتِي نُورًا، اَللّٰهُمَّ أَعْطِنِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْ لِي نُورًا، وَفِي عَصَبِي نُورًا، وَفِي لَحْمِي نُورًا، وَفِي دَمِي نُورًا، وَفِي شَعْرِي نُورًا، وَفِي بَشَرِي نُورًا، وَفِي لِسَانِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْ فِي نَفْسِي نُورًا، وَأَعْظِمْ لِي نُورًا، وَاجْعَلْنِي نُورًا.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-jʿal fī qalbī nūrā, wa fī baṣarī nūrā, wa fī samʿī nūrā, wa ʿan yamīnī nūrā, wa ʿan shimālī nūrā, wa min khalfī nūrā, wa min amāmī nūrā, wa-jʿal min fawqī nūrā, wa min taḥtī nūrā. Allāhumma aʿṭinī nūrā, wa-jʿal lī nūrā, wa fī ʿaṣabī nūrā, wa fī laḥmī nūrā, wa fī damī nūrā, wa fī shaʿrī nūrā, wa fī basharī nūrā, wa fī lisānī nūrā, wa-jʿal fī nafsī nūrā, wa aʿẓim lī nūrā, wa-jʿalnī nūrā.
- **English:** O Allah, place light in my heart, light in my sight and light in my hearing. Place light on my right, light on my left, light behind me and light before me. Place light above me and light beneath me. O Allah, give me light and make light for me: light in my sinews, my flesh, my blood, my hair and my skin, and light on my tongue. Place light in my soul, make my light great, and make me light.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Bukhārī, Muslim, Abū Dāwūd, al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah; some of the added phrases: Muslim, al-Ḥākim.
- status: draft

### 8
اَللّٰهُمَّ افْتَحْ لَنَا أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ، وَسَهِّلْ لَنَا أَبْوَابَ رِزْقِكَ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-ftaḥ lanā abwāba raḥmatik, wa sahhil lanā abwāba rizqik.
- **English:** O Allah, open for us the doors of Your mercy, and make easy for us the doors of Your provision.
- **Source (Nomani):** Ibn Mājah, Abū ʿAwāna.
- status: draft

### 9

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-ʿṣimnī mina-sh-shayṭāni-r-rajīm.
- **English:** O Allah, protect me from Satan, the outcast.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah, Ibn Ḥibbān, al-Ḥākim, Ibn al-Sunnī.

### 10
اَللّٰهُمَّ اهْدِنِي لِأَحْسَنِ الْأَخْلَاقِ، لَا يَهْدِي لِأَحْسَنِهَا إِلَّا أَنْتَ، وَاصْرِفْ عَنِّي سَيِّئَهَا، لَا يَصْرِفُ عَنِّي سَيِّئَهَا إِلَّا أَنْتَ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-hdinī li-aḥsani-l-akhlāq, lā yahdī li-aḥsanihā illā ant, waṣrif ʿannī sayyi'ahā, lā yaṣrifu ʿannī sayyi'ahā illā ant.
- **English:** O Allah, guide me to the best of character; no one guides to the best of it but You. Turn its bad away from me; no one turns its bad away from me but You.
- **Source (Nomani):** Muslim, the four Sunan, Ibn Ḥibbān, al-Ṭabarānī.
- status: draft

### 11
اَللّٰهُمَّ بَاعِدْ بَيْنِي وَبَيْنَ خَطَايَايَ كَمَا بَاعَدْتَ بَيْنَ الْمَشْرِقِ وَالْمَغْرِبِ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma bāʿid baynī wa bayna khaṭāyāya kamā bāʿadta bayna-l-mashriqi wal-maghrib.
- **English:** O Allah, put a distance between me and my sins, as You have put a distance between the East and the West.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Bukhārī, Muslim, Abū Dāwūd, al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah.
- status: draft

### 12
اَللّٰهُمَّ اغْسِلْ خَطَايَايَ بِالْمَاءِ وَالثَّلْجِ وَالْبَرَدِ، وَنَقِّنِي مِنَ الْخَطَايَا كَمَا نَقَّيْتَ الثَّوْبَ الْأَبْيَضَ مِنَ الدَّنَسِ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-ghsil khaṭāyāya bil-mā'i wa-th-thalji wal-barad, wa naqqinī mina-l-khaṭāyā kamā naqqayta-th-thawba-l-abyaḍa mina-d-danas.
- **English:** O Allah, wash away my sins with water, snow and hail. Cleanse me of sins as You have cleansed a white garment of dirt.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī (al-Muʿjam al-Kabīr and al-Awsaṭ).
- status: draft

### 13
اَللّٰهُمَّ لَكَ الْحَمْدُ مِلْءَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَمِلْءَ الْأَرْضِ وَمِلْءَ مَا بَيْنَهُمَا وَمِلْءَ مَا شِئْتَ مِنْ شَيْءٍ بَعْدُ، أَهْلُ الثَّنَاءِ وَالْكِبْرِيَاءِ وَالْمَجْدِ، أَحَقُّ مَا قَالَ الْعَبْدُ، وَكُلُّنَا لَكَ عَبْدٌ، لَا مَانِعَ لِمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَلَا مُعْطِيَ لِمَا مَنَعْتَ، وَلَا رَادَّ لِمَا قَضَيْتَ، وَلَا يَنْفَعُ ذَا الْجَدِّ مِنْكَ الْجَدُّ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma laka-l-ḥamdu mil'a-s-samāwāti wa mil'a-l-arḍi wa mil'a mā baynahumā wa mil'a mā shi'ta min shay'in baʿd, ahlu-th-thanā'i wal-kibriyā'i wal-majd, aḥaqqu mā qāla-l-ʿabd, wa kullunā laka ʿabd. Lā māniʿa limā aʿṭayt, wa lā muʿṭiya limā manaʿt, wa lā rādda limā qaḍayt, wa lā yanfaʿu dha-l-jaddi minka-l-jadd.
- **English:** O Allah, to You belongs praise filling the heavens, filling the earth, filling all that is between them, and filling whatever else You will. You are worthy of praise, majesty and glory. This is the truest thing a servant has said, and we are all Your servants. No one can hold back what You give, no one can give what You hold back, and no one can turn back what You decree. A man's wealth or standing is of no use to him against You.
- **Source (Nomani):** Muslim, Abū Dāwūd, al-Tirmidhī, al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Mājah; the phrase «ولا رادّ لما قضيت»: al-Bazzār.
- status: draft

### 14
اَللّٰهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي كُلَّهُ، دِقَّهُ وَجِلَّهُ، وَأَوَّلَهُ وَآخِرَهُ، وَعَلَانِيَتَهُ وَسِرَّهُ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma-ghfir lī dhanbī kullah, diqqahū wa jillah, wa awwalahū wa ākhirah, wa ʿalāniyatahū wa sirrah.
- **English:** O Allah, forgive me all my sins: the small and the great, the first and the last, the open and the secret.
- **Source (Nomani):** Muslim, Abū Dāwūd.
- status: draft

### 15
رَبِّ أَعْطِ نَفْسِي تَقْوَاهَا، وَزَكِّهَا أَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْ زَكَّاهَا، أَنْتَ وَلِيُّهَا وَمَوْلَاهَا.

- **Transliteration:** Rabbi aʿṭi nafsī taqwāhā, wa zakkihā anta khayru man zakkāhā, anta waliyyuhā wa mawlāhā.
- **English:** My Lord, give my soul its God-consciousness and purify it, for You are the best to purify it. You are its Guardian and its Master.
- **Source (Nomani):** Aḥmad.

### 16
اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي ظَلَمْتُ نَفْسِي ظُلْمًا كَثِيرًا، وَلَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، فَاغْفِرْ لِي مَغْفِرَةً مِنْ عِنْدِكَ، وَارْحَمْنِي، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ.

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī ẓalamtu nafsī ẓulman kathīrā, wa lā yaghfiru-dh-dhunūba illā ant, fa-ghfir lī maghfiratan min ʿindik, warḥamnī, innaka anta-l-ghafūru-r-raḥīm.

### 48

## Uncertain readings and points for the reviewer

Line numbers count the Arabic text lines inside the page frame, from the top. Page numbers are `nomani.pdf` pages, which match the printed page numbers.

1. **p. 50, above line 1:** an empty framed box sits above entry 1. It may be a heading or basmala that did not print or scan. Dawud has a basmala here. It is not added to the text.
2. **p. 56, line 10:** the numeral ٢١ is printed beside «إِنَّكَ لَا تُخْلِفُ الْمِيعَادَ» rather than beside line 9, «رَبَّنَا وَآتِنَا». I took 21 to start at «رَبَّنَا وَآتِنَا», since 20 closes with ۝ at the end of line 8.
3. **p. 61, line 11:** «اللهم إنا نجعلك في نحورهم…» has no numeral, although other numerals on this page show in the margin. The next page begins at ٤٧, so I numbered it **46**.
4. **p. 63, line 1 (mid-line):** «لا إله إلا الله الحليم الكريم…» starts a new footnote source (ṣalāt al-ḥāja) but has no numeral. I numbered it **50**. It could instead be read as the end of 49. Dawud and Dabhel both print it as a separate duʿāʾ.
5. **p. 55, line 1:** «أَهْلُ» clearly has a ḍamma. Dawud reads «أَهْلَ». Either is grammatical; the reviewer should choose one for the app.
6. **p. 51, line 8:** «وَنُثْنِي عَلَيْكَ الْخَيْرَ» clearly has no «كُلَّهُ». Many Hanafi qunūt texts add it, as Dawud does.
7. **p. 62, line 9:** «وَنُورَ بَصَرِي» is clear, and Dawud agrees. Many hadith sources read «نُورَ صَدْرِي». It is kept as printed.
8. **p. 59, lines 9–10 (entry 34):** no footnote marker is visible. The source tag is inferred from the note on 33.
9. **p. 57 (entries 24–27):** the source note's marker sits at 25. The single tag is applied to all of 24–27.
10. **Lighter checks:** these ḥarakāt were crowded and were confirmed at higher zoom:
    - مُلْحِقٌ (p. 52, line 2), kasra on the ḥāʾ;
    - مُخْلِصًا (p. 58, line 1), kasra on the lām;
    - أَهْلُ (p. 55, line 1).

    All agree with Dawud except أَهْلُ (see 5).
