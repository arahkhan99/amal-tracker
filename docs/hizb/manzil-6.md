# al-Hizb al-Aʿzam — Manzil 6 (Thursday, يوم الخميس)

Mulla ʿAli al-Qari, *al-Hizb al-Aʿzam wa al-Wird al-Afkham*. Draft transcription for the Zad app, for scholarly review.

- **Check text:** Hazim Dawud (Cairo 2011), `alexandrina-297077.pdf`, «ورد يوم الخميس», PDF pp. 107–122 (printed pp. 105–120).

### 1
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ يَا كَبِيرُ يَا سَمِيعُ يَا بَصِيرُ، يَا مَنْ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَلَا وَزِيرَ لَهُ، وَيَا خَالِقَ الشَّمْسِ وَالْقَمَرِ الْمُنِيرِ، وَيَا عِصْمَةَ الْبَائِسِ الْخَائِفِ الْمُسْتَجِيرِ، وَيَا رَازِقَ الطِّفْلِ الصَّغِيرِ، وَيَا جَابِرَ الْعَظْمِ الْكَسِيرِ، أَدْعُوكَ دُعَاءَ الْبَائِسِ الْفَقِيرِ، كَدُعَاءِ الْمُضْطَرِّ الضَّرِيرِ، أَسْأَلُكَ بِمَعَاقِدِ الْعِزِّ مِنْ عَرْشِكَ، وَبِمَفَاتِيحِ الرَّحْمَةِ مِنْ كِتَابِكَ، وَبِالْأَسْمَاءِ الثَّمَانِيَةِ الْمَكْتُوبَةِ عَلَى قَرْنِ الشَّمْسِ، أَنْ تَجْعَلَ الْقُرْآنَ رَبِيعَ قَلْبِي وَجَلَاءَ حُزْنِي، رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا كَذَا وَكَذَا.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma yā Kabīru yā Samīʿu yā Baṣīr, yā man lā sharīka lahū wa lā wazīra lah, wa yā khāliqa sh-shamsi wal-qamari l-munīr, wa yā ʿiṣmata l-bāʾisi l-khāʾifi l-mustajīr, wa yā rāziqa ṭ-ṭifli ṣ-ṣaghīr, wa yā jābira l-ʿaẓmi l-kasīr, adʿūka duʿāʾa l-bāʾisi l-faqīr, ka-duʿāʾi l-muḍṭarri ḍ-ḍarīr, asʾaluka bi-maʿāqidi l-ʿizzi min ʿarshik, wa bi-mafātīḥi r-raḥmati min kitābik, wa bil-asmāʾi th-thamāniyati l-maktūbati ʿalā qarni sh-shams, an tajʿala l-Qurʾāna rabīʿa qalbī wa jalāʾa ḥuznī, rabbanā ātinā fi d-dunyā kadhā wa kadhā.
- **English:** O Allah, O Most Great, O All-Hearing, O All-Seeing, O You who have no partner and no helper; O Creator of the sun and the shining moon; O Protector of the wretched, the fearful and the one who seeks refuge; O Provider for the small child; O Setter of the broken bone. I call on You with the call of the wretched and needy, like the call of the desperate and afflicted. I ask You by the places where glory is bound to Your Throne, by the keys of mercy from Your Book, and by the eight names written on the rim of the sun, to make the Qurʾān the spring of my heart and the remover of my grief. Our Lord, give us in this world such-and-such (here one names one's need).
- **Source (Nomani):** Abū al-Shaykh al-Iṣbahānī, from Anas.
- **status: draft**

### 2
- **Arabic:** يَا مُؤْنِسَ كُلِّ وَحِيدٍ، وَيَا صَاحِبَ كُلِّ فَرِيدٍ، وَيَا قَرِيبًا غَيْرَ بَعِيدٍ، وَيَا شَاهِدًا غَيْرَ غَائِبٍ، وَيَا غَالِبًا غَيْرَ مَغْلُوبٍ، يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ، يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ، يَا نُورَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، يَا زَيْنَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، يَا جَبَّارَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، يَا عِمَادَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، يَا بَدِيعَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، يَا قَيَّامَ السَّمٰوٰتِ وَالْأَرْضِ، يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ، يَا صَرِيخَ الْمُسْتَصْرِخِينَ، وَمُنْتَهَى الْعَائِذِينَ، وَالْمُفَرِّجَ عَنِ الْمَكْرُوبِينَ، وَالْمُرَوِّحَ عَنِ الْمَغْمُومِينَ، وَمُجِيبَ دُعَاءِ الْمُضْطَرِّينَ، وَيَا كَاشِفَ الْكُرَبِ، يَا إِلٰهَ الْعَالَمِينَ، وَيَا أَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِينَ، مَنْزُولٌ بِكَ كُلُّ حَاجَةٍ.
- **Transliteration:** Yā mūnisa kulli waḥīd, wa yā ṣāḥiba kulli farīd, wa yā qarīban ghayra baʿīd, wa yā shāhidan ghayra ghāʾib, wa yā ghāliban ghayra maghlūb, yā Ḥayyu yā Qayyūm, yā Dha l-Jalāli wal-Ikrām, yā Nūra s-samāwāti wal-arḍ, yā Zayna s-samāwāti wal-arḍ, yā Jabbāra s-samāwāti wal-arḍ, yā ʿImāda s-samāwāti wal-arḍ, yā Badīʿa s-samāwāti wal-arḍ, yā Qayyāma s-samāwāti wal-arḍ, yā Dha l-Jalāli wal-Ikrām, yā Ṣarīkha l-mustaṣrikhīn, wa muntaha l-ʿāʾidhīn, wal-mufarrija ʿani l-makrūbīn, wal-murawwiḥa ʿani l-maghmūmīn, wa mujība duʿāʾi l-muḍṭarrīn, wa yā kāshifa l-kurab, yā Ilāha l-ʿālamīn, wa yā Arḥama r-rāḥimīn, manzūlun bika kullu ḥājah.
- **English:** O Companion of everyone who is alone, O Friend of everyone who is solitary, O You who are near and never far, present and never absent, victorious and never overcome. O Ever-Living, O Self-Subsisting, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Light of the heavens and the earth, O Adornment of the heavens and the earth, O Compeller of the heavens and the earth, O Pillar of the heavens and the earth, O Originator of the heavens and the earth, O Sustainer of the heavens and the earth, O Lord of Majesty and Honour. O Helper of those who cry for help, final refuge of those who seek refuge, Reliever of the distressed, Comforter of the grieving, Answerer of the call of the desperate, and Remover of hardships. O God of the worlds, O Most Merciful of the merciful: every need is brought to You.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Daylamī, *Musnad al-Firdaws*, from Anas.
- **status: draft**

### 3
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ مَوْتِ الْهَمِّ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ مَوْتِ الْغَمِّ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْجُوعِ فَإِنَّهُ بِئْسَ الضَّجِيعُ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخِيَانَةِ فَإِنَّهَا بِئْسَتِ الْبِطَانَةُ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika min mawti l-hamm, wa aʿūdhu bika min mawti l-ghamm, wa aʿūdhu bika mina l-jūʿi fa-innahū biʾsa ḍ-ḍajīʿ, wa aʿūdhu bika mina l-khiyānati fa-innahā biʾsati l-biṭānah.
- **English:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from dying in worry, and from dying in grief. I seek refuge in You from hunger, for it is an evil bedfellow, and from treachery, for it is an evil inner trait.
- **Source (Nomani):** Ibn al-Najjār, from Abū Hurayra.
- **status: draft**

### 4
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ اجْعَلْ سَرِيرَتِي خَيْرًا مِنْ عَلَانِيَتِي، وَاجْعَلْ عَلَانِيَتِي صَالِحَةً.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma jʿal sarīratī khayran min ʿalāniyatī, wajʿal ʿalāniyatī ṣāliḥah.
- **English:** O Allah, make my inner self better than my outward self, and make my outward self righteous.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Tirmidhī (one note covers duʿas 4 and 5).
- **status: draft**

### 5
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ صَالِحِ مَا تُؤْتِي النَّاسَ مِنَ الْمَالِ وَالْأَهْلِ وَالْوَلَدِ، غَيْرِ ضَالٍّ وَلَا مُضِلٍّ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī asʾaluka min ṣāliḥi mā tuʾti n-nāsa mina l-māli wal-ahli wal-walad, ghayri ḍāllin wa lā muḍill.
- **English:** O Allah, I ask You for the good of what You give people in wealth, family and children, neither astray nor leading others astray.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Tirmidhī.
- **status: draft**

### 6
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ اجْعَلْنَا مِنْ عِبَادِكَ الْمُنْتَخَبِينَ، الْغُرِّ الْمُحَجَّلِينَ، الْوَفْدِ الْمُتَقَبَّلِينَ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma jʿalnā min ʿibādika l-muntakhabīn, al-ghurri l-muḥajjalīn, al-wafdi l-mutaqabbalīn.
- **English:** O Allah, make us among Your chosen servants: those whose faces and limbs shine bright, the welcomed company of guests.
- **Source (Nomani):** Aḥmad, *Musnad*.
- **status: draft**

### 7
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ أَنْ أُشْرِكَ بِكَ شَيْئًا وَأَنَا أَعْلَمُ بِهِ، وَأَسْتَغْفِرُكَ لِمَا لَا أَعْلَمُ بِهِ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika min an ushrika bika shayʾan wa ana aʿlamu bih, wa astaghfiruka limā lā aʿlamu bih.
- **English:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from knowingly associating anything with You, and I ask Your forgiveness for what I do unknowingly.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī.
- **status: draft**

### 8
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِوَجْهِكَ الْكَرِيمِ، وَبِاسْمِكَ الْعَظِيمِ، مِنَ الْكُفْرِ وَالْفَقْرِ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bi-wajhika l-karīm, wa bismika l-ʿaẓīm, mina l-kufri wal-faqr.
- **English:** O Allah, I seek refuge in Your noble Face and Your mighty Name from disbelief and poverty.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī, *Kitāb al-Sunna*, from ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Abī Bakr.
- **status: draft**

### 9
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ قِنِي شَرَّ نَفْسِي، وَاعْزِمْ لِي عَلَى أَرْشَدِ أَمْرِي.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma qinī sharra nafsī, waʿzim lī ʿalā arshadi amrī.
- **English:** O Allah, protect me from the evil of my own self, and make me resolute in the most rightly guided of my affairs.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ḥākim (*al-Mustadrak*), al-Nasāʾī, Ibn Ḥibbān (via Ibn al-Jazarī, *al-Ḥiṣn*).
- **status: draft**

### 10
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ لَا تَكِلْنِي إِلَى نَفْسِي طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ، وَلَا تَنْزِعْ مِنِّي صَالِحَ مَا أَعْطَيْتَنِي، فَإِنَّهُ لَا نَازِعَ لِمَا أَعْطَيْتَ، وَلَا يَعْصِمُ ذَا الْجَدِّ مِنْكَ الْجَدُّ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma lā takilnī ilā nafsī ṭarfata ʿayn, wa lā tanziʿ minnī ṣāliḥa mā aʿṭaytanī, fa-innahū lā nāziʿa limā aʿṭayt, wa lā yaʿṣimu dha l-jaddi minka l-jadd.
- **English:** O Allah, do not leave me to myself for the blink of an eye, and do not take from me the good You have given me. No one can take away what You give, and a man's fortune does not protect him from You.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Bazzār, from Ibn ʿUmar.
- **status: draft**

### 11
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ غِنَى الْأَهْلِ وَالْمَوْلَى، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ يَدْعُوَ عَلَيَّ رَحِمٌ قَطَعْتُهَا.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī asʾaluka ghina l-ahli wal-mawlā, wa aʿūdhu bika an yadʿuwa ʿalayya raḥimun qaṭaʿtuhā.
- **English:** O Allah, I ask You for sufficiency for my family and my kin, and I seek refuge in You from being cursed by a relative whose ties I have cut.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī, from Zayd ibn Thābit.
- **status: draft**

### 12
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ نَفْسًا بِكَ مُطْمَئِنَّةً، تُؤْمِنُ بِلِقَائِكَ، وَتَرْضَى بِقَضَائِكَ، وَتَقْنَعُ بِعَطَائِكَ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī asʾaluka nafsan bika muṭmaʾinnah, tuʾminu bi-liqāʾik, wa tarḍā bi-qaḍāʾik, wa taqnaʿu bi-ʿaṭāʾik.
- **English:** O Allah, I ask You for a soul at peace with You, one that believes in meeting You, accepts Your decree and is content with what You give.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī (*al-Kabīr*) and al-Ḍiyāʾ al-Maqdisī, from Abū Umāma.
- **status: draft**

### 13
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَنْ يَمْشِي عَلَى بَطْنِهِ، وَمِنْ شَرِّ مَنْ يَمْشِي عَلَى رِجْلَيْنِ، وَمِنْ شَرِّ مَنْ يَمْشِي عَلَى أَرْبَعٍ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika min sharri man yamshī ʿalā baṭnih, wa min sharri man yamshī ʿalā rijlayn, wa min sharri man yamshī ʿalā arbaʿ.
- **English:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the evil of what crawls on its belly, of what walks on two legs, and of what walks on four.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī (*al-Kabīr*), from Ibn ʿAbbās.
- **status: draft**

### 14
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنِ امْرَأَةٍ تُشَيِّبُنِي قَبْلَ الْمَشِيبِ، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ وَلَدٍ يَكُونُ عَلَيَّ وَبَالًا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ مَالٍ يَكُونُ عَلَيَّ عَذَابًا، وَأَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ صَاحِبِ خَدِيعَةٍ، إِنْ رَأَى حَسَنَةً دَفَنَهَا، وَإِنْ رَأَى سَيِّئَةً أَفْشَاهَا.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī aʿūdhu bika mini mraʾatin tushayyibunī qabla l-mashīb, wa aʿūdhu bika min waladin yakūnu ʿalayya wabālā, wa aʿūdhu bika min mālin yakūnu ʿalayya ʿadhābā, wa aʿūdhu bika min ṣāḥibi khadīʿah, in raʾā ḥasanatan dafanahā, wa in raʾā sayyiʾatan afshāhā.
- **English:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from a wife who makes me grey before my time, from a child who becomes a burden on me, from wealth that becomes a torment for me, and from a deceitful companion who buries any good he sees and spreads any wrong he sees.
- **Source:** none named by Nomani.
- **status: draft**

### 15
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنَّكَ تَعْلَمُ سِرِّي وَعَلَانِيَتِي فَاقْبَلْ مَعْذِرَتِي، وَتَعْلَمُ حَاجَتِي فَأَعْطِنِي سُؤْلِي، وَتَعْلَمُ مَا فِي نَفْسِي فَاغْفِرْ لِي ذُنُوبِي.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innaka taʿlamu sirrī wa ʿalāniyatī faqbal maʿdhiratī, wa taʿlamu ḥājatī fa-aʿṭinī suʾlī, wa taʿlamu mā fī nafsī faghfir lī dhunūbī.
- **English:** O Allah, You know what I hide and what I show, so accept my excuse. You know my need, so grant my request. You know what is in my soul, so forgive my sins.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī (*al-Awsaṭ*) and Ibn ʿAsākir, from ʿĀʾisha.
- **status: draft**

### 16
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ إِيمَانًا يُبَاشِرُ قَلْبِي، وَيَقِينًا صَادِقًا، حَتَّى أَعْلَمَ أَنَّهُ لَا يُصِيبُنِي إِلَّا مَا كَتَبْتَ لِي، وَرِضًا بِمَا قَسَمْتَ لِي، إِنَّكَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī asʾaluka īmānan yubāshiru qalbī, wa yaqīnan ṣādiqā, ḥattā aʿlama annahū lā yuṣībunī illā mā katabta lī, wa riḍan bimā qasamta lī, innaka ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr.
- **English:** O Allah, I ask You for faith that fills my heart and for true certainty, so that I know nothing will befall me except what You have written for me. I ask You also for contentment with what You have allotted me. You have power over all things.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Ṭabarānī (*al-Awsaṭ*) and Ibn ʿAsākir, from ʿĀʾisha (the same note as 15).
- **status: draft**

### 17

- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma rabba s-samāwāti s-sabʿi wa rabba l-ʿarshi l-ʿaẓīm, Allāhumma kfinī kulla muhimmin min ḥaythu shiʾta wa min ayna shiʾt, ḥasbiya llāhu li-dīnī, ḥasbiya llāhu limā ahammanī, ḥasbiya llāhu liman baghā ʿalayy, ḥasbiya llāhu liman ḥasadanī, ḥasbiya llāhu liman kādanī bi-sūʾ, ḥasbiya llāhu ʿinda l-mawt, ḥasbiya llāhu ʿinda l-masʾalati fi l-qabr, ḥasbiya llāhu ʿinda l-mīzān, ḥasbiya llāhu ʿinda ṣ-ṣirāṭ, ḥasbiya llāhu lā ilāha illā huwa ʿalayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa rabbu l-ʿarshi l-ʿaẓīm.

Allah is enough for me at death, at the questioning in the grave, at the Scale and at the Bridge. *[9:129, Pickthall:]* "Allah sufficeth me. There is no God save Him. In Him have I put my trust, and He is Lord of the Tremendous Throne."

- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْتَجِيرُكَ مِنْ جَمِيعِ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ خَلَقْتَ، وَأَحْتَرِسُ بِكَ مِنْهُنَّ، وَأُقَدِّمُ بَيْنَ يَدَيَّ: بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ، قُلْ هُوَ اللّٰهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللّٰهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ ۝ مِنْ أَمَامِي، وَمِنْ خَلْفِي، وَعَنْ يَمِينِي، وَعَنْ شِمَالِي، وَمِنْ فَوْقِي، وَمِنْ تَحْتِي.
- **Qurʾān:** Sūrat al-Ikhlāṣ 112:1–4, quoted in full.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī astajīruka min jamīʿi kulli shayʾin khalaqt, wa aḥtarisu bika minhunn, wa uqaddimu bayna yadayy: bismi llāhi r-raḥmāni r-raḥīm, qul huwa llāhu aḥad, Allāhu ṣ-ṣamad, lam yalid wa lam yūlad, wa lam yakun lahū kufuwan aḥad, min amāmī, wa min khalfī, wa ʿan yamīnī, wa ʿan shimālī, wa min fawqī, wa min taḥtī.
- **English:** O Allah, I seek Your protection from every single thing You have created, and I guard myself through You against them. I place before me: In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful. *[112:1–4, Qurʾān translation from Pickthall:]* "Say: He is Allah, the One! Allah, the eternally Besought of all! He begetteth not nor was begotten. And there is none comparable unto Him." [I place this] in front of me, behind me, on my right, on my left, above me and below me.
- **Source (Nomani):** the same report as 47 (Anas). Nomani's note says the Ikhlāṣ is recited within it.
- **Note:** **no number is visible** at this point in the scan (p. 131, line 1). The number is inferred: Nomani goes from 47 (p. 130) to 49 (p. 131, line 6), and this is the only plausible break. Dawud (p. 118) and Dabhel (p. 227) both run this on as part of 47. The reviewer may prefer to merge 47 and 48.
- **status: draft**

### 49
- **Arabic:** خَلَقْتَ رَبَّنَا فَسَوَّيْتَ، وَقَدَّرْتَ رَبَّنَا فَقَضَيْتَ، وَعَلَى عَرْشِكَ اسْتَوَيْتَ، وَأَمَتَّ فَأَحْيَيْتَ، وَأَطْعَمْتَ فَأَشْبَعْتَ، وَأَسْقَيْتَ فَأَرْوَيْتَ، وَحَمَلْتَ فِي بَرِّكَ وَبَحْرِكَ عَلَى فُلْكِكَ وَعَلَى دَوَابِّكَ وَعَلَى أَنْعَامِكَ، فَاجْعَلْ لِي عِنْدَكَ وَلِيجَةً، وَاجْعَلْ لِي عِنْدَكَ زُلْفَى وَحُسْنَ مَآبٍ، وَاجْعَلْنِي مِمَّنْ يَخَافُ مَقَامَكَ وَوَعِيدَكَ، وَيَرْجُو لِقَاءَكَ، وَاجْعَلْنِي مِمَّنْ يَتُوبُ إِلَيْكَ تَوْبَةً نَصُوحًا، وَأَسْأَلُكَ عَمَلًا مُتَقَبَّلًا، وَعِلْمًا نَجِيحًا، وَسَعْيًا مَشْكُورًا، وَتِجَارَةً لَنْ تَبُورَ.
- **Transliteration:** Khalaqta rabbanā fa-sawwayt, wa qaddarta rabbanā fa-qaḍayt, wa ʿalā ʿarshika stawayt, wa amatta fa-aḥyayt, wa aṭʿamta fa-ashbaʿt, wa asqayta fa-arwayt, wa ḥamalta fī barrika wa baḥrika ʿalā fulkika wa ʿalā dawābbika wa ʿalā anʿāmik, fajʿal lī ʿindaka walījah, wajʿal lī ʿindaka zulfā wa ḥusna maʾāb, wajʿalnī mimman yakhāfu maqāmaka wa waʿīdak, wa yarjū liqāʾak, wajʿalnī mimman yatūbu ilayka tawbatan naṣūḥā, wa asʾaluka ʿamalan mutaqabbalā, wa ʿilman najīḥā, wa saʿyan mashkūrā, wa tijāratan lan tabūr.
- **English:** You created, our Lord, and gave due proportion. You decreed, our Lord, and carried it out. You established Yourself upon Your Throne. You gave death and gave life, You fed and satisfied, You gave drink and quenched, and You carried us over Your land and Your sea on Your ships, Your mounts and Your cattle. So grant me close intimacy with You, nearness to You and a good place of return. Make me one of those who fear standing before You and fear Your warning, and who hope to meet You. Make me one of those who turn to You in sincere repentance. I ask You for accepted deeds, successful knowledge, effort that is rewarded, and a trade that will never fail.
- **Source (Nomani):** al-Daylamī, from Abū Hurayra.
- **status: draft**

### 50
- **Arabic:** اللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّي أُشْهِدُكَ بِمَا شَهِدْتَ بِهِ عَلَى نَفْسِكَ، وَشَهِدَتْ بِهِ مَلَائِكَتُكَ وَأَنْبِيَاؤُكَ وَأُولُو الْعِلْمِ، وَمَنْ لَمْ يَشْهَدْ بِمَا شَهِدْتَ بِهِ فَاكْتُبْ شَهَادَتِي مَكَانَ شَهَادَتِهِ، أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ، تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ.
- **Transliteration:** Allāhumma innī ushhiduka bimā shahidta bihī ʿalā nafsik, wa shahidat bihī malāʾikatuka wa anbiyāʾuka wa ulu l-ʿilm, wa man lam yashhad bimā shahidta bihī faktub shahādatī makāna shahādatih, anta s-Salāmu wa minka s-salām, tabārakta yā Dha l-Jalāli wal-Ikrām.
- **English:** O Allah, I call You to witness that I bear witness to what You have testified about Yourself, and what Your angels, Your prophets and the people of knowledge have testified. Whoever does not bear witness to what You have testified, write my testimony in place of his. You are Peace, and from You comes peace. Blessed are You, O Lord of Majesty and Honour.
- **Source (Nomani):** Ibn Turkān (?), *Kitāb al-Duʿāʾ*, and al-Daylamī, from Anas.
- **status: draft**

## Uncertain readings

Nomani PDF page and Arabic line, counted from the top of the text frame on that page.

1. **p. 131, line 1: number 48.** No numeral is visible. The 48 is inferred from the jump 47 → 49 (see the note under 48). Both check texts run it on as part of 47.
2. **p. 130, line 1 (unnumbered takbīr line), and line 2, where "47" stands.** Whether «اللّٰهُ أَكْبَرُ ×3» closes 46 or opens 47 is unclear in Nomani. We placed it at the start of 47, following Dabhel p. 226.
3. **p. 130, line 6: «مِنْ سُوءٍ».**
   - The scan shows «مِنْ سُوءٍ», with a small caret-like mark above «وَجِوَارِكَ», which could be a correction sign for a missing word.
   - Nomani's Urdu ("ہر برائی سے" = "from every evil"), Dawud and Dabhel all point to «مِنْ كُلِّ سُوءٍ».
   - Kept as printed; please decide.
4. **p. 123, line 3: «وَاَسٰى».** We read it as «وَأَسَاءَ», written without the final hamza; Dawud has «وَأَسَاءَ».
5. **p. 123, line 3: «يَسُرُّهُ».** At 7× zoom the scan shows two dots below (yāʾ), not tāʾ. This agrees with Dawud, but the print is crowded.
6. **p. 119, line 7: «صَاحِبِ خَدِيعَةٍ».** The vowel under the bāʾ is not clear; it could be «صَاحِبٍ». We read the iḍāfa, as Dawud has it.
7. **p. 121, line 4: «مِنْ وَرَائِنَا».** The vowel on the mīm is faint. We read kasra (min), which the Urdu supports. Dawud has «مَنْ».
8. **p. 116, line 1: «مُؤْنِسَ».** Nomani writes «مُوْنِسَ» in Indo-Pak style; we normalised it to «مُؤْنِسَ», as in Dawud.
9. **p. 125, line 3: «فَمَسِّكْنِي».** The shadda is clear but the vowel under the sīn is crowded. Dawud agrees.
10. **p. 128, lines 1 and 3: «وَأَنَّ لَكَ» (fatha, not «وَإِنَّ») and «نَذِلَّ».** Both read as printed and agree with Dawud.
