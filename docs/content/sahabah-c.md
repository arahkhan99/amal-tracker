# Sahabi of the Month: set C

Six Companions, 31 daily facts each (26 for Saʿīd ibn Zayd, see his last note), ordered as a story: lineage and early life, Islam, deeds and character, worship, death.

Sources:
- **Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah**: *The Lives of the Sahabah*, Mufti Afzal Hoosen Elias's English translation, 3 vols. Printed page = PDF page + 2, except in vol. 2 up to pdf p. 134, where printed page = PDF page + 1 (checked against each page header).
- **al-Iṣābah**: Ibn Ḥajar, *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah* (Arabic scan, 8 vols). Printed page = PDF page − 1 in the volumes used here.

## Ṭalḥah ibn ʿUbaydillāh
- **Title:** The generous one, shield of the Prophet ﷺ at Uḥud

### C-TALHAH-01
- **Text:** Ṭalḥah ibn ʿUbaydillāh was from the Quraysh, of the clan of Taym. His kunya was Abū Muḥammad.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 430 (pdf p. 431)

### C-TALHAH-02
- **Text:** His mother was al-Ṣaʿbah, daughter of ʿAbdullāh al-Ḥaḍramī. She was a woman from Yemen and the sister of al-ʿAlāʾ ibn al-Ḥaḍramī.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 430 (pdf p. 431)

### C-TALHAH-03
- **Text:** His son Mūsā described him: fair with a reddish tinge, of middle height leaning to short, broad in the chest and shoulders, with large feet. When he turned to look, he turned his whole body.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 430 (pdf p. 431)

### C-TALHAH-04
- **Text:** As a young trader at the fair of Buṣrā in Shām, he met a monk who asked if anyone was from the Ḥaram. The monk told him that Aḥmad, the last of the prophets, would appear that month, and warned him not to let others reach him first.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 297 (pdf p. 295)

### C-TALHAH-05
- **Text:** He hurried back to Makkah and heard that Muḥammad, known as al-Amīn, was calling people to Allah, and that Abū Bakr followed him. Abū Bakr told him, "Go and follow him, for he calls to the truth."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, pp. 297–298 (pdf pp. 295–296)

### C-TALHAH-06
- **Text:** Abū Bakr took him to the Prophet ﷺ and he accepted Islam. The Prophet ﷺ was pleased to hear what the monk had said.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 298 (pdf p. 296)

### C-TALHAH-07
- **Text:** He was one of the first eight people to accept Islam, and one of the five who became Muslim through Abū Bakr.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 430 (pdf p. 431)

### C-TALHAH-08
- **Text:** After he became Muslim, he was seen between Ṣafā and Marwah with his arm in a sling, while his own mother followed him in anger, cursing him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 297 (pdf p. 295)

### C-TALHAH-09
- **Text:** A chief of the Quraysh seized him and Abū Bakr and tied the two together with one rope. Because of this they were called "the Two Companions".
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 298 (pdf p. 296)

### C-TALHAH-10
- **Text:** In Makkah the Prophet ﷺ made him a brother in Islam to al-Zubayr. In Madinah he made him a brother to Abū Ayyūb al-Anṣārī.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 431 (pdf p. 432)

### C-TALHAH-11
- **Text:** He made the Hijrah to Madinah with the party that brought the Prophet's ﷺ daughters and the family of Abū Bakr.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 370 (pdf p. 368)

### C-TALHAH-12
- **Text:** At the time of Badr he was away trading in Shām. The Prophet ﷺ still gave him his share of the spoils and his share of the reward.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 430 (pdf p. 431)

### C-TALHAH-13
- **Text:** At Uḥud, when the Muslims were scattered, he was among a small group who pledged to the Prophet ﷺ that they would stand by him until death.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 431 (pdf p. 432)

### C-TALHAH-14
- **Text:** As the enemy closed in, the Prophet ﷺ kept asking who would hold them back. Ṭalḥah offered himself each time, but was told to wait while one Anṣārī after another went forward. In the end he alone was left, and he fought as hard as all of them together.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, pp. 504–505 (pdf pp. 502–503)

### C-TALHAH-15
- **Text:** When his fingers were badly cut he cried out in pain. The Prophet ﷺ told him that had he said "Bismillāh" instead, the angels would have lifted him up to the heavens as people watched.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 505 (pdf p. 503)

### C-TALHAH-16
- **Text:** Wearing two coats of armour, the Prophet ﷺ could not climb onto a rock on the mountain. Ṭalḥah crouched beneath him and lifted him up. The Prophet ﷺ said that Ṭalḥah had made Jannah certain for himself.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 431 (pdf p. 432)
- **Note:** The word in the hadith is "awjaba", "he has made it binding". Al-Iṣābah cites Tirmidhi and Abū Yaʿlā.

### C-TALHAH-17
- **Text:** Qays ibn Abī Ḥāzim said he saw Ṭalḥah's hand withered and lame. It was the hand he had used to shield the Prophet ﷺ at Uḥud.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 432 (pdf p. 433)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites Bukhari.

### C-TALHAH-18
- **Text:** Whenever Abū Bakr spoke of Uḥud he would say, "That whole day belonged to Ṭalḥah." Afterwards they found him lying in a ditch with more than seventy wounds from spears, arrows and swords.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 290 (pdf p. 288)

### C-TALHAH-19
- **Text:** Leaving Uḥud, the Prophet ﷺ asked the poet Ḥassān to praise Ṭalḥah. ʿUmar too said a verse: he defended the Prophet ﷺ when everyone else had scattered. The Prophet ﷺ said, "You have spoken the truth, ʿUmar."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 528 (pdf p. 526)

### C-TALHAH-20
- **Text:** On one expedition they passed a salty spring called Baysān. The Prophet ﷺ renamed it Nuʿmān and called it good. Ṭalḥah bought it and gave it away in charity, and the Prophet ﷺ called him "Ṭalḥah al-Fayyāḍ", Ṭalḥah the overflowing in generosity.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 430 (pdf p. 431)

### C-TALHAH-21
- **Text:** When Allah accepted Kaʿb ibn Mālik's repentance, Ṭalḥah was the only one of the Muhājirūn who rose, hurried to him and shook his hand in congratulation. Kaʿb said he would never forget it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, pp. 457–458 (pdf pp. 455–456)

### C-TALHAH-22
- **Text:** Qabīṣah ibn Jābir said, "I kept company with Ṭalḥah, and I never saw a man who gave away more wealth without even being asked."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 432 (pdf p. 433)

### C-TALHAH-23
- **Text:** His wife Suʿdā said he once gave away a hundred thousand dirhams in a single day. That same day he was late for the masjid because she had to stitch his garment together.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 210 (pdf p. 208)

### C-TALHAH-24
- **Text:** Suʿdā once found him troubled and asked if she had upset him. "You are an excellent wife," he said. "It is wealth that has piled up with me." On her advice he gave it to his family and tribe: four hundred thousand.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 256 (pdf p. 254)

### C-TALHAH-25
- **Text:** He once sold land for seven hundred thousand. The money stayed in his house overnight, and he could not sleep for fear of it. By morning he had given it all away.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 256 (pdf p. 254)

### C-TALHAH-26
- **Text:** He saw ʿUmar slip into a house late at night. The next day he found a blind, disabled old woman there, whom ʿUmar had cared for and cleaned up after for years. Ṭalḥah told himself off: "Were you looking for ʿUmar's faults?"
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 124 (pdf p. 123)

### C-TALHAH-27
- **Text:** When a man doubted Abū Hurayrah's hadith, Ṭalḥah defended him. The rest of us had homes and families and came to the Prophet ﷺ morning and evening, he said, but Abū Hurayrah was always with him, so he heard what we did not.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 232 (pdf p. 230)

### C-TALHAH-28
- **Text:** He dreamt that a man who died a year after his martyred friend entered Jannah first. The Prophet ﷺ explained: had he not fasted another Ramaḍān and prayed thousands more rakʿahs in that year?
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 127 (pdf p. 125)

### C-TALHAH-29
- **Text:** After leading people in salāh, he asked them whether they were pleased with his leading, since he had not asked them first. He had heard that the salāh of an imam his people dislike does not rise past his ears.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 175 (pdf p. 173)

### C-TALHAH-30
- **Text:** He reported the du'ā the Prophet ﷺ made on seeing the new moon: that Allah let it rise over us with blessing and faith, safety and Islam. "My Lord and your Lord is Allah."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 390 (pdf p. 388)
- **Note:** The book cites Tirmidhi.

### C-TALHAH-31
- **Text:** ʿUmar named him among the six with whom the Prophet ﷺ was pleased when he left this world. Ṭalḥah passed away in Jumādā al-Ūlā of the year 36 AH, aged sixty-four.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 462 (pdf p. 460); al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 432 (pdf p. 433)
- **Note:** ʿUmar's words are from Ḥayāt; the date and age from al-Iṣābah, which also gives Ibn Saʿd's date of 10 Jumādā al-Ākhirah. Per the brief, nothing is said about the circumstances of his death; al-Iṣābah does not use the word "martyr" here, so the text says "passed away".

## al-Zubayr ibn al-ʿAwwām
- **Title:** The disciple (ḥawārī) of the Prophet ﷺ

### C-ZUBAYR-01
- **Text:** Al-Zubayr ibn al-ʿAwwām was from the Quraysh, of the clan of Asad. He was a cousin of the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 457 (pdf p. 458)

### C-ZUBAYR-02
- **Text:** His mother was Ṣafiyyah bint ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib, the Prophet's ﷺ aunt. She called him Abū al-Ṭāhir, but he took the kunya Abū ʿAbdillāh after his son, and that name stayed.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 457 (pdf p. 458)

### C-ZUBAYR-03
- **Text:** His father died while he was small, and his mother raised him firmly. When a relative objected, she replied in verse that she was training him to grow up wise and brave enough to lead an army.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 458 (pdf p. 459)

### C-ZUBAYR-04
- **Text:** He grew up very tall. When he rode, his feet trailed on the ground.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 458 (pdf p. 459)

### C-ZUBAYR-05
- **Text:** He came to the Prophet ﷺ with Ṭalḥah. The Prophet ﷺ explained Islam to them, recited the Qurʾān and told them of what Allah had promised. Both of them accepted Islam.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 104 (pdf p. 102)

### C-ZUBAYR-06
- **Text:** He accepted Islam when he was only eight years old, and made the Hijrah to Madinah at eighteen.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 298 (pdf p. 296)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah (vol. 2, p. 457) gives twelve as one view and eight as another.

### C-ZUBAYR-07
- **Text:** His uncle would wrap him in a straw mat, hang him up and light a fire beneath to smoke him, telling him to give up Islam. Al-Zubayr would answer, "I will never return to disbelief."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 298 (pdf p. 296)

### C-ZUBAYR-08
- **Text:** As a boy he heard a false rumour that the Prophet ﷺ had been seized. He ran through Makkah with his sword drawn. The Prophet ﷺ prayed for him and for his sword. It was the first sword drawn for Allah's sake.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 529 (pdf p. 527)

### C-ZUBAYR-09
- **Text:** Among the Muslims in Abyssinia, he was one of the youngest. When the Negus went to battle, al-Zubayr swam the Nile with an inflated waterskin tied to his chest, then came running back waving a cloth with news of victory.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 355 (pdf p. 353)

### C-ZUBAYR-10
- **Text:** At Badr he wore a yellow turban. The Prophet ﷺ said, "The angels have come down wearing the mark of al-Zubayr."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 459 (pdf p. 460)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites Ibn Saʿd.

### C-ZUBAYR-11
- **Text:** At Uḥud the standard-bearer of the enemy called for a challenger and the Muslims held back. Al-Zubayr alone stepped forward and defeated him. The Prophet ﷺ said that had al-Zubayr not gone, he would have gone himself.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 529 (pdf p. 527)

### C-ZUBAYR-12
- **Text:** ʿĀʾishah told his son ʿUrwah that his father was among those who answered the call of Allah and His Messenger ﷺ even after being wounded at Uḥud.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 459 (pdf p. 460)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites Bukhari. The verse is Āl ʿImrān 3:172.

### C-ZUBAYR-13
- **Text:** When his uncle Ḥamzah was martyred at Uḥud, his mother brought two cloths to shroud him. Finding a martyred Anṣārī beside Ḥamzah with nothing, they gave him one cloth, drawing lots for which cloth each would have.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 604 (pdf p. 602)

### C-ZUBAYR-14
- **Text:** At the time of Banū Qurayẓah the Prophet ﷺ asked who would bring him news of the enemy. Al-Zubayr volunteered, and the Prophet ﷺ said, "Every prophet has a ḥawārī, and my ḥawārī is al-Zubayr."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 459 (pdf p. 460)
- **Note:** A ḥawārī is a close, devoted helper.

### C-ZUBAYR-15
- **Text:** Al-Zubayr said the Prophet ﷺ once said to him, "May my father and mother be your ransom."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 459 (pdf p. 460)

### C-ZUBAYR-16
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ sent him with al-Miqdād to take down the body of Khubayb, who had been martyred at Tanʿīm. They found it still fresh. When the enemy chased them and they had to lay it down, the earth took it in.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 597 (pdf p. 595)

### C-ZUBAYR-17
- **Text:** He reported that the Prophet ﷺ made du'ā for him, for his children and for his descendants.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 381 (pdf p. 379)

### C-ZUBAYR-18
- **Text:** In the campaign in Egypt he climbed the wall of the enemy's fortress. Seeing this, they opened the gates and asked for terms.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 247 (pdf p. 245)

### C-ZUBAYR-19
- **Text:** At Yarmūk he charged through the enemy ranks alone and came back with two wounds on his shoulder, either side of an old wound from Badr. As a child, his son ʿUrwah would put his fingers into those scars.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 530 (pdf p. 528)

### C-ZUBAYR-20
- **Text:** A travelling companion once saw that his whole body was scarred by swords. Al-Zubayr said, "Every one of these wounds I received with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ in Allah's path."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 298 (pdf p. 296)

### C-ZUBAYR-21
- **Text:** His wife Asmāʾ, Abū Bakr's daughter, said that when he married her he owned nothing but a horse. She cared for the horse, fed the camel and carried date stones home from his land.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 705 (pdf p. 703)

### C-ZUBAYR-22
- **Text:** He narrated few hadith. When his son asked why, he said he had been close to the Prophet ﷺ, but he had heard him say: whoever says of me what I did not say, let him take his seat in the Fire.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 458 (pdf p. 459)

### C-ZUBAYR-23
- **Text:** When the verse came down that people will argue their cases before their Lord on the Day of Qiyāmah, he asked if wrongs between people would be judged too. Told yes, until everyone gets their due, he said, "Then it is a serious matter indeed."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 82 (pdf p. 80)

### C-ZUBAYR-24
- **Text:** When the verse came down that people will be asked about their blessings, he asked what blessings they would be asked about, when all they had were the two black things: dates and water.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 82 (pdf p. 80)

### C-ZUBAYR-25
- **Text:** ʿUthmān said of him, "He is the best of them, and the one the Messenger of Allah ﷺ loved most."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 458 (pdf p. 459)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites Aḥmad and Bukhari.

### C-ZUBAYR-26
- **Text:** ʿUmar said, "Al-Zubayr is one of the pillars of the religion."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 460 (pdf p. 461)

### C-ZUBAYR-27
- **Text:** Many Companions, including ʿUthmān, Ibn Masʿūd and ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf, named him in their wills to look after their families. He protected their wealth and spent on their children from his own.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 460 (pdf p. 461)

### C-ZUBAYR-28
- **Text:** He had a thousand slaves who worked and brought him their earnings. He gave it all away each night, and not a single dirham of it entered his house.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 256–257 (pdf pp. 254–255)

### C-ZUBAYR-29
- **Text:** When people left money with him for safekeeping, he took it as a loan instead, so that he would owe it back to them if it were lost. He never served as a governor or tax collector. He only went out to fight with the Prophet ﷺ and the caliphs after him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 257 (pdf p. 255)

### C-ZUBAYR-30
- **Text:** He told his son to settle his debts, and if any part became hard, to ask his Mawlā for help. "Who is your Mawlā?" his son asked. "Allah," he said. Whenever it became hard, his son would pray, "O Mawlā of al-Zubayr, settle his debt," and it was settled.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 257 (pdf p. 255)

### C-ZUBAYR-31
- **Text:** Al-Zubayr was martyred in Jumādā al-Ūlā of the year 36 AH, aged sixty-six or sixty-seven.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 2, p. 460 (pdf p. 461); Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 257 (pdf p. 255)
- **Note:** The date and age are from al-Iṣābah; Ḥayāt states that he was martyred. Nothing more is said about the circumstances, per the brief.

## ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf
- **Title:** The generous merchant who gave for Allah

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-01
- **Text:** ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn ʿAwf was from the Quraysh, of the clan of Zuhrah. His kunya was Abū Muḥammad.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 4, p. 290 (pdf p. 291)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-02
- **Text:** His mother, named as Ṣafiyyah or al-Shifāʾ, was also from Zuhrah. He was born about ten years after the Year of the Elephant.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 4, p. 290 (pdf p. 291)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-03
- **Text:** Before Islam his name was ʿAbd al-Kaʿbah, or ʿAbd ʿAmr. The Prophet ﷺ changed it to ʿAbd al-Raḥmān.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 4, p. 291 (pdf p. 292)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-04
- **Text:** Even in the days before Islam, he had made wine unlawful for himself.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 4, p. 293 (pdf p. 294)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-05
- **Text:** He was tall and fair with a reddish tinge, handsome, with large eyes, long lashes and hair falling below his ears. He did not dye his hair.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 4, p. 292 (pdf p. 293)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-06
- **Text:** He accepted Islam early, before the Prophet ﷺ began meeting the Muslims in the house of al-Arqam. He made both Hijrahs and was present at Badr and every battle after it.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 4, p. 290 (pdf p. 291)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-07
- **Text:** In Madinah the Prophet ﷺ made him a brother to Saʿd ibn al-Rabīʿ, who offered him half his wealth. ʿAbd al-Raḥmān prayed for blessing on Saʿd's family and wealth and said, "Just show me the way to the market."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 377 (pdf p. 375)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-08
- **Text:** He traded and soon married, giving a date stone's weight of gold as dowry. The Prophet ﷺ told him, "Give a walīmah, even if only with one goat."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, pp. 377–378 (pdf pp. 375–376)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-09
- **Text:** Allah put such barakah in his trade that he used to say that if he lifted a stone, he would hope to find gold or silver under it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 378 (pdf p. 376)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-10
- **Text:** At Badr he stood in the ranks between two young Anṣārī boys. Each quietly asked him to point out Abū Jahl, the enemy of the Prophet ﷺ. When he did, they rushed at him like falcons.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 535 (pdf p. 533)
- **Note:** The book cites Bukhari.

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-11
- **Text:** At Uḥud he received twenty-one wounds. An injury to his foot left him with a limp.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 492 (pdf p. 490)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-12
- **Text:** In one battle the Prophet ﷺ was told that an enemy troop was about to attack him. The Prophet ﷺ said, "The angels are fighting by his side." He was later found beside seven fallen enemies. He said he had struck down two of them; the rest were struck by someone he had never seen.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 540 (pdf p. 538)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-13
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ sent him to Dūmat al-Jandal. He invited its people to Islam for three days, and their Christian chief accepted Islam. With the Prophet's ﷺ permission he married the chief's daughter, Tumāḍir.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 140 (pdf p. 138)

### C-ABDURRAHMAN-14
- **Text:** He once went to the Prophet ﷺ to claim something promised to him, and heard him say that Allah makes self-sufficient whoever asks Him for it. He decided he would not ask the Prophet ﷺ after all.

- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 290 (pdf p. 288)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-08
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said, "Every nation has a trustworthy one, and the trustworthy one of this Ummah is Abū ʿUbaydah ibn al-Jarrāḥ."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 475 (pdf p. 476)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah notes the hadith is in the Ṣaḥīḥ.

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-09
- **Text:** When people from Yemen asked for a teacher, the Prophet ﷺ took Abū ʿUbaydah by the hand and sent him with them, saying, "This is the most trustworthy of this Ummah."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 237 (pdf p. 235)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-10
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ sent him to collect the jizyah from Bahrain. When the Anṣār heard he was back, they all came to Fajr, and the Prophet ﷺ warned them that he did not fear poverty for them, but that the world would open up and they would compete for it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 286 (pdf p. 284)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-11
- **Text:** He led an expedition of hundreds of Companions with only a bag of dates. In the end he gave each man a single date a day, which they sucked like infants and followed with water.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 329 (pdf p. 327)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-12
- **Text:** On that journey the sea cast up a huge fish. He told his hungry men, "We are envoys of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, out in Allah's path, so eat."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 476 (pdf p. 477)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah gives this from the Ṣaḥīḥ, about the army that ate from the ʿanbar fish.

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-13
- **Text:** Once the Prophet ﷺ passed a cup to him first. Abū ʿUbaydah said the Prophet ﷺ was more deserving and tried to hand it back. The Prophet ﷺ told him to drink, saying that blessing lies with our elders.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 471 (pdf p. 469)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-14
- **Text:** ʿĀʾishah was asked which Companion the Prophet ﷺ loved most. She said Abū Bakr, then ʿUmar, then Abū ʿUbaydah ibn al-Jarrāḥ.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 477 (pdf p. 478)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-15
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that, had he wished, he could have found something in the manners of each of his Companions to correct, except Abū ʿUbaydah.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 554 (pdf p. 552)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-16
- **Text:** Ibn ʿUmar said three men of the Quraysh had the finest faces, the best character and the deepest modesty: Abū Bakr, ʿUthmān and Abū ʿUbaydah.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 554 (pdf p. 552)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-17
- **Text:** When Abū Thaʿlabah asked the Prophet ﷺ for a good teacher, he sent him to Abū ʿUbaydah, saying he would teach him well and give him fine manners.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 242 (pdf p. 240)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-18
- **Text:** At Dhāt al-Salāsil he led reinforcements to ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀṣ, and ʿAmr claimed the command. Gentle by nature, Abū ʿUbaydah recalled the Prophet's ﷺ parting words, "Work together," and handed the command to ʿAmr.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 110 (pdf p. 109)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-19
- **Text:** After the Prophet ﷺ passed away, Abū Bakr offered him the khilāfah. He replied that he could never go ahead of the man whom the Prophet ﷺ had told to lead them in salāh.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 58 (pdf p. 57)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-20
- **Text:** ʿUmar sent him to Shām as commander, and most of Shām was opened at his hands.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 476 (pdf p. 477)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-21
- **Text:** In Shām ʿUmar asked, "Where is my brother?" and embraced him. In his house he found only a sword, a shield and a saddle. Abū ʿUbaydah slept on a saddle blanket, and said, "This is enough to take me to the grave."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 312 (pdf p. 310)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-22
- **Text:** ʿUmar once sent him a thousand dinars for a task he had done. He said, "I did not do it for you, son of al-Khaṭṭāb. I did it for Allah." He accepted only when ʿUmar told him the Prophet ﷺ had advised them not to refuse such gifts.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 275 (pdf p. 273)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-23
- **Text:** ʿUmar sent him four hundred dinars "for your needs" and had the messenger watch. Abū ʿUbaydah prayed for ʿUmar and sent it all out at once: seven to one person, five to another, until nothing was left.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 260 (pdf p. 258)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-24
- **Text:** He was once found weeping. The Prophet ﷺ had told him that if he lived to see the conquests, three servants and three mounts would be enough for him, and now his house was full. He recalled: "Closest to me is the one who meets me as I left him."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 295 (pdf p. 293)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-25
- **Text:** Walking among his troops, he said: many keep their clothes white while staining their religion. Wipe out old sins with new good deeds, for one good deed done afterwards rises above them all.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 513 (pdf p. 511)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-26
- **Text:** As governor of Shām he told the people, "By Allah, if any one of you is ahead of me in taqwā, I wish I were in his place."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 478 (pdf p. 479)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-27
- **Text:** When plague struck Shām, ʿUmar urgently called him back to Madinah. He wrote that he would not leave his army to save himself, and asked to be released from the order. ʿUmar wept on reading it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 125–126 (pdf pp. 124–125)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-28
- **Text:** He told the people that the plague was a mercy from their Lord and the du'ā of their Prophet ﷺ, and the way the righteous before them had died. He asked Allah for his share of it, and it reached him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 593 (pdf p. 591)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-29
- **Text:** On his deathbed in Jordan he advised them: keep up salāh, Zakāh, Ramaḍān, Hajj and ʿUmrah, encourage one another and wish your leaders well. The wisest is the one who obeys his Lord most and works hardest for his return. Then he told Muʿādh to lead the salāh.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 167 (pdf p. 165)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-30
- **Text:** Muʿādh told the people he had never seen a man with a heart freer of ill-will, further from evil, who loved the Ākhirah more or cared more for people. He led the funeral prayer on the open plain.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 66 (pdf p. 64)

### C-ABUUBAYDAH-31
- **Text:** He passed away in the plague of ʿAmwās in Shām in the year 18 AH, aged fifty-eight, and was buried in Jordan.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 478 (pdf p. 479)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah says the year is agreed upon (one odd report gives 17 AH); fifty-eight is al-Wāqidī's figure, Ibn Isḥāq gives forty-one. It names Fiḥl in Jordan, or Baysān, as his burial place.
