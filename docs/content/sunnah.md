# Monthly Sunnah cards (N01–N12)

Sources: Ascent to Felicity (Shurunbulālī, tr. Faraz A. Khan) for how to practise; Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3 (English, "Meaning and Message of the Traditions", Part V–VI), Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English, scan) and Zād al-Tālibīn (370 Provisions for the Seekers) for the hadith. Printed page first, then PDF page. Riyād al-Sālihīn printed page = PDF page − 1. Ascent to Felicity printed page = PDF page + 5.

### N01
- **Title:** Keep the miswāk close
- **Text:** Keep a miswāk by your bed and use it when you wake, and in every wudūʾ before or while rinsing the mouth. With none, a finger will do. The Prophet ﷺ used it whenever he woke, by night or day, and told his Companions he had urged them about it again and again.
- **Narrator:** ʿĀʾishah (waking); Anas (urging)
- **Collection:** Abu Dawood (waking); Bukhari (urging)
- **Source:** Ascent to Felicity, Purification, p. 42 and n. 59 (pdf p. 37); Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3, Part VI, p. 458–459, nos. 1531–1532 (pdf p. 438–439); Riyād al-Sālihīn, ch. 199, p. 764, no. 642 (pdf p. 765)
- **Note:** Ascent counts the miswāk as a sunnah of wudūʾ, so the card ties it to wudūʾ and waking, not to each salāh. The Riyād hadith no. 641 ("with every prayer") was left out for that reason.

### N02
- **Title:** Guard the sunnah of Fajr
- **Text:** Never leave the two sunnah rakʿahs before Fajr; they are the most emphasised of all the sunnah prayers. ʿĀʾishah said the Prophet ﷺ kept to no voluntary prayer more closely than these, and he said they are better than this world and all it holds.
- **Narrator:** ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her)
- **Collection:** Bukhari (his regularity); Muslim ("better than this world")
- **Source:** Ascent to Felicity, The Emphasized Sunna Prayers, p. 97 and n. 338 (pdf p. 92); Riyād al-Sālihīn, ch. 182, p. 729, nos. 579 and 581 (pdf p. 730)

### N03
- **Title:** Pray Duhā in the morning
- **Text:** Once the sun is well up (about 15–20 minutes after sunrise) and before midday, pray Duhā: four rakʿahs, or more up to twelve. ʿĀʾishah said the Prophet ﷺ used to pray four rakʿahs of Duhā and add as many as Allah willed.
- **Narrator:** ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her)
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Ascent to Felicity, The Emphasized Sunna Prayers, p. 99 and n. 342 (pdf p. 94); Riyād al-Sālihīn, ch. 191, p. 740, no. 606 (pdf p. 741)

### N04
- **Title:** Greet the masjid
- **Text:** When you enter a masjid, pray two rakʿahs before sitting, unless it is a time when salāh is disliked. Joining the congregation straight away also counts. The Prophet ﷺ said: when one of you enters the masjid, let him pray two rakʿahs before he sits.
- **Narrator:** Abū Qatādah (may Allah be pleased with him)
- **Collection:** Agreed upon (Bukhari and Muslim)
- **Source:** Ascent to Felicity, The Emphasized Sunna Prayers, p. 98 and n. 341 (pdf p. 93), with p. 71, n. 192 (pdf p. 66) on the disliked times; Riyād al-Sālihīn, ch. 192, p. 742, no. 610 (pdf p. 743)

### N05
- **Title:** Two rakʿahs after wudūʾ
- **Text:** After making wudūʾ, pray two rakʿahs before the washed limbs dry, outside the disliked times. The Prophet ﷺ heard the sound of Bilāl's sandals in Paradise, and Bilāl said the deed he hoped in most was praying whatever Allah willed after every wudūʾ, by day or night.
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him)
- **Collection:** (the book does not name one on this page)
- **Source:** Ascent to Felicity, The Emphasized Sunna Prayers, p. 98 (pdf p. 93); Riyād al-Sālihīn, ch. 193, p. 743, no. 611 (pdf p. 744)
- **Note:** Riyād prints this hadith with its Arabic chain but no collection name, so none is given.

### N06
- **Title:** Prepare well for Jumuʿah
- **Text:** On Friday, take a ghusl, clean yourself and use what scent you have. Go to Jumuʿah, don't push between two people, pray what you can and listen in silence to the khutbah. The Prophet ﷺ said such a person is forgiven what lies between that Friday and the one before.
- **Narrator:** Salmān al-Fārisī (may Allah be pleased with him)
- **Collection:** Bukhari
- **Source:** Ascent to Felicity, The Purificatory Bath, p. 49 (pdf p. 44) and n. 91, p. 50 (pdf p. 45); Riyād al-Sālihīn, ch. 194, p. 745, no. 615 (pdf p. 746)
- **Note:** Ascent lists the Friday ghusl as a sunnah (n. 91: an emphasised sunnah), so the card avoids the word "compulsory" that appears in Riyād no. 614.

### N07
- **Title:** Sleep in wudūʾ, right side
- **Text:** Before bed, make wudūʾ, then lie down on your right side with your right hand under your cheek. The Prophet ﷺ taught this to al-Barāʾ ibn ʿĀzib, and said that if he died that night, he would die upon the natural faith (fitrah).
- **Narrator:** al-Barāʾ ibn ʿĀzib (may Allah be pleased with him)
- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3, Part V, p. 159–161, nos. 1176–1177 (pdf p. 148–150); also Part VI, p. 458, no. 1530 and commentary (pdf p. 438)
- **Note:** The hand under the cheek comes from Hudhayfah's hadith (no. 1176, Bukhari) and its commentary. Ascent to Felicity does not cover this practice.

### N08
- **Title:** Be first with salām
- **Text:** Be the first to say "As-salāmu ʿalaykum", to those you know and those you don't. The Prophet ﷺ named this among the best deeds in Islam, and said the one who greets first is the most deserving of Allah's mercy and nearness.
- **Narrator:** ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar (best deeds); Abū Umāmah (greeting first)
- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim (best deeds); Musnad Ahmad, Tirmidhi and Abu Dawood (greeting first)
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3, Part VI, p. 437, no. 1491 and p. 440, no. 1494 (pdf p. 417 and 420); Zād al-Tālibīn, no. 97, p. 56 (pdf p. 56)
- **Note:** Maʿārif names the narrator of no. 1491 as ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿUmar; given as printed.

### N09
- **Title:** Meet others with a smile
- **Text:** This month, meet every Muslim you see, at home, at work and in the masjid, with a warm and cheerful face. The Prophet ﷺ counted even meeting your brother with a smiling face as a good deed.
- **Narrator:** (not named in the book)
- **Collection:** Muslim, Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Zād al-Tālibīn, no. 96, p. 56 (pdf p. 56)

### N10
- **Title:** Bismillāh and the right hand
- **Text:** Say "Bismillāh" before eating, eat with your right hand and from what is in front of you. The Prophet ﷺ taught this to the young ʿUmar ibn Abī Salamah at his own table.
- **Narrator:** ʿUmar ibn Abī Salamah
- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3, Part VI, p. 512, no. 1609 (pdf p. 490)

### N11
- **Title:** Praise Allah after eating
- **Text:** When you finish a meal or a drink, praise and thank Allah, even with a simple "Al-hamdu lillāh". The Prophet ﷺ said Allah is greatly pleased with the servant who praises Him when he eats and praises Him when he drinks.
- **Narrator:** Anas (may Allah be pleased with him)
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3, Part VI, p. 523–524, no. 1630 (pdf p. 501–502)

### N12
- **Title:** Visit the sick
- **Text:** Visit someone who is ill this month: ask after them and help with what they need. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Feed the hungry, visit the sick." On the Day of Judgement Allah will say to one who neglected a sick servant of His: had you visited him, you would have found Me with him.
- **Narrator:** Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī (visit the sick); Abū Hurayrah (Day of Judgement)
- **Collection:** Bukhari; Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3, Part VI, p. 411–412, nos. 1451–1452 (pdf p. 392–393); Zād al-Tālibīn, no. 152, p. 75 (pdf p. 75)
- **Note:** "You would have found Me with him" is the hadith's own wording, kept short and literal; no further explanation added. Maʿārif's commentary adds that visiting includes caring for the sick person, which the card reflects.
