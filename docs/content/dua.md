# Weekly Duʿās (D01–D52)

52 duʿās, one per week.

All items come from Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3 (English edition, "Meaning and Message of the Traditions, Part V"), Book of Adhkār and Duʿās. Page numbers are the printed page, then the PDF page.

**Arabic: read from the scanned page images** (approved for this set by the user; the PDF has no selectable Arabic). Conventions for the reviewer:
- The vowel marks and spelling follow the print (Indo-Pak style: e.g. اَسْئَلُكَ, اِنِّىْ, اَللّٰهُمَّ, hamza-less initial alif with a vowel). Letters are typed with standard Arabic code points (ك, ى/ي), not the Urdu shapes ک/ی the press uses.
- Words that the print runs together (e.g. لَاحَوْلَ) are typed with a normal space.
- The pronoun hāʾ (ـهٗ / ـهٖ) is typed with the small Indo-Pak sign where the scan shows one; where the scan is too faint to tell it from a plain damma/kasra, the Note says so.
- Anything we could not read with confidence is listed in the Note as "unsure: …".

Transliteration is deliberately simple (no macrons, dots or ʿ ʾ marks), per the user's decision.

Duʿās already in the app (Adhkār, Adhkār after Salāh, al-Hizb al-Aʿzam) were left out.

### D01
- **Title:** On waking in the morning
- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ بِكَ اَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ اَمْسَيْنَا وَبِكَ نَحْيَا وَبِكَ نَمُوْتُ وَاِلَيْكَ الْمَصِيْرُ
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma bika asbahna, wa bika amsayna, wa bika nahya, wa bika namutu, wa ilaykal-masir.
- **Text:** O Allah, by You we reach the morning and by You we reach the evening. By You we live and by You we die, and to You is the return.
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Abu Dawood and Tirmizi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1165/122, p. 150 (pdf p. 139)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ taught this to his Companions for the morning. In the evening the order is turned round ("bika amsayna wa bika asbahna …") and it ends "wa ilaykan-nushur" (and to You is the rising), as the same hadith says on p. 150–151.

### D02
- **Title:** The good of this day
- **Arabic:** اَصْبَحْنَا وَاَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلّٰهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِيْنَ اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّىْ اَسْئَلُكَ خَيْرَ هٰذَا الْيَوْمِ وَفَتْحَهٗ وَنَصْرَهٗ وَنُوْرَهٗ وَبَرَكَتَهٗ وَهُدَاهُ وَاَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا فِيْهِ وَشَرِّ مَا بَعْدَهٗ
- **Transliteration:** Asbahna wa asbahal-mulku lillahi rabbil-'alamin. Allahumma inni as'aluka khayra hadhal-yawmi, fat-hahu wa nasrahu wa nurahu wa barakatahu wa hudahu, wa a'udhu bika min sharri ma fihi wa sharri ma ba'dah.
- **Text:** We have reached the morning, and the whole dominion belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds. O Allah, I ask You for the good of this day: its openings, its help, its light, its blessing and its guidance. And I seek refuge in You from the evil in it and the evil that comes after it.
- **Narrator:** Abū Mālik al-Ashʿarī
- **Collection:** Abu Dawood
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1170/127, p. 155 (pdf p. 144)
- **Note:** In the evening one says the same, changing the words for evening (the hadith says "a similar supplication"). Unsure: the small sign on the pronoun hāʾ in فَتْحَهٗ, نَصْرَهٗ, نُوْرَهٗ, بَرَكَتَهٗ, بَعْدَهٗ (looks like the Indo-Pak inverted damma ٗ).

### D03
- **Title:** Thanks at bedtime
- **Arabic:** اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِىْ اَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا وَكَفَانَا وَاٰوَانَا فَكَمْ مِمَّنْ لَا كَافِىَ لَهٗ وَلَا مُؤْوِىَ
- **Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amana wa saqana wa kafana wa awana, fa kam mimman la kafiya lahu wa la mu'wi.
- **Text:** All praise is for Allah, who fed us, gave us drink, saw to our needs and gave us a home. How many there are with no one to provide for them and no one to give them shelter.
- **Narrator:** Anas
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1175/132, p. 159 (pdf p. 148)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ said this when he went to bed. Reviewer decided (14 Nov): فَكَمْ مِمَّنْ, with no لَهٗ at the end.

### D04
- **Title:** Sleeping and waking
- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ بِاسْمِكَ اَمُوْتُ وَاَحْيٰ ۔ اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِىْ اَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا اَمَاتَنَا وَاِلَيْهِ النُّشُوْرُ
- **Transliteration:** (On lying down) Allahumma bismika amutu wa ahya. (On waking) Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'da ma amatana wa ilayhin-nushur.
- **Text:** On lying down: O Allah, in Your name I die and I live. On waking: All praise is for Allah, who gave us life after He had made us die, and to Him is the rising.
- **Narrator:** Hudhayfah
- **Collection:** Bukhari (the book adds that Muslim has it from al-Barāʾ ibn ʿĀzib)
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1176/133, p. 159 (pdf p. 148)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ lay down with his hand under his cheek and said the first line; on waking he said the second. The book explains sleep as a small death and waking as a reminder of the Resurrection. The "۔" between the two duʿās is ours, to separate them. Unsure: the ending of وَاَحْيٰ (the print shows a small standing alif after the yāʾ).

### D05
- **Title:** Last words before sleep
- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ اَسْلَمْتُ وَجْهِىْ اِلَيْكَ وَفَوَّضْتُ اَمْرِىْ اِلَيْكَ وَاَلْجَاْتُ ظَهْرِىْ اِلَيْكَ رَهْبَةً وَّرَغْبَةً اِلَيْكَ لَا مَلْجَاَ وَلَا مَنْجَاَ مِنْكَ اِلَّا اِلَيْكَ اٰمَنْتُ بِكِتَابِكَ الَّذِىْ اَنْزَلْتَ وَنَبِيِّكَ الَّذِىْ اَرْسَلْتَ
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma aslamtu wajhi ilayk, wa fawwadtu amri ilayk, wa alja'tu zahri ilayk, rahbatan wa raghbatan ilayk. La malja'a wa la manja minka illa ilayk. Amantu bi kitabikal-ladhi anzalta wa nabiyyikal-ladhi arsalt.
- **Text:** O Allah, I have turned my face to You, handed my affairs to You and leant my back on You, in awe of You and longing for You. There is no refuge and no escape from You except to You. I believe in Your Book which You sent down, and in Your Prophet whom You sent.
- **Narrator:** al-Barāʾ ibn ʿĀzib
- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1177/134, p. 160 (pdf p. 149)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ told him to make wudūʾ, lie on his right side, and make these his last words; whoever dies that night dies upon the fitrah. When al-Barāʾ said "Your Messenger" in the last line, the Prophet ﷺ corrected him: "Your Prophet whom You sent." Unsure: the mark over the alif in وَاَلْجَاْتُ, and the fatha on the final alif of مَلْجَاَ and مَنْجَاَ.

### D06
- **Title:** After a bad dream
- **Arabic:** اَعُوْذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ غَضَبِهٖ وَعَذَابِهٖ وَمِنْ شَرِّ عِبَادِهٖ وَمِنْ هَمَزَاتِ الشَّيَاطِيْنِ وَاَنْ يَّحْضُرُوْنَ
- **Transliteration:** A'udhu bi kalimatillahit-tammati min ghadabihi wa 'adhabihi, wa min sharri 'ibadihi, wa min hamazatish-shayatini wa an yahdurun.
- **Text:** I seek refuge in Allah's perfect words from His anger and His punishment, from the evil of His servants, from the promptings of the devils, and from their coming near me.
- **Narrator:** ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀs
- **Collection:** Abu Dawood and Tirmizi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1184/141, p. 165 (pdf p. 154)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever is frightened in sleep should say this, and it will not harm him. ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr taught it to his older children. Unsure: whether the pronoun hāʾ in غَضَبِهٖ, عَذَابِهٖ, عِبَادِهٖ carries the standing kasra or a plain kasra.

### D07
- **Title:** Entering the toilet
- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّىْ اَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخُبُثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-khubuthi wal-khaba'ith.
- **Text:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the unclean devils, male and female.
- **Narrator:** Anas
- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, commentary to no. 1187/144, p. 169 (pdf p. 158)
- **Note:** Said before going in. The book first gives Zayd ibn Arqam's wording (Abu Dawood, Ibn Majah) beginning "aʿūdhu billāh"; this is the wording it quotes from Anas.

### D08
- **Title:** Leaving the toilet
- **Arabic:** اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِىْ اَذْهَبَ عَنِّى الْاَذٰى وَعَافَانِىْ
- **Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi adhhaba 'anniyal-adha wa 'afani.
- **Text:** All praise is for Allah, who took the harm away from me and kept me well.
- **Narrator:** Anas
- **Collection:** Ibn Majah
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1188/145, p. 169 (pdf p. 158)
- **Note:** The book calls this a fitting duʿāʾ: being able to relieve oneself is itself a great blessing.

### D09
- **Title:** Stepping out of the house
- **Arabic:** بِسْمِ اللهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ اِلَّا بِاللهِ
- **Transliteration:** Bismillahi, tawakkaltu 'alallah, la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.
- **Text:** In the name of Allah. I put my trust in Allah. There is no power and no strength except through Allah.
- **Narrator:** Anas
- **Collection:** Tirmizi and Abu Dawood
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1189/146, p. 170 (pdf p. 159)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says this on leaving home is told: "You are guided, you are taken care of, you are protected," and the devil turns away from him.

### D10
- **Title:** Safe from wrong on the way
- **Arabic:** بِسْمِ اللهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنَّا نَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ اَنْ نَّزِلَّ اَوْ نَضِلَّ اَوْ نَظْلِمَ اَوْ يُظْلَمَ عَلَيْنَا اَوْ نَجْهَلَ اَوْ يُجْهَلَ عَلَيْنَا
- **Transliteration:** Bismillahi, tawakkaltu 'alallah. Allahumma inna na'udhu bika min an nazilla aw nadilla, aw nazlima aw yuzlama 'alayna, aw najhala aw yujhala 'alayna.
- **Text:** In the name of Allah. I put my trust in Allah. O Allah, we seek refuge in You from slipping or going astray, from wronging others or being wronged, and from acting foolishly or being treated foolishly.
- **Narrator:** Umm Salamah
- **Collection:** Ahmad, Tirmizi and Nasa'i
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1190/147, p. 171 (pdf p. 160)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ said this whenever he went out of his house. Unsure: whether نَّزِلَّ carries a shadda on the nūn (the print runs مِنْ اَنْ نَزِلَّ together; it may be plain نَزِلَّ).

### D11
- **Title:** Coming home
- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ اَسْئَلُكَ خَيْرَ الْمَوْلِجِ وَخَيْرَ الْمَخْرَجِ بِسْمِ اللهِ وَلَجْنَا وَبِسْمِ اللهِ خَرَجْنَا وَعَلَى اللهِ رَبِّنَا تَوَكَّلْنَا
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma as'aluka khayral-mawliji wa khayral-makhraj. Bismillahi walajna, wa bismillahi kharajna, wa 'alallahi rabbina tawakkalna.
- **Text:** O Allah, I ask You for the good of coming in and the good of going out. In the name of Allah we come in, in the name of Allah we go out, and in Allah our Lord we put our trust.
- **Narrator:** Abū Mālik al-Ashʿarī
- **Collection:** Abu Dawood
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1191/148, pp. 171–172 (pdf pp. 160–161)
- **Note:** After saying it, one greets the family with salām.

### D12
- **Title:** Entering and leaving the masjid
- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِىْ اَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ ۔ اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّىْ اَسْئَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ
- **Transliteration:** (Going in) Allahummaftah li abwaba rahmatik. (Coming out) Allahumma inni as'aluka min fadlik.
- **Text:** Going in: O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy. Coming out: O Allah, I ask You for Your bounty.
- **Narrator:** Abū Usayd
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1192/149, p. 173 (pdf p. 162)
- **Note:** The book explains that "mercy" in the Qurʾān is mostly the blessings of the dīn and the Ākhirah, sought on entering the masjid, and "bounty" is mostly worldly provision, sought on going back out (al-Jumuʿah 62:10). The "۔" between the two duʿās is ours. Overlap check: al-Hizb al-Aʿzam has a different wording ("iftah lana abwaba rahmatik wa sahhil lana abwaba rizqik"), so this short pair was kept.

### D13
- **Title:** Before leaving a gathering
- **Arabic:** سُبْحَانَكَ اَللّٰهُمَّ وَبِحَمْدِكَ اَشْهَدُ اَنْ لَّا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اَنْتَ اَسْتَغْفِرُكَ وَاَتُوْبُ اِلَيْكَ
- **Transliteration:** Subhanakallahumma wa bihamdik, ash-hadu an la ilaha illa ant, astaghfiruka wa atubu ilayk.
- **Text:** Glory be to You, O Allah, and praise. I bear witness that there is no god but You. I ask Your forgiveness and I turn to You.
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Tirmizi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1193/150, p. 174 (pdf p. 163)
- **Note:** Whoever says it before getting up from a gathering where there was much idle talk is forgiven what happened there. The next hadith (no. 1194, Abu Dawood) adds that in a gathering of good or dhikr it seals it like a stamp. The author mentions pious people who said it often after speaking with others.

### D14
- **Title:** Going to the market
- **Arabic:** بِسْمِ اللهِ اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّىْ اَسْئَلُكَ خَيْرَ هٰذِهِ السُّوْقِ وَخَيْرَ مَا فِيْهَا وَاَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّهَا وَشَرِّ مَا فِيْهَا اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّىْ اَعُوْذُ بِكَ اَنْ اُصِيْبَ فِيْهَا صَفْقَةً خَاسِرَةً
- **Transliteration:** Bismillah. Allahumma inni as'aluka khayra hadhihis-suqi wa khayra ma fiha, wa a'udhu bika min sharriha wa sharri ma fiha. Allahumma inni a'udhu bika an usiba fiha safqatan khasirah.
- **Text:** In the name of Allah. O Allah, I ask You for the good of this market and the good of what is in it, and I seek refuge in You from its evil and the evil of what is in it. O Allah, I seek refuge in You from making a losing deal in it.
- **Narrator:** Buraydah
- **Collection:** Bayhaqi (in al-Daʿawāt al-Kabīr)
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1196/153, p. 177 (pdf p. 166)
- **Note:** The book explains that the market is a place where people easily forget Allah, so the Prophet ﷺ took extra care to remember Him there.

### D15
- **Title:** On seeing someone in trouble
- **Arabic:** اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِىْ عَافَانِىْ مِمَّا ابْتَلَاكَ بِهٖ وَفَضَّلَنِىْ عَلٰى كَثِيْرٍ مِّمَّنْ خَلَقَ تَفْضِيْلًا
- **Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi 'afani mimmab-talaka bihi, wa faddalani 'ala kathirin mimman khalaqa tafdila.
- **Text:** All praise is for Allah, who has kept me safe from what He has tried you with, and has favoured me over many of those He created.
- **Narrator:** ʿUmar ibn al-Khattāb and Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Tirmizi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1198/155, p. 180 (pdf p. 169)
- **Note:** Whoever says it on seeing someone afflicted will be kept safe from that trial. The book quotes Imām Bāqir: say it quietly, so the afflicted person does not hear it and feel worse. Unsure: whether بِهٖ has the standing kasra.

### D16
- **Title:** After eating or drinking
- **Arabic:** اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِىْ اَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا وَجَعَلَنَا مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِيْنَ
- **Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amana wa saqana wa ja'alana minal-muslimin.
- **Text:** All praise is for Allah, who gave us food and drink and made us Muslims.
- **Narrator:** Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī
- **Collection:** Abu Dawood and Tirmizi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1199/156, p. 181 (pdf p. 170)

### D17
- **Title:** Not by my own strength
- **Arabic:** اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ الَّذِىْ اَطْعَمَنِىْ هٰذَا الطَّعَامَ وَرَزَقَنِيْهِ مِنْ غَيْرِ حَوْلٍ مِّنِّىْ وَلَا قُوَّةٍ
- **Transliteration:** Alhamdu lillahil-ladhi at'amani hadhat-ta'ama wa razaqanihi min ghayri hawlin minni wa la quwwah.
- **Text:** All praise is for Allah, who fed me this food and provided it for me, without any power or strength of my own.
- **Narrator:** Muʿādh ibn Anas
- **Collection:** Tirmizi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1200/157, p. 181 (pdf p. 170)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ said that whoever says this after eating is forgiven his past sins. The book explains: a small deed, heavy with Allah, because it admits that every meal is His gift. Unsure: whether the mīm of مِّنِّىْ carries a shadda in the print.

### D18
- **Title:** For the one who fed you
- **Arabic:** اَفْطَرَ عِنْدَكُمُ الصَّائِمُوْنَ وَاَكَلَ طَعَامَكُمُ الْاَبْرَارُ وَصَلَّتْ عَلَيْكُمُ الْمَلَائِكَةُ
- **Transliteration:** Aftara 'indakumus-sa'imun, wa akala ta'amakumul-abrar, wa sallat 'alaykumul-mala'ikah.
- **Text:** May those who fast break their fast with you, may the righteous eat your food, and may the angels pray for you.
- **Narrator:** Anas
- **Collection:** Abu Dawood
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1202/159, pp. 182–183 (pdf pp. 171–172)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ said this after eating bread and oil at the home of Saʿd ibn ʿUbādah. The hadith before it teaches that praying for the host is how guests "repay" him.

### D19
- **Title:** Blessing for a household
- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَهُمْ فِيْمَا رَزَقْتَهُمْ وَاغْفِرْ لَهُمْ وَارْحَمْهُمْ
- **Transliteration:** Allahumma barik lahum fima razaqtahum, waghfir lahum warhamhum.
- **Text:** O Allah, bless them in what You have provided for them, forgive them and have mercy on them.
- **Narrator:** ʿAbdullāh ibn Busr
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1203/160, p. 183 (pdf p. 172)
- **Note:** The Prophet ﷺ was a guest of Busr; as he left, Busr held his mount's reins and asked him to pray for them, and he said this.

### D20

### D48
- **Title:** Waking in the night
- **Arabic:** لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهٗ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهٗ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْئٍ قَدِيْرٌ اَلْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ وَسُبْحَانَ اللهِ وَلَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللهُ وَاللهُ اَكْبَرُ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ اِلَّا بِاللهِ

### D49
- **Title:** Remembering Allah in the market
- **Arabic:** لَا اِلٰهَ اِلَّا اللهُ وَحْدَهٗ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهٗ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ يُحْيِىْ وَيُمِيْتُ وَهُوَ حَىٌّ لَّا يَمُوْتُ بِيَدِهِ الْخَيْرُ وَهُوَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْئٍ قَدِيْرٌ

### D52

- **Arabic:** اَللّٰهُمَّ رَبَّ السَّمٰوَاتِ السَّبْعِ وَرَبَّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيْمِ كُنْ لِىْ جَارًا مِنْ شَرِّ فُلَانِ ابْنِ فُلَانٍ وَّ شَرِّ الْجِنِّ وَالْاِنْسِ وَاَتْبَاعِهِمْ اَنْ يَّفْرُطَ عَلَىَّ اَحَدٌ مِنْهُمْ اَوْ اَنْ يَّطْغٰى عَزَّ جَارُكَ وَجَلَّ ثَنَاؤُكَ وَلَا اِلٰهَ غَيْرُكَ

- **Text:** O Allah, Lord of the seven heavens and Lord of the Mighty Throne, be my protector from the harm of so-and-so son of so-and-so, and from the harm of jinn and people and their followers, so that none of them rushes against me or oversteps. - **Collection:** Tabarani (al-Kabīr)
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1226/183, pp. 201–202 (pdf pp. 190–191)
