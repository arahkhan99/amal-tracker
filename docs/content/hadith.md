# Weekly hadith (H01–H52)

52 cards, one per week. Counts per book: Zād al-Tālibīn 14, Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 1 13, Maʿārif al-Hadīth vol. 3 13, Riyād al-Sālihīn 12.

### H01
- **Title:** Deeds and intentions
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said: deeds are judged only by intentions, and each person gets only what they intended. Whoever made hijrah for Allah and His Messenger, it counts for Allah and His Messenger. Whoever made it for worldly gain or a marriage, it counts for that alone.
- **Narrator:** Not named in the book at this point
- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim
- **Source:** Zād al-Tālibīn (Provisions for the Seekers), p. 24 (pdf p. 24)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: the book's Arabic does not extract as clean text (pdftotext gives broken letters).

### H02
- **Title:** What Allah looks at
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said: Allah does not look at your appearance or your wealth. He looks at your hearts and your deeds.
- **Narrator:** Not named in the book
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Zād al-Tālibīn (Provisions for the Seekers), no. 95, p. 56 (pdf p. 56)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext.

### H03
- **Title:** For Allah alone
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah says: "I have no need of any partner. If someone does a deed for Me and also for another, I leave him, and that deed, to the one he joined with Me."
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 1, no. 396/156, p. 451 (pdf p. 441)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext. A hadith qudsī.

### H04
- **Title:** Hearts that still fear
- **Text:** ʿĀʾishah asked about those who "give what they give with hearts afraid" (23:60). Were they people who drank and stole? The Prophet ﷺ said no: they are those who fast, pray and give charity, yet fear their deeds may not be accepted. They are the ones who race to do good.
- **Narrator:** ʿĀʾishah (may Allah be pleased with her)
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 1, no. 148/8, p. 248 (pdf p. 242)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext.

### H05
- **Title:** Seven in the shade
- **Text:** On the Day with no other shade, Allah will shade seven: a just ruler; a youth raised in worship; one whose heart is tied to the masjid; two who love for Allah; one who refuses sin, saying "I fear Allah"; one who gives in secret; one who remembers Allah alone and weeps.
- **Narrator:** Abū Saʿīd (as the English text gives it)
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 221, pp. 385–386 (pdf pp. 385–386)
- **Note:** The English names only Abū Saʿīd; the Arabic chain printed above it reads "from Abū Hurayrah or Abū Saʿīd" and is Tirmidhi's (it ends with Abū ʿĪsā's comment). User decided 14 Nov: cite Tirmidhi. No Arabic (scan).

### H06

- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim

### H07
- **Title:** Keep to the middle way
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that no one's deeds alone will save him, not even his own, unless Allah covers him with His grace and mercy. So do good sincerely and with moderation, worship in the morning, the afternoon and part of the night, and keep to the middle way. You will arrive.
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Bukhari
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 107, p. 228 (pdf p. 228)

### H08
- **Title:** Do not drop it
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said to ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr: "Do not be like so-and-so. He used to pray at night, then he gave it up."
- **Narrator:** ʿAbdullāh ibn ʿAmr ibn al-ʿĀs (may Allah be pleased with them)
- **Collection:** Bukhari
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 116, p. 236 (pdf p. 236)

### H09
- **Title:** One thing to hold on to
- **Text:** A man told the Prophet ﷺ that the good deeds were too many for him, and asked for one thing he could cling to. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Let your tongue stay soft with the remembrance of Allah."
- **Narrator:** ʿAbdullāh ibn Busr
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1053/10, p. 50 (pdf p. 41)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext.

### H10
- **Title:** A time for each duty
- **Text:** Hanzalah feared he was a hypocrite: with the Prophet ﷺ, Paradise and Hell felt before his eyes, but at home he forgot. The Prophet ﷺ said that if they kept that state always, angels would shake their hands. "But, Hanzalah, give each duty its due."
- **Narrator:** Hanzalah al-Usayyidī
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 113, pp. 232–233 (pdf pp. 232–233)
- **Note:** The book's English renders the last line as fulfilling one's obligations (giving Allah His due). Abū Bakr's part in the story is left out for length.

### H11
- **Title:** No good deed is small
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said: do not think any good deed small. If you have nothing else to give, meet your brother with a cheerful face. When you cook meat, add water to the broth and send some to your neighbour.
- **Narrator:** Abū Dharr al-Ghifārī
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 1, no. 271/31, p. 356 (pdf p. 347)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext.

### H12
- **Title:** Even half a date
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that each of you will stand before Allah with no screen and no interpreter in between. So shield yourself from the Fire, even with half a date given in charity, and if you do not have that, then with a kind word.
- **Narrator:** ʿAdī ibn Hātim
- **Collection:** Bukhari
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 326, pp. 535–536 (pdf pp. 535–536)

### H13
- **Title:** ʿIshāʾ and Fajr together
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said: whoever prays ʿIshāʾ in congregation, it is as if he stood half the night in prayer. And whoever prays Fajr in congregation, it is as if he prayed the whole night.
- **Narrator:** Not named in the book
- **Collection:** Muslim
- **Source:** Zād al-Tālibīn (Provisions for the Seekers), no. 242, p. 106 (pdf p. 106)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext.

### H14
- **Title:** Those who left their beds
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that on the Day of Judgement, when all are gathered on one wide plain, a caller will ask: "Where are those whose sides left their beds at night?" They will stand, few in number, and enter Paradise without reckoning.
- **Narrator:** Asmāʾ bint Yazīd
- **Collection:** Bayhaqi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 1, no. 100/100, p. 200 (pdf p. 194)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext. The book explains this as those who rose for Tahajjud.

### H15
- **Title:** How did you leave My servants?
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that angels take turns with you by night and by day, and meet at Fajr and ʿAsr. When they rise, Allah, who knows best, asks them how they left His servants. They say: "We left them praying, and we found them praying."
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Bukhari
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 554, p. 714 (pdf p. 715)

### H16
- **Title:** What wipes away sins
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ asked: "Shall I tell you what Allah uses to erase sins and raise ranks?" Doing wudūʾ fully even when it is hard, taking many steps to the masjid, and waiting for the next prayer after the last.
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 558, p. 716 (pdf p. 717)

### H17

- **Narrator:** Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī
- **Collection:** Bayhaqi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 1, no. 99/99, p. 199 (pdf p. 193)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext.

### H19

### H20
- **Title:** I am with him when he remembers Me
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah says: "I am as My servant thinks of Me, and I am with him when he remembers Me. If he remembers Me privately, I remember him privately. If he remembers Me in a gathering, I remember him in a better gathering."
- **Narrator:** Abū Hurayrah
- **Collection:** Bukhari and Muslim
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 3, no. 1047/4, pp. 45–46 (pdf pp. 36–37)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext. A hadith qudsī. The book explains "with him" as Allah's pleasure and approval, and the first line as: Allah treats His servant as the servant expects of Him.

### H21
- **Title:** Remember Allah
- **Text:** When two thirds of the night had passed, the Prophet ﷺ would rise and call: "O people, remember Allah! Remember Allah! The first blast is coming and the second follows it. Death is coming with all it brings."
- **Narrator:** Ubayy ibn Kaʿb
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Maʿārif al-Hadīth, vol. 1, no. 144/4, p. 244 (pdf p. 238)
- **Note:** Arabic omitted: garbled in pdftotext. The book explains he did this to wake his household and the believers for Tahajjud and dhikr.

### H26
- **Title:** A tear for Allah
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said: when tears fall from a believer's eyes out of fear of Allah, even as little as a fly's head, and run down his face, Allah keeps that face from the Fire.
- **Narrator:** ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd
- **Collection:** Ibn Majah

### H27
- **Title:** Hope reaches past the line
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ drew a square with a line in the middle running out past it. The line was a person, the square his set term around him, the small lines the troubles that strike him, and the line outside the square his hopes.
- **Narrator:** ʿAbdullāh ibn Masʿūd
- **Collection:** Tirmidhi
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 259, p. 447 (pdf p. 447)

### H28
- **Title:** The bier speaks
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said: when a body is carried on the shoulders, if it was righteous it says, "Take me on, quickly!" If not, it cries, "Woe to me, where are you taking me?" Every creature hears it except people.
- **Narrator:** Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī
- **Collection:** Bukhari
- **Source:** Riyād al-Sālihīn (abridged English), no. 217, p. 382 (pdf p. 382)

### H34

### H40
- **Title:** One part of mercy
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said: Allah divided mercy into a hundred parts. He kept ninety-nine with Him and sent one part to the earth. From that one part, creatures show mercy to each other, so that a mare lifts her hoof away from her foal for fear of hurting it.

- **Collection:** Bukhari

### H49
