# Sahabi of the Month: set B

Six Companions, 31 daily facts each, ordered as a story: lineage and early life, Islam, deeds and character, worship, death.

Sources:
- **Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah**: *The Lives of the Sahabah*, Mufti Afzal Hoosen Elias's English translation, 3 vols. Printed page = PDF page + 2 in all three volumes, except early vol. 2 (up to about PDF p. 126), where it is + 1.
- **al-Iṣābah**: Ibn Ḥajar, *al-Iṣābah fī Tamyīz al-Ṣaḥābah* (Arabic scan, 8 vols). Printed page = PDF page − 1 in the volumes used here.

## ʿĀʾishah bint Abī Bakr
- **Title:** Mother of the Believers, scholar of the Sunnah

### B-AISHAH-01
- **Text:** ʿĀʾishah was the daughter of Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq. Her mother was Umm Rūmān bint ʿĀmir al-Kināniyyah.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 231 (pdf p. 232)

### B-AISHAH-02
- **Text:** She was born about four or five years after the Prophet ﷺ was first sent with the message.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 231 (pdf p. 232)

### B-AISHAH-03
- **Text:** She was given the kunya Umm ʿAbdillāh, after her sister's son, ʿAbdullāh ibn al-Zubayr.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 232 (pdf p. 233)

### B-AISHAH-04
- **Text:** When the proposal came, Abū Bakr wondered if it was allowed, since he and the Prophet ﷺ called each other brother. The reply came: "He is my brother in Islam," so the marriage could take place.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 664 (pdf p. 662)

### B-AISHAH-05
- **Text:** No camel or goat was slaughtered for her wedding. The only meal was a platter of food that Saʿd ibn ʿUbādah used to send to the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 665 (pdf p. 663)

### B-AISHAH-06
- **Text:** On the Hijrah to Madinah her camel bolted with her and her mother on it. It was caught far down the valley, and she said, "Allah kept us safe."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 370 (pdf p. 368)

### B-AISHAH-07
- **Text:** When the Prophet ﷺ was asked whom he loved most, he said, "ʿĀʾishah." Asked about the men, he said, "Her father."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 536 (pdf p. 534)

### B-AISHAH-08
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ once told her, "Jibrīl is here and sends you salām." She returned the greeting of peace to the angel.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 506–507 (pdf pp. 504–505)

### B-AISHAH-09
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that revelation never came to him while he was under the blanket of any of his wives except ʿĀʾishah.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 233 (pdf p. 234)

### B-AISHAH-10
- **Text:** A poor woman once brought her a gift and she refused it out of pity. The Prophet ﷺ told her to accept it and give something back: "Be humble, ʿĀʾishah, for Allah loves the humble."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 283 (pdf p. 281)

### B-AISHAH-11
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ advised her: if you want to be with me in the Ākhirah, be content in this world with a traveller's provisions, and do not replace a garment until you have patched it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 322 (pdf p. 320)

### B-AISHAH-12
- **Text:** She took that advice to heart. She would not take new clothes until she had patched her old ones so often that the patches lay on top of each other.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 322 (pdf p. 320)

### B-AISHAH-13
- **Text:** She was fasting with only one piece of bread at home when a beggar came. She had it given to him. That evening someone sent her meat and bread, and she said, "This is better than that piece of bread."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 206 (pdf p. 204)
- **Note:** The book cites the Muwaṭṭaʾ.

### B-AISHAH-14
- **Text:** She once gave a beggar a single grape. When someone looked surprised, she asked how many atoms' weight of good were in it, recalling the verse on an atom's weight of good.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 206 (pdf p. 204)

### B-AISHAH-15
- **Text:** A hundred thousand was brought to her while she was fasting, and she gave it all away. Asked why she kept nothing to buy meat for iftār, she said, "Had you reminded me, I would have."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 262 (pdf p. 260)

### B-AISHAH-16
- **Text:** ʿAbdullāh ibn al-Zubayr said he never saw women more generous than ʿĀʾishah and her sister Asmāʾ. ʿĀʾishah would gather things up and then give them all away together.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 184 (pdf p. 182)

### B-AISHAH-17
- **Text:** She warned that overeating was the first trouble to reach the Ummah after the Prophet ﷺ: full bellies make bodies heavy, hearts weak and desires strong.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 318 (pdf p. 316)

### B-AISHAH-18
- **Text:** Asked about the Prophet's ﷺ character, she asked, "Do you read the Qurʾān?" When the man said yes, she said, "His character was the Qurʾān."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 185 (pdf p. 183)

### B-AISHAH-19
- **Text:** Abū Mūsā al-Ashʿarī said that whenever the Companions were unsure about something, they took it to ʿĀʾishah and found that she knew the answer.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 307 (pdf p. 305)

### B-AISHAH-20
- **Text:** She gave fatwas in the time of ʿUmar and ʿUthmān until she passed away, and both of them sent people to ask her about the Sunnah.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, pp. 307–308 (pdf pp. 305–306)

### B-AISHAH-21
- **Text:** Masrūq, a great student of hers, would begin her hadith with: "The truthful one, daughter of the truthful one, the beloved of Allah's beloved, told me."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 233 (pdf p. 234)

### B-AISHAH-22
- **Text:** She also knew medicine. She said she learnt it while nursing the Prophet ﷺ in his last illness, when doctors sent him remedies and she gave them to him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 308 (pdf p. 306)

### B-AISHAH-23
- **Text:** She told the preacher of Madinah to avoid rhyming du'ās, to preach once a week or three times at most so people do not tire of the Qurʾān, and not to interrupt people's talk.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 252 (pdf p. 250)

### B-AISHAH-24
- **Text:** She asked the Prophet ﷺ for a du'ā. He prayed that Allah forgive all her sins, past and future, open and secret. Then he said, "This is my du'ā for my Ummah in every salāh."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 379 (pdf p. 377)

### B-AISHAH-25
- **Text:** While she was in salāh, the Prophet ﷺ told her to make du'ās that cover everything. He taught her to ask for all good, near and far, known and unknown, and for Jannah and every deed that leads to it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, pp. 395–396 (pdf pp. 393–394)

### B-AISHAH-26
- **Text:** Wanting to know the Name by which du'ā is answered, she made wuḍūʾ, prayed two rakʿahs and called on Allah by all His beautiful names. The Prophet ﷺ smiled: "It is among the names you said."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, pp. 376–377 (pdf pp. 374–375)

### B-AISHAH-27
- **Text:** She once wept at the thought of Jahannam. The Prophet ﷺ told her of three places where no one will think of anyone else: the Scales, the giving of the books of deeds, and the Bridge.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 97 (pdf p. 95)

### B-AISHAH-28
- **Text:** Despite her rank, she feared Allah greatly. She would say, "I wish I were a tree. I wish I were a clod of earth. I wish Allah had never created me."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 632 (pdf p. 630)

### B-AISHAH-29
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ passed away in her house, during her turn, resting between her chest and throat, and he was buried in her home.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 234 (pdf p. 235)

### B-AISHAH-30
- **Text:** Near her death Ibn ʿAbbās came and praised her. Afterwards she said that she did not want anyone praising her that day. She wished she had been completely forgotten.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 632–633 (pdf pp. 630–631)

### B-AISHAH-31
- **Text:** She passed away in Ramaḍān of the year 58 AH and was buried in al-Baqīʿ.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 235 (pdf p. 236)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah gives 58 AH (the night of 17 Ramaḍān) as the view of most, and also mentions 57 AH.

## Fāṭimah bint Muḥammad
- **Title:** Daughter of the Prophet ﷺ, leader of the women of Jannah

### B-FATIMAH-01
- **Text:** Fāṭimah was the daughter of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ. She was known as al-Zahrāʾ, and her kunya was Umm Abīhā, "her father's mother".
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 262 (pdf p. 263)

### B-FATIMAH-02
- **Text:** Al-ʿAbbās said she was born while the Quraysh were rebuilding the Kaʿbah, when the Prophet ﷺ was thirty-five years old.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 263 (pdf p. 264)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah notes that reports differ on her year of birth. This is the report it quotes from al-ʿAbbās.

### B-FATIMAH-03
- **Text:** Several narrators said she was the youngest of the Prophet's ﷺ daughters and the one he loved most.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 263 (pdf p. 264)

### B-FATIMAH-04
- **Text:** When the Prophet ﷺ was told to warn his family, he called out, "O Fāṭimah, daughter of Muḥammad, ask me for anything of my wealth, but I cannot help you at all before Allah."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, pp. 127–128 (pdf pp. 125–126)

### B-FATIMAH-05
- **Text:** She made the Hijrah to Madinah with her sister Umm Kulthūm and with Sawdah, the Prophet's ﷺ wife, escorted by Zayd ibn Ḥārithah and Abū Rāfiʿ.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 370 (pdf p. 368)

### B-FATIMAH-06
- **Text:** When ʿAlī came to ask for her hand, he was too shy to speak. The Prophet ﷺ asked if he had come about Fāṭimah, and her dowry was ʿAlī's suit of armour.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 676–677 (pdf pp. 674–675)

### B-FATIMAH-07
- **Text:** Her new home had only a straw mat on the floor, a pillow stuffed with palm bark, a clay jug and a clay cup.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 678 (pdf p. 676)

### B-FATIMAH-08
- **Text:** On her wedding night she came forward shy and trembling in her shawl. The Prophet ﷺ prayed over her and said, "I have married you to the member of my family I love most."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 678–679 (pdf pp. 676–677)

### B-FATIMAH-09
- **Text:** Jābir said he had never seen a better wedding than hers. The guests sat on a mat stuffed with palm bark and were served raisins and dates.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 679 (pdf p. 677)

### B-FATIMAH-10
- **Text:** ʿAlī said that in their early marriage their only bedding was a sheepskin. They slept on it at night and fed their camel on it by day, and they had no servant.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 334 (pdf p. 332)

### B-FATIMAH-11
- **Text:** One morning there was nothing at all to eat in her home. ʿAlī took their sons out so they would not cry to her while she had nothing to give them.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 321 (pdf p. 319)

### B-FATIMAH-12
- **Text:** She did her own housework. When she kneaded dough, she bent so low over the bowl that her plaits touched it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 578 (pdf p. 576)

### B-FATIMAH-13
- **Text:** She baked a loaf and could not bring herself to eat it until she had brought a piece to her father. He told her it was the first food he had eaten in three days.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 317 (pdf p. 315)

### B-FATIMAH-14
- **Text:** Though her own family was in need, she saved a gift of bread and meat for her father. When she uncovered it, the dish was full, and she said, "It is from Allah, who provides for whom He wills."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 624 (pdf p. 622)

### B-FATIMAH-15
- **Text:** When the Prophet ﷺ came home from a journey, he would pray two rakʿahs in the masjid and then visit her first. Once, seeing his pale face and worn clothes, she wept.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 82 (pdf p. 80)

### B-FATIMAH-16
- **Text:** ʿĀʾishah said no one was more like the Prophet ﷺ in speech and manner than Fāṭimah. Whenever either came to the other, the other would stand, welcome and kiss them.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 514 (pdf p. 512)

### B-FATIMAH-17
- **Text:** ʿĀʾishah also said, "I have never seen anyone better than Fāṭimah, except her father."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 264 (pdf p. 265)

### B-FATIMAH-18
- **Text:** ʿUmar told her, "I have never seen anyone the Prophet ﷺ loved more than you. After your father, no one is dearer to me than you."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 344 (pdf p. 342)

### B-FATIMAH-19
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said, "Fāṭimah is a part of me. Whatever hurts her hurts me."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 265 (pdf p. 266)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites both Ṣaḥīḥs.

### B-FATIMAH-20
- **Text:** Abū Sufyān once asked her to plead his case, offering her leadership of the women of her tribe. She replied, "The matter is not mine. It rests with Allah and His Messenger."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 374 (pdf p. 372)

### B-FATIMAH-21
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ spread a cloak over ʿAlī, Fāṭimah, al-Ḥasan and al-Ḥusayn and prayed, "O Allah, be pleased with them as I am pleased with them."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 382 (pdf p. 380)

### B-FATIMAH-22
- **Text:** Hungry, she went to her father for food. He offered her five goats or five words that Jibrīl had taught him. She chose the words, and said she came back with something for the Ākhirah.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 79 (pdf p. 77)

### B-FATIMAH-23
- **Text:** Her hands were calloused from grinding grain, so she went to ask her father for a servant. When she got there she felt too shy to ask, and only greeted him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, pp. 348–349 (pdf pp. 346–347)

### B-FATIMAH-24
- **Text:** When she and ʿAlī asked together, the Prophet ﷺ said he could not give them a servant while the people of the Ṣuffah went hungry. He would sell the captives to feed them.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 349 (pdf p. 347)

### B-FATIMAH-25
- **Text:** Instead he taught them something better than a servant: at bedtime, say SubḥānAllāh 33 times, Alḥamdulillāh 33 times and Allāhu Akbar 34 times. ʿAlī said he never once left it.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 349 (pdf p. 347)

### B-FATIMAH-26
- **Text:** Once her face was pale with hunger. The Prophet ﷺ put his hand on her and prayed that Allah not let her go hungry. The colour came back, and she said she never felt hunger again.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 647 (pdf p. 645)

### B-FATIMAH-27
- **Text:** In his last illness the Prophet ﷺ told her quietly that he would soon die, and she wept. Then he told her she would be the first of his family to join him, and she laughed.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 354 (pdf p. 352)

### B-FATIMAH-28
- **Text:** As he neared death she wept. He told her to say "Innā lillāhi wa innā ilayhi rājiʿūn", for it brings reward for every loss. She asked, "Even for losing you?" He said, "Even for losing me."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 354 (pdf p. 352)

### B-FATIMAH-29
- **Text:** She kept what he had whispered to herself, saying she would not give away his secret. Only after he passed away did she tell what he had said.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 514–515 (pdf pp. 512–513)

### B-FATIMAH-30
- **Text:** After the Prophet ﷺ passed away, no one saw her laugh again. At most, the corner of her mouth would lift in a slight smile.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 364 (pdf p. 362)

### B-FATIMAH-31
- **Text:** She passed away about six months after her father ﷺ, as ʿĀʾishah reported.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 8, p. 266 (pdf p. 267)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah quotes this from the Ṣaḥīḥ and lists other views (three months, eight months and others).

## Muʿādh ibn Jabal
- **Title:** The young scholar of the Anṣār, most learned in what is lawful and unlawful

### B-MUADH-01
- **Text:** Muʿādh ibn Jabal ibn ʿAmr was one of the Anṣār, from the tribe of Khazraj. His kunya was Abū ʿAbd al-Raḥmān.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 6, p. 107 (pdf p. 108)

### B-MUADH-02
- **Text:** He was a young, handsome and very generous man, counted among the most righteous of his tribe.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 184 (pdf p. 182)

### B-MUADH-03
- **Text:** He gave his pledge to the Prophet ﷺ at ʿAqabah. Back in Madinah he and other young Muslims kept throwing a chief's idol into a pit, until the chief saw it was helpless and accepted Islam.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 248 (pdf p. 246)
- **Note:** The chief was ʿAmr ibn al-Jamūḥ. His Islam is told on the following pages of the same chapter.

### B-MUADH-04
- **Text:** He fought at Badr when he was twenty-one, and he took part in every battle after it.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 6, pp. 107–108 (pdf pp. 108–109)

### B-MUADH-05
- **Text:** He was one of the few Anṣār who had memorised the whole Qurʾān in the lifetime of the Prophet ﷺ.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 239 (pdf p. 237)

### B-MUADH-06
- **Text:** When the Prophet ﷺ left for Ḥunayn, he put Muʿādh in charge of the people of Makkah to teach them the Qurʾān and help them understand the religion.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 235 (pdf p. 233)

### B-MUADH-07
- **Text:** Asked how he could show that his faith was true, he said, "Each morning I think I will not see the evening, and each evening I think I will not see the morning." The Prophet ﷺ told him, "Hold firm to it."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, pp. 59–60 (pdf pp. 57–58)

### B-MUADH-08
- **Text:** He gave to anyone who asked until he was deep in debt and lost everything. Even then he would not ask the Prophet ﷺ for anything.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 185–186 (pdf pp. 183–184)

### B-MUADH-09
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ once told him, "I love you," and taught him something to say after every salāh.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 6, p. 108 (pdf p. 109)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites Abū Dāwūd and shortens the hadith, so the words of the dhikr are not given on this page.

### B-MUADH-10
- **Text:** When the Prophet ﷺ sent him to Yemen, he wrote to its people: "I have sent you the best of my people."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 6, p. 109 (pdf p. 110)

### B-MUADH-11
- **Text:** As Muʿādh rode out to Yemen, the Prophet ﷺ walked beside him giving advice. He said they might not meet again after that year, and Muʿādh wept at the thought of parting.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 355 (pdf p. 353)

### B-MUADH-12
- **Text:** Asked how he would judge in Yemen, he said: by Allah's Book, then the Sunnah, and if neither had the answer, by his own careful reasoning. The Prophet ﷺ praised Allah for that answer.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 294 (pdf p. 292)

### B-MUADH-13
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that of all his Companions, Muʿādh knew best what is lawful and what is unlawful.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 6, p. 109 (pdf p. 110)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites Tirmidhi and others.

### B-MUADH-14
- **Text:** Ibn Masʿūd said Muʿādh was a man who taught people every kind of good and who obeyed Allah and His Messenger ﷺ.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 303 (pdf p. 301)

### B-MUADH-15
- **Text:** Some slaves given to him as gifts prayed behind him. He asked whom they prayed for. "For Allah," they said. "Then you all belong to Allah," he said, and set every one of them free.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 187 (pdf p. 185)

### B-MUADH-16
- **Text:** ʿUmar sent him 400 dinars to test him. Muʿādh prayed for ʿUmar and gave it all to needy families. When his wife said they were poor too, only two dinars were left for her.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 260 (pdf p. 258)

### B-MUADH-17
- **Text:** He was so careful to be fair to his two wives that on one wife's day he would not even make wuḍūʾ in the other's house.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 624 (pdf p. 622)

### B-MUADH-18
- **Text:** In the masjid of Ḥimṣ he sat silent among about thirty older Companions. When they disagreed about something, they turned to the young Muʿādh and asked him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 648 (pdf p. 646)

### B-MUADH-19
- **Text:** ʿUmar once found him weeping over a hadith: the smallest showing-off is shirk, and Allah's most beloved are the hidden God-fearing, not missed when absent nor noticed when present.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 636 (pdf p. 634)

### B-MUADH-20
- **Text:** He asked the Prophet ﷺ which deed to hold on to. Pointing to his mouth, the Prophet ﷺ said: keep silent unless you speak good, for the tongue is what throws people into the Fire.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 640–641 (pdf pp. 638–639)

### B-MUADH-21

### B-MUSAB-01

### B-MUSAB-13

### B-MUSAB-16

## Salmān al-Fārisī
- **Title:** The seeker of truth from Persia, learned and content with little

### B-SALMAN-01
- **Text:** Salmān al-Fārisī's kunya was Abū ʿAbdillāh. He was also called "Salmān ibn al-Islām", the son of Islam, and "Salmān al-Khayr", Salmān the good.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 118 (pdf p. 119)

### B-SALMAN-02
- **Text:** His family came from Rāmhurmuz, or by another report from Iṣfahān.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 119 (pdf p. 120)

### B-SALMAN-03
- **Text:** He had heard that a Prophet ﷺ was soon to be sent, so he left home to search for him. On the way he was captured and sold as a slave in Madinah.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 119 (pdf p. 120)

### B-SALMAN-04
- **Text:** Bukhari reports from Salmān that he was passed from one master to another, more than ten of them in all.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 119 (pdf p. 120)

### B-SALMAN-05
- **Text:** When he still owed part of the price of his freedom, the Prophet ﷺ gave him a piece of gold the size of a hen's egg. It weighed forty ūqiyah, enough to pay the whole debt and set him free.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 641 (pdf p. 639)

### B-SALMAN-06
- **Text:** Slavery kept him from the early battles. His first was the Battle of the Trench (al-Khandaq). After that he took part in every battle and in the conquest of Iraq.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 119 (pdf p. 120)

### B-SALMAN-07
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ made Salmān and Abū al-Dardāʾ brothers in faith.

### B-SALMAN-08
- **Text:** Visiting Abū al-Dardāʾ, he learnt that his brother had turned away from worldly needs. When Abū al-Dardāʾ said he was fasting, Salmān would not eat until he ate too. That night he told him to sleep, and woke him to pray only near the end of the night.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 705 (pdf p. 703)

### B-SALMAN-09
- **Text:** Then he told him: your Lord has a right over you, your body has a right, and your wife has a right, so give each its due. When this reached the Prophet ﷺ, he said, "Salmān is right."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 705 (pdf p. 703)

### B-SALMAN-10
- **Text:** Abū al-Dardāʾ once went to propose marriage for Salmān, but the family gave the girl to Abū al-Dardāʾ instead. Salmān said, "I should be the one embarrassed, for I asked for a woman Allah had written for you."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 684 (pdf p. 682)

### B-SALMAN-11
- **Text:** He was in a gathering of dhikr when the Prophet ﷺ passed by. He told them to carry on, for he had seen mercy coming down on them and wished to join them.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 324 (pdf p. 322)

### B-SALMAN-12
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that Allah loves four of his Companions, and named Salmān among them.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 119 (pdf p. 120)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah quotes this from Abū al-Shaykh, via Buraydah.

### B-SALMAN-13
- **Text:** ʿAlī said Salmān had gained the knowledge of the earlier prophets and of the final Prophet ﷺ. He called him an ocean whose bed cannot be reached, and said, "He is one of us, the Prophet's family."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 302 (pdf p. 300)

### B-SALMAN-14
- **Text:** Ibn ʿAbd al-Barr described him as a learned man who turned away from the world. Companions such as Anas, Ibn ʿAbbās and Abū Saʿīd narrated hadith from him.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, p. 119 (pdf p. 120)

### B-SALMAN-15
- **Text:** He narrated the Prophet's ﷺ sermon on the last day of Shaʿbān: a great, blessed month is coming, with a night better than a thousand months. Whoever gives a fasting person something to break the fast gets the same reward.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 412 (pdf p. 410)

### B-SALMAN-16
- **Text:** Under a tree, he shook a dry branch until its leaves fell, as the Prophet ﷺ once did for him. Then he explained: when a Muslim makes wuḍūʾ well and prays the five salāhs, his sins fall away like these leaves.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 126 (pdf p. 124)

### B-SALMAN-17
- **Text:** A man stayed the night to watch his worship and saw less than he expected. Salmān told him to guard the five salāhs, to use the night for prayer rather than sin, and to keep to moderation and constancy rather than haste.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 188 (pdf p. 186)

### B-SALMAN-18
- **Text:** Visiting a sick man, he gave him good news: Allah makes a believer's illness a means of forgiveness and of His pleasure.

### B-SALMAN-19
- **Text:** Abū Bakr advised him: many lands will be opened, so take from them only what you need to eat and to cover yourself. Whoever prays the five salāhs is under Allah's protection morning and evening.

### B-SALMAN-22

- **Text:** Visitors found him, the commander of twenty thousand men, sitting weaving baskets. When told the guest loved him, he prayed, "May Allah love him."

### B-SALMAN-24
- **Text:** A traveller, not knowing he was the governor, told him to carry a load of figs. He carried it, and when the man found out and apologised, Salmān kept going, saying he had already made his intention for Allah.

### B-SALMAN-25
- **Text:** With money from his own handiwork he would buy meat or fish, then invite people suffering from leprosy to share the meal with him.

### B-SALMAN-26

### B-SALMAN-27

### B-SALMAN-28

### B-SALMAN-29
- **Text:** Three things made him weep: parting from the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions, the agony of death, and standing before Allah not knowing whether he was bound for Jannah or the Fire.

### B-SALMAN-30
- **Text:** On his deathbed he wept, because the Prophet ﷺ had told them to keep only a traveller's provisions. Yet all he owned was a water jug, a washing bowl and a few other things, worth less than twenty dirhams.

### B-SALMAN-31
- **Text:** At the end he had musk sprinkled around his bed, saying visitors were coming who enjoy fragrance but do not eat: the angels. His wife left him, and when she came back he had passed away, as if asleep.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 69 (pdf p. 67)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah, vol. 3, pp. 119–120 (pdf pp. 120–121) gives his death as 36 AH (Abū ʿUbayd) or 37 AH (Khalīfah), and also discusses an earlier date, so we left the year out.

### B-ABUDHARR-09
- **Text:** He said he was the first person to greet the Prophet ﷺ with the greeting of Islam, "Peace be on you, O Messenger of Allah." The Prophet ﷺ returned his salām, and Abū Bakr took him home as his guest.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 306 (pdf p. 304)

### B-ABUDHARR-10
- **Text:** When he told his brother he had become Muslim, his brother followed him, and so did their mother. Then he called his own people, and some of them accepted Islam too.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 306 (pdf p. 304)

### B-ABUDHARR-11
- **Text:** He stayed among his people until after Badr and Uḥud, and then came to Madinah. He was tall and lean.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 7, p. 107 (pdf p. 108)

### B-ABUDHARR-12
- **Text:** In Madinah he served the Prophet ﷺ. Having no home, he slept on the floor of the Masjid when his work was done.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 150 (pdf p. 148)

### B-ABUDHARR-13
- **Text:** Abū al-Dardāʾ said that the Prophet ﷺ would greet Abū Dharr first when he was there, and ask after him when he was away.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 7, p. 107 (pdf p. 108)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites al-Ṭabarānī.

### B-ABUDHARR-14
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ said that the sky has never shaded, and the earth has never carried, anyone more truthful in speech than Abū Dharr.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 7, p. 108 (pdf p. 109)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah cites Abū Dāwūd and Aḥmad.

### B-ABUDHARR-15
- **Text:** He pledged to the Prophet ﷺ never to ask anyone for anything, not even to pick up his whip if it fell from his hand while riding.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 1, p. 258 (pdf p. 256)

### B-ABUDHARR-16
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ told him: when you commit a sin, follow it with a good deed to wipe it out. He asked if "Lā ilāha illallāh" was a good deed. "It is the best of good deeds," came the reply.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 336 (pdf p. 334)

### B-ABUDHARR-17
- **Text:** He asked about a man who loves people but cannot do deeds like theirs. "You, Abū Dharr, will be with those you love," said the Prophet ﷺ. "I love Allah and His Messenger," he said, and heard the same reply.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 338 (pdf p. 336)

### B-ABUDHARR-18
- **Text:** The Prophet ﷺ told him that the words Allah loves most are "SubḥānAllāhi wa biḥamdihi".
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 339 (pdf p. 337)

### B-ABUDHARR-19
- **Text:** He once said the wealthy were taking all the reward. The Prophet ﷺ taught him to say Allāhu Akbar, Alḥamdulillāh and SubḥānAllāh thirty-three times each after every salāh.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 347 (pdf p. 345)
- **Note:** The book cites Abū Dāwūd. It also gives a closing phrase in Arabic, which we leave out because the text is not cleanly printed.

### B-ABUDHARR-20
- **Text:** When he asked for advice, the Prophet ﷺ told him to hold to taqwā, the root of every affair, and to keep reciting the Qurʾān and remembering Allah: a light in this world and a treasure in the Ākhirah.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 505 (pdf p. 503)

### B-ABUDHARR-21
- **Text:** He also told him to love the poor and keep their company, and to look at those below him, not above, so that he would not belittle Allah's favours on him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 505 (pdf p. 503)

### B-ABUDHARR-22
- **Text:** On the march to Tabūk his camel was too slow, so he put his things on his back and walked after the army. Seeing a lone figure, the Prophet ﷺ said, "Let it be Abū Dharr," and it was. He said, "May Allah have mercy on Abū Dharr. He walks alone, will die alone, and will be raised alone."
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 7, p. 109 (pdf p. 110)

### B-ABUDHARR-23
- **Text:** Abū Dāwūd said that Abū Dharr was the equal of Ibn Masʿūd in knowledge.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 7, p. 109 (pdf p. 110)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah quotes this through al-Ājurrī.

### B-ABUDHARR-24
- **Text:** He said the Prophet ﷺ left them so much knowledge that even a bird flapping its wings in the sky reminded them of something he had taught.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, p. 299 (pdf p. 297)

### B-ABUDHARR-25
- **Text:** He dressed his servant in exactly the same kind of shawl he wore himself, following the Prophet's ﷺ teaching: servants are your brothers, so feed them what you eat and clothe them as you dress.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, pp. 377–378 (pdf pp. 375–376)

### B-ABUDHARR-26
- **Text:** Offered money for his needs, he declined. He had goats to milk, donkeys to ride, a freed slave to help him and more clothing than he needed, and he feared being asked about anything beyond his needs.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 279 (pdf p. 277)

### B-ABUDHARR-27
- **Text:** At the Kaʿbah he told people: you take provisions for a journey, and the journey to Qiyāmah is the longest of all. Make Hajj, fast on a hot day, pray two rakʿahs in the dark of night for the loneliness of the grave, and give in charity.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, pp. 524–525 (pdf pp. 522–523)

### B-ABUDHARR-28
- **Text:** When someone remarked that none of his children lived long, he said, "Praise be to Allah, who takes them from this passing home and keeps them as a treasure for me in the everlasting one."
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 603 (pdf p. 601)

### B-ABUDHARR-29
- **Text:** After he passed away, a man came from Basrah to ask his wife about his worship. She said he would spend the whole day alone in reflection.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 2, p. 639 (pdf p. 637)

### B-ABUDHARR-30
- **Text:** As he lay dying in the open country, his wife wept that she had no cloth to shroud him. He told her the Prophet ﷺ had said one of his group would die in the wilderness with believers present. She watched the road, and travellers came and shrouded him.
- **Source:** Ḥayāt al-Ṣaḥābah, vol. 3, pp. 106–107 (pdf pp. 104–105)

### B-ABUDHARR-31
- **Text:** He passed away at al-Rabadhah in 31 or 32 AH. It is reported that Ibn Masʿūd led his funeral prayer.
- **Source:** al-Iṣābah, vol. 7, p. 109 (pdf p. 110)
- **Note:** Al-Iṣābah gives 31 AH and says most hold the following year. It gives the funeral prayer by Ibn Masʿūd as "it is said", and al-Madāʾinī reports the same.
