# Adhkār after Salāh

**Source:** the user's poster «أذكار بعد الصلاة» / "Supplications after Prayers" (screenshot `Screenshot 2026-11-10 155825.png`, 10 Nov 2026). Working crops: `design-review/manzil-work/poster/`.
**Added:** the full Āyat al-Kursī (2:255), approved by the user, placed after the tasbīh (#7) and before the Quls.
**Status:** draft, pending scholar review. Data: `src/tools/data/after-salah.js`; `tests/new-tools.test.js` checks that every Arabic string there appears verbatim in this file.

Conventions:
- The Arabic is typed fresh with full harakat, in the same IndoPak-leaning spelling as `docs/adhkar-20.md` (اللّٰه, إِلٰهَ, عَلٰى, هٗ, sukun on long vowels). It follows the poster's wording; the poster's own vowel marks are too blurred to copy mark by mark.
- Transliteration is simple. The English is our own; the poster's English is not copied.
- References: the poster prints them in Urdu, very small. The app shows the collection, and the number only where it is legible. Our reading of each is given, with "?" where unsure. No gradings.
- Qurʾānic items are rendered in the app from the Quran data (IndoPak text, Pickthall) by reference; no Arabic is stored for them.
- **The Quls:** The three Quls: 3 times after Fajr and Maghrib, once after Zuhr, ʿAsr and ʿIshāʾ. The reader picks the prayer that just passed (the latest fard whose time has begun; before Fajr it is last night's ʿIshāʾ), and the user can switch it.

## 5. None can withhold what You give (poster #5)

**Count:** 1

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَحْدَهٗ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهٗ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ اَللّٰهُمَّ لَا مَانِعَ لِمَا أَعْطَيْتَ وَلَا مُعْطِيَ لِمَا مَنَعْتَ وَلَا يَنْفَعُ ذَا الْجَدِّ مِنْكَ الْجَدُّ</p>

**Transliteration:** La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa ʿala kulli shayʾin qadir. Allahumma la maniʿa lima aʿtayta wa la muʿtiya lima manaʿta wa la yanfaʿu dhal-jaddi minkal-jadd.

**Translation:** There is no god but Allah, alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. O Allah, no one can hold back what You give, and no one can give what You hold back, and a man's wealth and standing are of no use to him against You.

**Reference (shown in app):** Bukhari

**Poster's reference, as we read it:** صحیح بخاری 6330? (or 844)

## 6. The long tahlīl (poster #6)

**Count:** 1

<p dir="rtl" lang="ar">لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَحْدَهٗ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهٗ لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَلَا نَعْبُدُ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ لَهُ النِّعْمَةُ وَلَهُ الْفَضْلُ وَلَهُ الثَّنَآءُ الْحَسَنُ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ مُخْلِصِيْنَ لَهُ الدِّيْنَ وَلَوْ كَرِهَ الْكَافِرُوْنَ</p>

**Transliteration:** La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, wa huwa ʿala kulli shayʾin qadir. La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah. La ilaha illallahu wa la naʿbudu illa iyyah, lahun-niʿmatu wa lahul-fadlu wa lahuth-thanaʾul-hasan. La ilaha illallahu mukhlisina lahud-dina wa law karihal-kafirun.

**Translation:** There is no god but Allah, alone, with no partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. There is no might and no power except through Allah. There is no god but Allah, and we worship none but Him. All blessings are His, all bounty is His, and the finest praise is His. There is no god but Allah; we keep our religion for Him alone, even if the disbelievers hate it.

**Reference (shown in app):** Muslim 594

**Poster's reference, as we read it:** صحیح مسلم 594

## Uncertain readings (for the scholar)

The poster photo is low resolution (792×1389). The Arabic wording of all eight poster items is clear; the fine points below could not be confirmed from the print and follow the standard wording of these adhkār:

1. **#3** «… تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ»: legible on the poster, including «يَا ذَا». Some printings add «يَا»-less forms; we follow the poster.
2. **#5** «وَلَا يَنْفَعُ ذَا الْجَدِّ مِنْكَ الْجَدُّ»: the last word's vowel is not legible; standard «الْجَدُّ» used.
3. **#6** «وَلَهُ الْفَضْلُ وَلَهُ الثَّنَآءُ الْحَسَنُ»: legible; «الثَّنَآءُ» written with madd as in IndoPak style.
4. **#7** The poster prints the three tasbīh words and then «لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَحْدَهٗ … قَدِيْرٌ» "to make the count 100, say this one time". The order Subhānallāh → Alhamdulillāh → Allāhu akbar is the poster's right-to-left order.
5. **#8 on the poster (Quls)**: the poster gives the three surahs by their first words only and no count. The 3×/1× rule comes from the user's instruction (Fajr and Maghrib 3×, others 1×).
6. **References**: only #4 (Abu Dawud 1522, Nasaʾi 1304) and #6 (Muslim 594) were legible enough to show a number. The others show the collection only; our guesses at the numbers are in "Poster's reference, as we read it".
7. **Translations**: own drafts. #5's «الْجَدّ» is rendered "wealth and standing" (the word covers fortune, rank and riches).
