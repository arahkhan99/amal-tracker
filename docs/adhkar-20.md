# Morning and Evening Adhkar: 20-item card

**Source card:** النور والضياء لأذكار الصبح والمساء, subtitled «صبح و شام کی دعاؤں کا بہترین مجموعہ»
**Publisher:** علمی کتاب گھر (Ilmi Kitab Ghar), Urdu Bazar, Karachi 74200
**Photos:** four WhatsApp photos dated 2026-11-07 (P1 = `5.28.57 PM.jpeg`, P2 = `5.28.57 PM (1).jpeg`, P3 = `5.28.57 PM (2).jpeg`, P4 = `5.28.58 PM.jpeg`)
**Status:** transcription draft for scholar review. **This list replaces the old 13-item list in `docs/tools-content.md`. That file has not been edited.**

Conventions:
- The Arabic follows the card's wording and harakat. Where the card uses IndoPak spelling (khari zabar ٰ, small ٖ after هٗ/هٖ, sukun on long vowels), it is kept.
- For morning/evening alternatives, the morning wording comes first and the evening word follows in [brackets].
- "Virtue" is our English rendering of the card's Urdu فضائل line. The Urdu is quoted first.
- References are copied exactly as the card prints them. No gradings are given.
- Quranic passages are marked *translation pending (licensed translation)*.

## 1. Istighfar (3 times)

**Count:** 3 (۳ مرتبہ)

> أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللّٰهَ الَّذِيْ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ الْحَيُّ الْقَيُّوْمُ وَأَتُوْبُ إِلَيْهِ

**Transliteration:** Astaghfirullahal-ladhi la ilaha illa huwal-hayyul-qayyumu wa atubu ilayh.

**Translation:** I seek the forgiveness of Allah, besides whom there is no god, the Ever-Living, the Sustainer of all, and I turn to Him in repentance.

**Virtue (card):** تمام گناہوں کا کفارہ اگرچہ سمندر کے جھاگ کے برابر ہوں
All sins are expiated, even if they are as much as the foam of the sea.

**Reference (card):** مجمع الزوائد

## 2. Tasbih with la hawla (3 times)

**Count:** 3

> سُبْحَانَ اللّٰهِ الْعَظِيْمِ وَبِحَمْدِهٖ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ

**Transliteration:** Subhanallahil-'azimi wa bihamdihi, wa la hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

**Translation:** Glory be to Allah the Magnificent, and praise be to Him. There is no power and no strength except through Allah.

**Virtue (card):** چار بیماریوں سے موت تک حفاظت، جذام، جنون، اندھا ہونا، فالج
Protection until death from four illnesses: leprosy, insanity, blindness and paralysis.

**Reference (card):** مجمع الزوائد

## 3. Protection from the Fire (7 times)

**Count:** 7

> اَللّٰهُمَّ أَجِرْنِيْ مِنَ النَّارِ

**Transliteration:** Allahumma ajirni minan-nar.

**Translation:** O Allah, protect me from the Fire.

**Virtue (card):** جہنم سے حفاظت
Protection from Hell.

**Reference (card):** ابوداود

## 4. Tahlil (10 times)

**Count:** 10

> لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا اللّٰهُ وَحْدَهٗ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَهٗ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، يُحْيِيْ وَيُمِيْتُ، وَهُوَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ

**Transliteration:** La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd, yuhyi wa yumit, wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir.

**Translation:** There is no god but Allah, alone, with no partner. To Him belongs the dominion and to Him belongs all praise. He gives life and causes death, and He has power over all things.

**Virtue (card):** دس نیکیاں، دس گناہوں کا مٹنا، دس درجات بلند ہونا، دس غلام آزاد کرنے کا ثواب ملنا، شیطان اور ہر مکروہ چیز سے حفاظت
Ten good deeds are recorded, ten sins are erased and one is raised ten ranks. One gets the reward of freeing ten slaves, and is protected from Shaytan and from everything disliked.

**Reference (card):** ترمذی

## 5. Bismillah of protection (3 times)

**Count:** 3

> بِسْمِ اللّٰهِ الَّذِيْ لَا يَضُرُّ مَعَ اسْمِهٖ شَيْءٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا فِي السَّمَآءِ وَهُوَ السَّمِيْعُ الْعَلِيْمُ

**Transliteration:** Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru ma'asmihi shay'un fil-ardi wa la fis-sama'i, wa huwas-sami'ul-'alim.

**Translation:** In the name of Allah, with whose name nothing on earth or in the heavens can cause harm. He is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing.

**Virtue (card):** ناگہانی آفتوں سے حفاظت
Protection from sudden calamities.

**Reference (card):** ابوداود، ترمذی

## 6. Refuge in Allah's perfect words (3 times)

**Count:** 3

> أَعُوْذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللّٰهِ التَّآمَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ

**Transliteration:** A'udhu bi kalimatillahit-tammati min sharri ma khalaq.

**Translation:** I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created.

**Virtue (card):** ہر موذی چیز سے حفاظت

**Reference (card):** مسلم، ابوداود، ترمذی

## 7. Contentment with Allah, Islam and the Prophet (3 times)

**Count:** 3

> رَضِيْتُ بِاللّٰهِ رَبًّا وَّبِالْإِسْلَامِ دِيْنًا وَّبِمُحَمَّدٍ رَّسُوْلًا نَّبِيًّا

**Virtue (card):** روزِ قیامت اللہ تعالیٰ کا بندے کو راضی کرنا

## 8. Hasbiyallah (7 times)

**Count:** 7

> حَسْبِيَ اللّٰهُ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۚ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَهُوَ رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْعَظِيْمِ

**Transliteration:** Hasbiyallahu la ilaha illa huwa, 'alayhi tawakkaltu wa huwa rabbul-'arshil-'azim.

**Translation:** Allah is sufficient for me. There is no god but Him. In Him I have put my trust, and He is the Lord of the Mighty Throne.
*(This wording is also the second half of at-Tawbah 9:129. The card prints it as a dhikr, with Quranic pause marks. If it is shown as Quran, use the licensed translation.)*

**Virtue (card):** دنیا اور آخرت کے مسائل کے حل کیلئے اللہ کا کافی ہونا
Allah suffices for resolving one's concerns of this world and the Hereafter.

**Reference (card):** ابوداود، کنز العمال، جمع الجوامع

## 9. Sayyid al-Istighfar (once)

**Count:** 1

> اَللّٰهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّيْ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِيْ وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلٰى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوْءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ، وَأَبُوْءُ بِذَنْبِيْ، فَاغْفِرْ لِيْ، فَإِنَّهٗ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوْبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ

**Transliteration:** Allahumma anta rabbi, la ilaha illa ant. Khalaqtani wa ana 'abduk, wa ana 'ala 'ahdika wa wa'dika mastata't. A'udhu bika min sharri ma sana't. Abu'u laka bi ni'matika 'alayya, wa abu'u bi dhanbi, faghfir li, fa innahu la yaghfirudh-dhunuba illa ant.

**Translation:** O Allah, You are my Lord. There is no god but You. You created me and I am Your servant. I keep to Your covenant and Your promise as far as I am able. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your favour upon me, and I acknowledge my sin, so forgive me, for none forgives sins except You.

**Virtue (card):** سیّد الاستغفار، جنت کا پروانہ ملنا
The "master of seeking forgiveness". It earns a pass to Paradise.

**Reference (card):** بخاری، ابوداود، ترمذی، نسائی

## 10. Thanks for the day's blessings (once)

**Count:** 1

> اَللّٰهُمَّ مَا أَصْبَحَ [أَمْسٰى] بِيْ مِنْ نِّعْمَةٍ أَوْ بِأَحَدٍ مِّنْ خَلْقِكَ فَمِنْكَ وَحْدَكَ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَكَ، فَلَكَ الْحَمْدُ وَلَكَ الشُّكْرُ

**Transliteration:** Allahumma ma asbaha [amsa] bi min ni'matin aw bi ahadin min khalqika fa minka wahdaka la sharika lak, fa lakal-hamdu wa lakash-shukr.

**Translation:** O Allah, whatever blessing has come to me or to any of Your creation this morning [this evening] is from You alone, with no partner. So to You belongs all praise, and to You belongs all thanks.

**Virtue (card):** دن رات کی نعمتوں کا شکرانہ
It fulfils the thanks owed for the blessings of the day and the night.

**Reference (card):** ابوداود

## 13. Refuge from worry, debt and oppression (once)

**Count:** 1

> اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحُزْنِ، وَأَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَأَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَأَعُوْذُ بِكَ مِنْ غَلَبَةِ الدَّيْنِ وَقَهْرِ الرِّجَالِ

**Transliteration:** Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan, wa a'udhu bika minal-'ajzi wal-kasal, wa a'udhu bika minal-jubni wal-bukhl, wa a'udhu bika min ghalabatid-dayni wa qahrir-rijal.

**Translation:** O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief. I seek refuge in You from helplessness and laziness. I seek refuge in You from cowardice and miserliness. I seek refuge in You from being overcome by debt and from being overpowered by people.

**Virtue (card):** ادائیگی قرض اور ہر پریشانی سے نجات کے لئے
For the paying off of debt and relief from every distress.

**Reference (card):** ابوداود

## 14. Tasbih with affirmation of Allah's power and knowledge (once)

**Count:** 1

> سُبْحَانَ اللّٰهِ وَبِحَمْدِهٖ، لَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ، مَا شَآءَ اللّٰهُ كَانَ، وَمَا لَمْ يَشَأْ لَمْ يَكُنْ، أَعْلَمُ أَنَّ اللّٰهَ عَلٰى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيْرٌ، وَّأَنَّ اللّٰهَ قَدْ أَحَاطَ بِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ عِلْمًا

**Virtue (card):** تمام آفتوں سے حفاظت کے لئے

## 16. Taking Allah and creation as witness (4 times)

**Count:** 4

> اَللّٰهُمَّ إِنِّيْ أَصْبَحْتُ [أَمْسَيْتُ] أُشْهِدُكَ وَأُشْهِدُ حَمَلَةَ عَرْشِكَ وَمَلٰٓئِكَتَكَ وَجَمِيْعَ خَلْقِكَ، إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ اللّٰهُ لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُكَ وَرَسُوْلُكَ

**Translation:** O Allah, this morning [this evening] I call You to witness, and I call to witness the bearers of Your Throne, Your angels and all Your creation: truly You are Allah, there is no god but You, and Muhammad is Your servant and Your Messenger.

**Virtue (card):** پورے جسم کی آگ سے آزادی اور مغفرت کے لئے

**Reference (card):** ترمذی

## 17. The supplication of Anas: Bismillah over self, family and wealth, with 9:129 and 7:196 (once)

## Notes for the scholar

1. **#2:** the card adds «وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللّٰهِ» after «سُبْحَانَ اللّٰهِ الْعَظِيْمِ وَبِحَمْدِهٖ». The commonly cited narration has only the tasbih, three times after Fajr. The card's virtue lists four illnesses, including جنون. Please confirm the wording to use.
2. **#3 and #4:** the common narrations tie these to specific times. #3 is said 7 times after Fajr and Maghrib, before speaking. #4 is said 10 times after Fajr, while still seated, before speaking. The card gives no timing. Consider adding a short instruction in the app.
3. **#6:** in the common narrations this is mainly an evening dhikr. The card lists it for both times.
4. **#7:** the card has «رَسُوْلًا نَّبِيًّا» together. Narrations variously have «نَبِيًّا» or «رَسُوْلًا» alone. The combined form is widely circulated.
5. **#9:** the card has «وَأَبُوْءُ بِذَنْبِيْ», without لَكَ. Both «وَأَبُوْءُ لَكَ بِذَنْبِيْ» and «وَأَبُوْءُ بِذَنْبِيْ» are found in the narrations.
6. **#10 and #16:** these have morning and evening forms, so the app should switch the word by time of day. #16 is repeated 4 times. Some narrations of #16 add «وَحْدَكَ لَا شَرِيْكَ لَكَ» after «لَآ إِلٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ». The card does not have it. On the possible إِنَّكَ, see the uncertain readings.
7. **#11:** the card gives a single count (1) for Ayat al-Kursi and Ghafir 1–3 together.
8. **#15:** two counts, 3 for the ta'awwudh and 1 for the Hashr ayat. The app should show both, not a single number.
9. **#8 and #17:** #8 (7 times) and the end of #17 both contain 9:129, so the user recites it twice. This matches the card and is not an error.
10. **#18:** the card has «رَبُّ الْعَرْشِ الْكَرِيْمِ». Many printed versions of this du'a read «الْعَظِيْمِ». Please confirm. Its ending echoes Hud 11:56, but the card does not present it as Quran.
11. **#20:** no wording is given for the durood. Please choose the form the app should display.
12. **Spelling in the Quranic passages (#11, #15, #17):** the card sometimes uses a full alif where the IndoPak mushaf has a khari alif (الْكِتَابِ، عَالِمُ، السَّلَامُ، سُبْحَانَ، الْخَالِقُ، الصَّالِحِيْنَ).
