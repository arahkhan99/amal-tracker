### What this means

- **"Zad" (زاد) is a popular word in Islamic app names**, mostly as "Zad al-Muslim". None of
  the ones found is a **salah tracker or qada tracker**. They are prayer times, Quran, adhkar
  and qibla apps. A tracker focus is the clearest way to stand apart.
- No app called "Zad: Prayer & Qada Tracker", "Zad: Salah & Qada Tracker" or
  "Zad - Salah, Qada & Amal" turned up in the searches (App Store searches for "zad qada",
  "zad tracker", "zad salah" and "zad amal" found nothing like them). Apple only confirms a
  name is free when you reserve it in App Store Connect, so reserve it early.
- Avoid "Zad Muslim", "Zad al-Muslim", or "Zad: Quran, Azkar ..." styles. They are already
  in use and would be confusing.

Chosen: **Zad Tracker** (11 characters), on both stores. The earlier options were:

| Zad - Salah, Qada & Amal | 24 | Shows all three parts, but "amal" is little known outside South Asian English speakers |

The app's own name on the home screen stays **Zad**. The store name can be longer.

## 2. Google Play

**App name** (max 30): 11 characters
```text
Zad Tracker
```

**Short description** (max 80): 80 characters
```text
Track your five daily prayers, make up qada and keep up your daily amal. No ads.
```

**Full description** (max 4000): 2584 characters
```text
Zad (زاد) means provision for the journey. "And take provisions, but indeed the best provision is taqwa." (Quran 2:197)

Zad is a calm, personal tracker for your salah and your daily good deeds. Log each prayer with one tap, keep an honest count of the prayers and fasts you owe, and build steady habits that last. It is free, has no ads and does no tracking.

FIVE DAILY PRAYERS, ONE TAP EACH
• Mark Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha as prayed in congregation, prayed on time, or missed.
• See today's prayer times and which prayer is next.
• Prayer times are worked out on your phone from your approximate location, so they work offline.
• Choose from 12 calculation methods (ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, Egyptian, Karachi, Moonsighting Committee, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Diyanet and Tehran), and Hanafi or standard Asr.

QADA, MADE MANAGEABLE
• A prayer marked as missed is added to your qada (makeup) balance automatically.
• Set a monthly makeup goal and work towards it one prayer at a time.
• Tap "prayed one" or "fasted one" each time you make up a prayer or a fast.
• Adjust what you owe at any time, for prayers and for fasts.

DAILY AMAL CHECKLIST
• Quran, with a note of where you stopped so you can pick up from there next time.
• Morning and evening adhkar.
• Salawat, with a count.
• Sadaqah, any amount, any kind.
• Add your own items to the list.

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Day, week, month and year views.
• Streaks to keep you going.
• Gentle insights on what you're doing well and what needs more care.
• Look back over the last 90 days, day by day, and keep a note for each day.

GENTLE REMINDERS
• A nudge before a prayer becomes qada, if you haven't logged it yet.
• An evening check-in at 9:30 PM, only if something is still unlogged.
• Optional alerts at the start of each prayer time.

A LITTLE INSPIRATION
• A daily ayah or hadith on the home screen.
• A moment from the seerah of the Prophet ﷺ each Friday.
• Today's Hijri date.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything is stored on your phone. No account is needed.
• Optional lock with your fingerprint or face.
• Export your logs as a CSV file, or save a backup file you can restore on a new phone.
• Optional cloud backup with Google sign-in, which syncs your logs between your devices.
• No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases.

A NOTE ON PRAYER TIMES
Prayer times in Zad are calculated estimates. Please check them against your local masjid's timetable and follow your masjid where they differ.

May Allah accept your salah and your good deeds, and make this a provision for your journey.
```

**Category:** Lifestyle.
Most Islamic and prayer apps on Google Play are in Lifestyle, including most of the "Zad" apps
listed above, so that's where people browse for them. Productivity is the other option, since
Zad is a habit tracker at heart. But Lifestyle puts it next to the apps it will be compared with.

**Tags** (pick up to 5 in Play Console under Store settings, from Google's list; use the
closest ones available): Religion, Prayer times, Habit tracker, Islam, Self-improvement.

**Contact details and policy links** to fill in: support email, privacy policy URL (the app
already has a privacy policy screen; it also needs a public web URL), and the account-deletion
URL, which Google Play requires because the app offers Google sign-in.

## 4. Notes for you

### Decide

1. **Name (decided).** "Zad Tracker" on both stores, so people see the same name everywhere.
   Reserve it in App Store Connect soon; Apple accepts the first app to claim a name.
2. **Salah or prayer.** Both are in the App Store subtitle. Google Play has no keyword field,
   so there the words come from the short and full descriptions, which use both.
3. **Category.** Lifestyle is my suggestion on both stores. Change it only if you want to
   be seen as a productivity app first.
4. **Support email and privacy policy URL.** Both stores require these. Google Play also
   needs an account-deletion link.

### Keep the wording accurate

- **Prayer times are calculated estimates.** Keep the note asking people to follow their
  local masjid. Don't call the times "exact" or "precise".
- **Location is approximate.** The app rounds coordinates to about 1 km before saving or
  sending them. Don't describe it as using your "exact" or "precise" location.
- **Don't promise features the app doesn't have.** There is no adhan audio, no qibla
  compass, no Quran text, no widgets and no iPad or Apple Watch version. That's why
  "adhan" and "qibla" are left out of the keywords, even though other Zad apps use them.
- **Qada for fasts:** missed *prayers* go into the balance automatically, but missed *fasts*
  are added by hand ("Adjust what you owe"). The description says this; keep it that way.
- **"No tracking"** is true only while there are no analytics or crash-reporting tools that
  send data off the phone. Google sign-in backup uses Firebase. Your Play Data safety form and
  Apple's privacy labels must list what is stored in the cloud when backup is turned on (all
  logs, notes and settings including the name, linked to the Google account and its email). If
  you add analytics later, take "no tracking" out.
- **"Free, no ads"**: if you ever add a paid feature, update the short description,
  promotional text and description.
- **iPhone reminders and lock:** the iPhone wording describes a native App Store build. The
  current iPhone version is a web app that can't schedule notifications, so make sure the
  App Store build really sends the qada nudge, the 9:30 PM check-in and the prayer alerts,
  and supports Face ID / Touch ID, before you submit this text. Otherwise remove those lines.
- **The 9:30 PM time** is fixed in the app today. If it becomes adjustable, change the
  wording to "an evening check-in".
- **Hadith and ayah references** in the app's daily reflections show their sources (Quran
  chapter:verse or the hadith collection and number). Keep them that way. Reviewers and
  users notice.
- **Arabic and the ﷺ sign** are fine in both descriptions. Check they display properly in
  the store preview; if a store shows boxes instead, replace ﷺ with "(peace be upon him)".
